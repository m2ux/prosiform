# Corpus vocabulary

The words the fixture corpus's declarations and the conformance report's entries state that the contract's own vocabulary does not. The design states the contract's words and [the glossary](glossary.md) defines each term the design marks; a declaration needs those and a little more — the English a sentence is written in, the names Markdown and its encodings carry, the documents the corpus's examples are built from, and the words the report states about itself. Registering a word admits it; the independence check reports every word none of these sources states, so a word arrives here by being written down rather than by going unnoticed.

A word stands for its inflections: a plural, a past tense, a present participle and an adverb resolve to the word they are formed from, so one row admits each form. Case is folded, so one row admits `ASCII` and `ascii` alike. A term the glossary names as deliberately not used is not admitted here, and a word that names a build, a language, a library or the machinery of packaging never is: that is the term the check exists to report.
