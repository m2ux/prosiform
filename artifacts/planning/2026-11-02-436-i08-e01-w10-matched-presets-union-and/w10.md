**Component:** main:src/prosiform/cli.py

## Overview

The presets one title selects union their settings, each document is governed by its own presets alone, and a preset rule the run cannot apply as written is refused as invalid usage, exit status `2`: two selected presets pairing one group with two depths or mapping one `subst` key to two alternatives, and, within one preset, one group named twice, two `subst` keys of one identity, a `subst` key and value of one identity, and a `subst` key naming a section alone.

## Problem

The design's *Selected presets union* clause is stated, and the build holds only part of it.

- **A group named twice in one preset passes or fails as a cross-document conflict.** `cli.joined` deduplicates two equal `group-depth` entries, so one preset pairing `modes` twice at one depth publishes, and two depths raise `E_GROUP_DEPTH_CONFLICT`, exit `1`, as if two documents disagreed. `test_a_group_its_preset_names_twice_holds_the_document_once` holds the first.
- **Two selected presets disagreeing raise the wrong failure.** Two keyword presets of one document pairing one group with two depths reach `cli.group_depths` and fail with `E_GROUP_DEPTH_CONFLICT`, exit `1`; the design calls it invalid usage, exit `2`.
- **`subst` rules are not compared.** Two `subst` keys of one preset normalising to one identity, a key and value normalising to one identity, a key naming a section alone, and two selected presets mapping one key two ways are all admitted.
- **AC5's two fixtures do not exist.** Nothing in the corpus shows a linking document's presets stopping at its own content.

## Proposal

**The scene.** `cli.read_document` decodes a document's presets and holds each `subst` side to the reference grammar with `cli.substs`; `cli.joined` reads, for each reference reaching a document, the presets that reference's title selects and collects their `group-depth` pairs with `cli.pairs`; `cli.group_depths` then holds every member of one group to one depth.

**The design.**

- **A preset states each rule once.** `cli.stated_once`, run when a document is opened after `cli.substs`, refuses within each preset two `group-depth` entries naming one group, a `subst` key naming a section alone, a `subst` key and value naming one identity, and two `subst` keys naming one identity. Each names the entry, and a rule stated twice carries the earlier entry as the related location.
- **A `subst` identity is read from its declaring document.** `cli.subst_identity` resolves a side from the declaring document's directory, and a side naming a section alone names a section of that document, so `rules.md` and `./rules.md` are one key and `#Checks` written in `guide.md` names `guide.md#Checks`.
- **Selected presets agree or are refused.** `cli.agreed`, run by `cli.joined` for each reference before its pairs are read, refuses two selected presets pairing one group with two depths, or mapping one `subst` key to two alternative identities, naming both presets, the title that selected them and the setting. `default` lies beneath every selection, so its settings disagree with none.
- **The refusal is invalid usage.** `cli.preset_refusal` builds the failure: `E_INVALID_USAGE`, exit status `2`, the reference that opened or selected the document and its chain, the entry's location, and a remedy naming the fix.
- **Union and own-document governance hold as built.** `cli.joined` already reads each document's own presets for the references reaching it and unions their pairs; two corpus fixtures now show it.
- **Cross-document depth conflicts are unchanged.** Two documents, or two references selecting different presets of one document, pairing one group with two depths remain `E_GROUP_DEPTH_CONFLICT`, exit `1`.
