## Overview

The guide tells a dispatching agent to give a sub-agent the path of the bundle file with the inputs its dispatching step states as given, and a bundle whose links all stay inside it opens on its own content, without the instruction heading and the run-pf line. A reader then spends no call on a guide that has nothing to say about its bundle.

## Problem

- **The guide leaves the hand-over unstated.**
  The `⇉` and `⫽` entries say to give a bundle to a sub-agent "with the inputs of the step that led there". They say neither whether the bundle travels as its path or its text, nor which inputs those are.
- **Every bundle tells its reader to run `pf`.**
  `opened()` puts the `§ Instructions` group ahead of every bundle, so a bundle none of whose links leaves it gives its reader a compile step with nothing to compile.

## Proposal

- **A bundle travels by its path.**
  The guide's `⇉` and `⫽` entries say the sub-agent is given the path of the bundle file and reads it there.
- **The inputs are the step's.**
  They say the inputs are those the dispatching step states as given.
- **A self-contained bundle opens with its content.**
  A bundle holding no `↳`, `⇉` or `⫽` link, no pick token and no link to a document outside it carries no run-pf line.
- **Every other bundle is untouched.**
  A bundle holding any such link opens with the run-pf line as it does today.

## Design Decisions

- **One test decides the line.**
  Every `↳` line a bundle publishes, every `⇉` link and dispatch-set item, and every pick token rides on a link naming a Markdown file by a local path. The run reads its second-pass text as a renderer does and opens the bundle on the instruction group exactly when some link or diagram link names such a file. A fragment, a remote address, an image and a non-Markdown resource name none.
- **The heading goes with the line.**
  The `§ Instructions` heading holds only the line, so a bundle carrying no line carries no heading and opens on its own first part.
- **The destinations hold either way.**
  The map and the dispatch anchors are built over the groups without the instruction heading. Its slug is one no authored heading can take, so prepending it changes no other destination.
