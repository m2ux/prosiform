## Overview

The five diagram workflows become a product skill and three control skills each, so the eval still separates what bundling, the diagram and the link-following add, now from real use.

## Problem

- **The diagram workflows are bare-prompt forms.**
  `linear`, `branching`, `looping`, `pricing` and `rework` give sessions in `bundle`, `tree`, `prose` and `linked`, each handed over by name.
- **`prose` is a bundle.**
  The prose form is compiled for the agent, so its skill has no plain-Markdown counterpart yet.

## Proposal

**The scene.** Each workflow holds `diagram/`, `prose/` and `linked/` trees.

- **Four skills per workflow.**
  `compiled` runs `pf` and links `diagram/`'s entry with `↳`; `tree`, `prose` and `linked` link their own tree's entry as plain Markdown.
- **`prose` is read unbundled.**
  Its control is the prose tree as written, so it isolates the diagram and compilation together.
- **The corpus holds every variant to its paths.**
  The corpus test walks each variant's skill from its placed directory.
