## Overview

The six feature workflows become a product skill and a `tree` control skill each, so each bundling feature is measured against its unbundled tree from real use.

## Problem

- **Feature scenarios hand over a bundle or a tree by name.**
  Each gives sessions in `bundle` and `tree` from a bare prompt.

## Proposal

**The scene.** Each holds a `diagram/` tree and declares the warnings its feature raises.

- **Two skills per workflow.**
  `compiled` runs `pf` and links `diagram/`'s entry, or `section-entry`'s entry fragment, with `↳`; `tree` links the entry as plain Markdown.
- **Declared warnings hold for the agent's compile.**
  A bundle the session compiles raises the codes `scenarios.toml` declares and no other diagnostic.
