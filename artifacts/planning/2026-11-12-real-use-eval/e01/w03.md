## Overview

The four deferral workflows, `agent-pick` among them, become product skills, so a deferred line and a `?` pick are reached from a skill an agent discovered.

## Problem

- **Deferral scenarios start from a compiled bundle.**
  The session is handed the entry's bundle beside the sources, and its stated runs resolve from the workspace.

## Proposal

**The scene.** `deferred-composition`, `deferred-dispatch`, `agent-pick` and `preset-deferral` are written in `compiled/` alone.

- **One skill per workflow.**
  `compiled` runs `pf` and links `compiled/`'s entry with `↳`.
- **Stated runs resolve from the skill.**
  Each `compile` entry names its reference from the skill's directory, where `sources/` holds what a `↳` line names.
- **`read_source` binds the skill's sources.**
  A session that opens a document under the skill's `sources/` takes `read_source`.
