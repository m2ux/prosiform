## Overview

The bare-prompt path leaves the eval once every family is a skill: no instruction file, no entry-file prompt and no form. The eval stays runnable throughout, since nothing is removed while a workflow still runs on it.

## Problem

- **Three instruction files drive the bare prompt.**
  `instruction.md`, `instruction-compiled.md` and `instruction-bootstrap.md` and the `ENTRY` placeholder, `entry_named()` and the per-form instruction table carry it.
- **The forms outlive their use.**
  The default `--forms` still lists `bundle`, `tree`, `prose`, `linked`, `compiled`, `authored` and `bootstrap` after every workflow gives sessions in its variants.

## Proposal

**The scene.** Every family is a skill, so the instruction files, the entry wiring and the forms are unused.

- **The files and wiring leave.**
  The three instruction files, `ENTRY_PLACEHOLDER`, `entry_named()` and the bundled forms' workspace layouts are removed, with the tests that held them.
- **The matrix holds skills alone.**
  `--forms` becomes `--variants`, and the corpus test refuses a workflow with no `skills/` text.
- **The README states the eval as it is.**
  The Forms section describes variants, and every sentence about an instruction or entry document leaves.
