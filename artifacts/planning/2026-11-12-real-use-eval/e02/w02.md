## Overview

The runbook states how the real-use baseline is run and the rule by which a shakedown sets its caps, so neither the shakedown nor the run makes a design judgement of its own.

## Problem

- **No runbook section covers the skill-entry eval.**
  The baseline section's caps came from a bare-prompt shakedown.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The runbook's rubric and stop rules bind every run; a run section states its arguments, caps and committed files.

- **A real-use baseline section.**
  Harness and model asked at the start of the run; the default matrix; five repetitions; caps by the shakedown's rule.
- **The shakedown rule.**
  One repetition of every cell on the harness and model the user names, its spending cap set before it starts, and the session cap, token budget and cost cap derived from its measured figures.
