## Overview

The runbook states how the field-scale run is made and the rule by which its shakedown sets its caps.

## Problem

- **No runbook section runs Work Planner.**
  The real-use baseline section covers the eval's own workflows, whose sessions cost a fraction of one that reads a skill of real size.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The runbook's rubric and stop rules bind every run, and its real-use baseline section states the rule deriving caps from a shakedown.

- **A field-scale section.**
  Harness and model asked at the start of the run; `--workflows work-planner`; both variants; five repetitions; caps by the shakedown's rule; the committed files.
- **The shakedown rule.**
  One repetition of every Work Planner cell on the harness and model the user names, its spending cap set before it starts, and each cap derived from its measured figures.
