## Overview

The report sets Work Planner's two variants side by side for each scenario: pass rate, mean tokens and mean reads, and the difference between the variants.

## Problem

- **No table compares one variant with another.**
  The report gives each cell's own pass rate and mean tokens, and a reader subtracts by hand.
- **Reads are recorded and not reported.**
  `subagents.json` holds each file a session read, and no report row counts them.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The report's tables give one row per model, variant and scenario, each with its sessions, passes, failure counts and mean tokens.

- **A variant comparison table.**
  For a workflow holding a `compiled` and a `tree` variant, one row per scenario gives each variant's pass rate, mean tokens and mean reads, and the `compiled` figure less the `tree` figure for each.
- **Reads counted from the session record.**
  A session's reads are the files `subagents.json` records it read, its sub-agents' included.
