## Overview

Profile and outline bring the production build to the point where it bundles one document correctly: the declared Markdown profile configured explicitly, a compatibility-tested source-heading slugger, eligible section extraction, the group heading shift with title absorption and Setext conversion, heading-only structural ancestors, and the grouping rules that merge one part of the tree. The corpus's profile and outline fixtures are what it is measured against.

## Problem

The production build starts with no profile, no slugger and no section model, and each of them changes how existing source is read.

- **A default parser configuration is not the declared profile.**
  Enabling everything available, or accepting defaults, changes whether a construct is a table, a strikethrough, a task marker or ordinary text.

- **A fragment is renderer-defined.**
  The fragment reaching a heading is generated from its visible text and its duplicate numbering, and no slugging component is selected yet.

- **A heading inside a container is still a heading.**
  It must shift with the rest and must not terminate the section that encloses it, which the extraction and shift rules treat as separate facts.

- **Setext headings have no prefix to edit.**
  Shifting one means converting it, which an edit-in-place build must do without disturbing its inline content.

- **A grouping rule moves a member's sections out of its own outline.**
  Merging documents at a heading level puts their sections under one shared outline, so a name two members both carry needs a place for both, and a member's content above that level needs a place of its own.

## Proposal

- **Options are set explicitly and pinned with the profile identifier.**
  Pipe tables and strikethrough on, source task markers preserved, typographic substitution off, unsupported extensions off.

- **A slugging component is selected against the compatibility fixtures.**
  The fixtures decide, not the component's documentation, and the chosen version is pinned.

- **The section model comes from the event stream.**
  Eligible roots, boundaries, container nesting and heading ancestry are derived from events with their verified ranges, and heading syntax is edited rather than regenerated.

- **Structural ancestors are generated structure.**
  Unselected ancestors are emitted as escaped literal headings, recorded separately from selected content, and not traversed.

- **A grouping rule merges one part of the tree, and names stay verbatim.**
  Every document under a named directory is merged at a source heading level beneath one heading carrying that directory's slug in title case. A merged name occurring once is emitted bare; one several members carry is emitted once with a variant heading per member. Each member's content above the grouping depth is held in the group's overview section.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **AC1.** The parser is configured to the declared profile, with typographic substitution absent and source task markers preserved, as the corpus's profile fixtures show.
- [ ] **AC2.** A source heading's fragment equals the one a GitHub renderer generates for it, as the corpus's slug compatibility fixtures show.
- [ ] **AC3.** A fragment selects its eligible section and the blocks up to the next document-level heading of equal or higher level, as the corpus's extraction fixtures show.
- [ ] **AC4.** Each emitted source heading sits exactly one level deeper than in its source with its visible text unchanged, Setext headings included, while a group whose title the file heading absorbs keeps its source levels, as the corpus's shift fixtures show.
- [ ] **AC5.** A selected section's unselected eligible ancestors are emitted as headings carrying no body, sibling or descendant content, as the corpus's structural ancestor fixtures show.
- [ ] **AC6.** A bundling that would place a heading beyond the sixth level fails with its declared error code, as the corpus's depth-overflow fixtures show.
- [ ] **AC7.** One entry bundled with and without a group selects the same content and differs only in how it is arranged, with each group emitting one heading carrying its slug in title case, a merged section emitted once however many member documents contribute to it, and a document no group names keeping its own group, as the corpus's grouping fixtures show.
- [ ] **AC8.** A merged heading occurring once in its group is emitted with its source text alone, while one drawn from several documents is emitted once with each document's content beneath a variant heading naming that document, and a merged section carrying body text from more than one document splits into variants by document, and a member's preamble is emitted under the group's overview section as a subsection named by that document's stem in title case less a trailing word the section states, as the corpus's grouping fixtures show.
