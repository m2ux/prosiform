## Overview

Continuous integration that runs every corpus fixture on each change.

## Problem

A corpus that runs only when someone remembers reports a regression after the change that caused it.

## Proposal

A continuous-integration job runs the whole corpus against the build under test on every change, and fails the change when any fixture fails. The job reports the failing fixtures by identifier.

## Work Breakdown

| Part | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Analysis | The runner's invocation and exit behaviour as a job step |
| Plan | The job definition, its triggers and its reporting |
| Implementation | The job and its wiring to the runner |
| Review | The job confirmed to fail the change on a failing fixture |
| Test | A deliberately failing fixture fails the job, and the passing corpus does not |
