## Overview

Selection of an eligible section and the boundary where it ends.

## Problem

A fragment names a heading, not a region, and a heading inside a list or a blockquote looks like a terminator without being one.

## Proposal

Only headings that are direct children of the document are selectable roots, in both ATX and Setext spellings. A section runs to the next document-level heading of equal or higher level and includes deeper document-level headings and their content. A container-nested heading does not terminate it, and a fragment naming one fails as an unsupported target.
