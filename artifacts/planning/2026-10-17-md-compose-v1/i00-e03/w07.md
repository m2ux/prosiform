## Overview

The grouping level: whether a group of the composite is one document or one source heading level merged across documents.

## Problem

A composite grouped by document puts every document's `Operations` section in a different place, so a reader comparing operations across a skill reads 20 separate groups. Grouping by section level would put them together, and the composite has no way to express that.

Merging also breaks two things a document-grouped composite relies on. A document stops being contiguous, so a whole-document reference has nowhere to resolve. And content above the first group-level heading, which is the document's preamble, belongs to no group.

## Proposal

The grouping level selects what a group is. At one, a group is one source document headed by its path, which is the existing arrangement. Deeper, a group is one source heading text at that level, emitted once and carrying every document's contribution; a source heading at the group level becomes the composite's H1 and the levels below follow.

A merged heading keeps its source text, and two documents contributing one text to one group fail with the declared error code naming both and the group. Content above a document's first group-level heading is emitted under a leading `Sources` group as one heading per document, which is where a whole-document reference resolves. A reference to a group-level heading resolves to the merged group.
