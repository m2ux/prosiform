## Overview

Heading-only ancestors that keep a selected section in its original outline.

## Problem

A selected child emitted without its parents reads as a top-level topic, which discards the relationship that placed it where it was.

## Proposal

For each selected section, the chain of eligible ancestor headings is emitted as headings alone, with escaped literal text and no body, siblings or other descendants. Structural ancestors are recorded separately from selected content, carry their canonical source identities and original levels, are emitted once, and are replaced by the normal transformed heading and body when dependency discovery later selects that ancestor.

## Work Breakdown

| Part | Description |
