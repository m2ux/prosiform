## Overview

Candidate validation mode and the invariants it checks against recorded provenance.

## Problem

A reparse that agrees with itself establishes that the output parses, not that each region came from the source it claims, nor that a region is missing.

## Proposal

The candidate is reparsed under the same grammar in a policy context that expects generated anchors, filename headings, shifted headings and scaffolds, and that does not apply frontmatter stripping, dependency discovery or generated-input rejection. Target integrity, exactly-once coverage, absence of new links, absence of surviving definitions, reference preservation, ownership stability, outline preservation and depth safety are checked against the provenance ranges and the separate generated-structure records. Nothing is published while a check fails.
