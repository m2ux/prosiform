## Overview

The order a revision follows, and the record that each revision cites its finding.

## Problem

Correcting a composition in code leaves the design asserting the original behaviour, and a fixture edited to match an observed result turns an accident into the contract.

## Proposal

An accepted finding changes the design clause it contradicts first. The corpus then gains or amends the fixture that would have caught it, and the build changes until that fixture passes. Each design and corpus revision cites the ledger entry that motivated it, so a change can be read back to the use that caused it.

## Work Breakdown

| Part | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Analysis | The clause a finding contradicts, and what makes a corpus change follow a design change rather than lead it |
| Plan | The revision order, the citation form and where it is recorded |
| Implementation | The revision procedure and its citation record |
| Review | A sample of revisions confirmed to cite a ledger entry and to change the design first |
| Test | A check over the revision history reporting any corpus change whose design change does not precede it or whose entry is not cited |
