## Overview

The summary of what remains unsettled, read from the ledger.

## Problem

Without a record of what remains open, there is no way to see how close the specification is to settling.

## Proposal

A summary reports, from the ledger, the entries awaiting resolution and those already resolved or deferred, grouped by triage state. It is the measure the freeze is judged against and is produced from the ledger rather than maintained alongside it.

## Work Breakdown

| Part | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Analysis | The states an entry can hold and what unsettled means for each |
| Plan | The summary's grouping, its source and where it is published |
| Implementation | The summary generator |
| Review | The summary confirmed to be derived rather than hand-maintained |
| Test | The summary reproduced from a fixed ledger, and an entry added in each state confirmed to move its group |
