## Overview

Recognition of supported custom anchors and their resolution as aliases of a heading.

## Problem

An anchor preceding a heading and that heading's own fragment are two names for one section, and treating them as two destinations splits the section in two.

## Proposal

An empty standalone HTML anchor carrying one destination attribute, immediately preceding an eligible heading, is supported; blank lines and other supported empty anchors may intervene, and prose or another substantive block prevents association. The anchor and the heading's own fragment select the same canonical section. An anchor at any other position fails as a target, a required fragment with several destinations fails as ambiguous, and a section linking to its own alias fails as a direct self-reference.
