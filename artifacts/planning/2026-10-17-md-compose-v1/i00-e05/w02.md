## Overview

Generated destination identities and the collision check that validates them.

## Problem

Two documents can each hold a section whose heading produces the same fragment, so a composite carrying both would have two destinations with one name.

## Proposal

A destination identifier combines a readable slug of the root-relative path without its extension, a readable slug of the canonical source fragment, and the first twelve hexadecimal characters of a digest over the path, the target kind and the canonical fragment. Identifiers carry the reserved prefix. An alias uses its owning section's canonical fragment. Two distinct identities producing one identifier fail explicitly, and each generated destination is verified to occur exactly once.
