## Overview

Whole-document emission beneath a generated heading naming the source file.

## Problem

Parsed content has no output form, and without a per-file heading two sources would share one outline.

## Proposal

A contributing source is emitted beneath one generated top-level heading whose visible text is its normalized root-relative filename, escaped so punctuation in a path cannot create a link, an image or inline HTML. The document's own content follows, with its preamble before its first retained heading.
