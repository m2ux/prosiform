## Overview

Removal of a leading byte-order mark and leading frontmatter from composed content.

## Problem

Source transport metadata would otherwise be published as body text, and stripping it moves every offset after it.

## Proposal

One optional leading byte-order mark is accepted and removed. A first physical line of exactly three hyphens opens frontmatter, closed by a line of exactly three hyphens or three dots; the opener, the metadata, the closer and the closer's terminator are removed. A recognized opener with no closer fails. The removal is recorded so diagnostics report original source coordinates.
