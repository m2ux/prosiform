## Overview

The wrapper that owns the delivery contract between the build and its caller.

## Problem

A transport limit or a broken pipe can truncate a bundle that compiled correctly, and Markdown arriving is not evidence that all of it arrived. A bundle that does arrive whole still says nothing about which part answers the request.

## Proposal

The wrapper invokes the entry point, checks process status, buffers the whole bundle and enforces the transport's response limit. A bundle it cannot deliver whole returns a delivery failure. It substitutes no summary, no partial document and no reference the caller must follow, and it keeps completion metadata, byte counts and warnings in its structured result rather than in the Markdown. Each entry reference's destination is read from the diagnostic stream and carried in that result for callers acting on it as data; the agent reads the bundle's own entry line instead.
