## Overview

Specification revision is the order an accepted finding travels in and the revisions that travel it. The design changes first, the corpus follows, and the build follows the corpus. A finding accepted after this epic opens becomes a further row of it, so the refinement that remains is visible rather than implied.

## Problem

A correction applied in the wrong place leaves the contract wrong and the next implementation reading it.

- **A fix in the build leaves the design asserting the original behaviour.**
  The design is what a second implementation reads, so a composition corrected only in code carries its correction nowhere.

- **A corpus changed first makes the design follow the fixtures.**
  A fixture edited to match an observed result turns an accident into the contract.

- **A revision with no trace cannot be reviewed.**
  A design change whose motivating finding is not recorded reads as an unexplained edit.

- **A clause added under refinement has no fixture.**
  It produces no failure, so the gap presents as a passing corpus.

- **An unbounded refinement has no visible end.**
  Without a measure of what remains open, there is no way to judge how close the specification is to settling.

## Proposal

- **Design, then corpus, then build.**
  An accepted finding changes the design clause it contradicts; the corpus gains or amends the fixture that would have caught it; the prototype changes until that fixture passes.

- **Each revision cites its finding.**
  The design and corpus revision history names the ledger entry that motivated the change.

- **Traceability is refreshed with each revision.**
  The clause-to-fixture mapping is regenerated whenever the design or the corpus changes, so a clause added without a fixture appears as uncovered on the revision that added it.

- **An accepted finding becomes a row.**
  Rows are added to this epic as findings are accepted, each covering the criterion stating what resolving it makes true.

- **A prerequisite section goes when composition replaces it.**
  A skill's prerequisite section names whole documents, coarsely but completely, and the inline links inside a procedure name sections precisely but may not cover everything that list covered. Removal is therefore gated on a recorded composition supplying what the list named, and a shortfall is a ledger entry rather than a silent loss.

- **The instruction replaces the list.**
  Where a section said which documents to read, the skill's own text says which invocation composes them. The agent is told to compose rather than left to infer it, and how clearly that reads is itself something use reports.

- **What remains open is reported from the ledger.**
  The summary is derived rather than maintained, so it cannot drift from the entries it describes.

- [ ] **AC1.** An accepted finding changes the design clause it contradicts before the corpus or the build changes, as the revision-order check over the revision history shows.
- [ ] **AC2.** Each design and corpus revision cites the ledger entry that motivated it, as the revision-order check shows.
- [ ] **AC3.** A clause added without a fixture appears as uncovered on the revision that added it, as the regenerated conformance report shows.
- [ ] **AC4.** The entries awaiting resolution are reported from the ledger, grouped by triage state, as the open-finding summary shows.
- [ ] **AC5.** A prerequisite section is removed only where a recorded composition run supplies the sources it named, as the removal record shows.
- [ ] **AC6.** A source a prerequisite section named that the composition did not supply is a ledger entry, as the ledger shows.
- [ ] **AC7.** A skill whose prerequisite section is removed carries in its own text the invocation that composes what the section named, as the removal record shows.
