## Overview

The measured output size of the closure the proposal used as its evidence.

## Problem

The output budget is an approved engineering default, and the composition the proposal measured as its evidence is of the same order as that default, so the first composition anyone attempts may be refused by a limit nobody has tested against real material.

## Proposal

A fixture composes the closure the proposal measured and records the resulting output size against the configured budget. The recorded figure is what the budget is judged by: the design says the numerical defaults are revisited with measured fixture and real-skill data, and this is that measurement. A composition the budget refuses is reported with the measured size, so a change to the default is a decision taken against a number rather than against an impression.
