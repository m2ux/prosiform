## Overview

Classification of references and recursive traversal of the supported ones to a fixed point.

## Problem

Instruction sets link back to one another, so a traversal without a fixed point repeats regions or does not terminate.

## Proposal

References classify into local Markdown documents, fragment-only links, images and non-Markdown resources, web URLs and other schemes. Supported local Markdown links in selected content are resolved against their own originating document, bundled and included. Traversal iterates over newly included content until nothing further is reachable, without a recursive-call-depth cap that would reject a valid cyclic graph. Apparent links inside code are not followed.
