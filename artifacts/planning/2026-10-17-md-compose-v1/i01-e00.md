## Overview

The ledger is where what use reveals is kept, and the capture tool is what makes an entry something that can be re-run. Together they turn a surprise met while composing into evidence a revision can be judged against.

## Problem

A finding met mid-task competes with the task, and a finding stated in words cannot be re-run.

- **The cheapest action is a local workaround.**
  The agent is carrying out a procedure, so without a recorded place for a surprise the surprise is absorbed and the design keeps its original claim.

- **Prose is not reproducible.**
  A finding described in sentences cannot establish whether a later change fixed it, so the question becomes a matter of opinion.

- **An untriaged entry is a backlog item.**
  An entry nobody classifies never reaches the design, the corpus or the build, and accumulates instead.

- **A real source tree is not a fixture.**
  The skill tree that triggered a surprise is too large to serve as a fixture and carries content the finding does not need.

## Proposal

- **The ledger is a file in the planning record.**
  Each entry carries its date, the invocation, the source identities it composed, the observed result and the result expected.

- **Triage is part of the entry.**
  One state among design change, corpus gap, build defect and no change, with the reason for it. A schema check validates every entry.

- **A capture tool reduces an entry to a fixture.**
  It takes the invocation and the source closure the entry reached and emits the smallest fixture directory and manifest that still reproduces the observed result.

- **A reduced fixture waits with its entry.**
  It enters the corpus with the revision that resolves the entry, so the corpus never carries a fixture declared to fail.
