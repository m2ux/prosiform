# I05:E07 W04 Compiled Step States Its Dispatch by Its Operators — October 2026

> Task delivery · Created 2026-10-30

## Executive Summary

The compiled form's dispatch steps stated their dispatch twice: once in the operator a link carried, and once in the sentence beside it. A session could follow the sentence and never read the `Dispatch` protocol `pf` publishes, so the eval measured whether an agent reads English rather than whether it follows compiled material, and the compiled and authored pass rates were not comparable.

This record holds the delivery of W04, which removes the second statement from the eval's compiled sources and makes the reader refuse it: a compiled step is its operators, and its protocol says what they mean. The authored form keeps its prose, because it has no protocol to lean on. The two forms now hold one statement each, which is what makes their difference the thing compilation adds.
