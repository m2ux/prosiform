**Component:** main:docs/hierarchy.md

## Overview

The hierarchy page states the heading rule the build emits for a titled group member, where a merged group stands once groups nest, and the depth a nested group costs, and names no retired group-heading rule.

## Problem

- **The page states member names without the title rule.**
  *Member names* states the distinguishing name and omits that a member's H1 is absorbed into its subsection where their slugs agree, that such a member takes the subsection even when everything else it carries sits at the grouping depth, and that a whole-document reference to it and a reference to its title resolve there.
- **The page states a retired group-heading rule.**
  *Groups* calls a group one top-level division, and the opening calls each contributing document a top-level heading, while a named group the build emits stands inside the division of the document whose link placed its first member.
- **The nesting cost is unstated.**
  Nothing tells an author that every enclosing division moves a nested group's headings one level deeper, that a heading pushed past H6 fails the run, or what the author changes when it does.

## Proposal

**The scene.** *Groups* and *Member names* state membership, variants and the subsection a member takes; *The level shift* states the H6 limit for a file group.

- **The titled member's heading.**
  A member's subsection absorbs its own H1 where the title's slug agrees with the file's stem, so the title is not emitted again, the member takes that subsection though nothing else it carries sits above the grouping depth, and a whole-document reference to it and a reference to its title resolve to that subsection. A title that does not agree is content above the grouping depth beneath the subsection. A titled parent opens its group on its title.
- **Where a group stands.**
  A file group stands at the top level wherever its document is placed. A named group stands inside the division holding the document whose link placed its first member, after that division's own content, one level deeper per enclosing division; a group whose first member is an entry stands at the top level. The page states this once and links the design's *Grouping* for the rule.
- **The nesting cost and its remedy.**
  A heading a nested group pushes past H6 fails the run rather than being lifted to the top level, so an author meeting it places the group's documents through a shallower document, or keeps their headings shallow.
