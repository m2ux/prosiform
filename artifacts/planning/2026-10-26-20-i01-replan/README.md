## Executive Summary

The MVP demonstration initiative delivered the example migration, a tagged demonstration revision, its deferred debt and a skill refactor. Several rows of the refinement initiative were written before that delivery and now describe work it did, work the design decided against, or a premise it changed. This record reconciles the refinement initiative with what landed: rows the demonstration superseded are removed, epics closed without delivery are recorded as cancelled, and work the demonstration deferred to refinement takes its own epic.

The goal is unchanged: the specification is settled by use, every finding is resolved or deferred, and design and corpus carry one tagged revision a production build can target. What changes is the ground it stands on. The exposure set now holds the demonstrated example and the skills agents invoke, the demonstration's measurements seed the ledger, and grouping owned by the file that writes the link joins the contract extensions this stage settles.
