**Component:** example/work-tracker

## Overview

A published destination is compiled by a later run, and the suite that holds that claim does not exist.

## Problem

The corpus asserts the emitted form of both operators and never compiles what was published, so the design's handoff row is reached by no fixture.

## Proposal

A pytest suite copies `example/work-tracker`, bundles its mode, parses the destinations the bundle published, compiles each, and compares the second bundle whole. The generated-input refusal is its own test.

| Part | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Analysis | Read `checks/runner.py`, `src/tests/test_linked_bootstrap.py` and the example tree. |
| Plan | Choose the invocation route and the scenarios. |

| Review | Check the destination is read from the bundle and never restated. |
| Test | Run the suite under `make test` and the full check set. |
