## Overview

Generated headings that coincide publish with a warning, `W_COINCIDENT_HEADING`.

## Problem

- **Coinciding file headings passed silently.**
  Two documents sharing a title, or untitled documents whose paths differ only by Markdown extension, were told apart by anchor ordinal alone (`#note`, `#note-1`) and nothing told the user.
- **Coinciding member names refused the bundle.**
  Members of one merged group whose shortest distinguishing names coincided failed with `E_AMBIGUOUS_FILE_GROUP`.

## Proposal

- **Warning.** `cli.coincident` reads the assembled groups. File headings (the parent's title included where the bundle opens on it) collect across the bundle; member names (`grouping.member_headings`) collect within each merged group. Texts equal under `casefold` coincide. One diagnostic per set, in bundle order: message names the count, the kind and the shared text; `location` is the first source, `related` every other.
- **Locations.** A titled file heading: the title's physical line, column 1. A path heading: `1:1`. A member heading: its `group:` declaration (`conversion.group_location`).
- **Emission.** `compose` returns the bundle and the warnings; `execute` emits them after the output budget check, before `W_RESOURCE_PORTABILITY`. A failing run emits none.
- **Removed.** `cli.unambiguous`, `E_AMBIGUOUS_FILE_GROUP` (code, schema, catalogue, invocation, design).
- **Update the design** (`05057116`) and repin `README.md`.
