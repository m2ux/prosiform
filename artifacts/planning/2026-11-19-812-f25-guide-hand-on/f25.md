## Overview

The guide's `⇉` entry says to give a sub-agent the path of the bundle file, which it reads there. A dispatching agent read the bundle too, before handing it on. This hot-fix tells the dispatcher to hand on the link without opening the bundle.

## Problem

- **A dispatcher reads the bundle it hands on.**
  On the #809 specimen's arm A, 5 of 8 Haiku 5.5 runs under the 0.28.0 guide read `count-the-words.bundle.md` before dispatching it, and 16 of 16 under 0.27.0; in the finding-classification slice, Opus 5.5's Investigate read the head of a bundle before dispatching it in two trials.
- **Saying whose the bundle is did not move it.**
  "The bundle is the sub-agent's to read; what you need to hand it on is its link and its brief." left Haiku at 7 of 8, and a dispatch line written as a bare path left it at 6 of 8.

## Proposal

- **The `⇉` entry ends on the dispatcher's part.**
  "Do not open the bundle yourself: hand on its link." Bundle links are unchanged.
