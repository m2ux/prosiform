## Overview

The v1 design states preset alternatives and the agent's pick before the build enforces them, so each documentation page can pin the commit that states the rule it describes.

## Problem

- **The contract states no alternative.**
  The design's Presets section layers selected presets over `default` and states nothing that requires one of a set, nor a tag that leaves the pick to the agent.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The v1 design on the engineering branch is the contract the documentation pins by commit; its Conversion metadata, Presets, Metadata failures, Link classification, Guide and Exit status sections hold the rules this initiative changes.

- **Presets.**
  `choice` marks an alternative; exactly one alternative is in force on the union of a bundle's references; an unpicked alternative raises no `W_PRESET_UNUSED`.
- **Metadata failures.**
  A malformed `choice`, a `choice` in `default` and a preset named `?` fail with `E_INVALID_CONVERSION_METADATA`.
- **Substitution.**
  A pair of an alternative is routing: no `W_SUBST_USED`, and `W_SUBST_UNUSED` only for a key no link of its document names.
- **Link classification.**
  `?` is accepted on `¬` and `¬↳` links only; a deferred link to alternatives names one or carries `?`, never both; `?` needs alternatives.
- **Guide and exit status.**
  The symbol-grammar entry for `?`, and `E_ALTERNATIVE_UNRESOLVED` with exit status 2 naming each alternative with its choice line.
