**Component:** invocation.md

## Overview

The contract's statement of the substitution mapping, the shorter statement the invocation guide carries, the vocabulary that admits both, and the pin that lets a fixture name the clause. Every later row of the epic lands its fixtures in the pull request that makes them pass, so the clause and the revision carrying it exist before the first of them.

## Problem

`checks/conformance.py` reads the design at the revision `README.md`'s Design link pins, and reports a fixture whose declared clause that revision does not state under `# Unnamed declarations`, which fails the report. A fixture-first plan therefore cannot land a single mock fixture until the contract states the behaviour, and `make check` runs conformance, so nothing in the epic can merge before this row.

The epic's plan names `docs/corpus-vocabulary.md` as the vocabulary's home. No such file exists on this branch, and no check reads one: `checks/vocabulary.py` holds `docs/glossary.md` to defining every term the design italicises and every definition to being used, and `checks/independence.py` admits any word the pinned design's prose states. The vocabulary is therefore two things in two places — the definitions in `docs/glossary.md` on this branch, and the admitted words in the design at the pin — and the plan's third file is a phantom.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The design lives on `engineering`; the build and the corpus live on `main`; `README.md` on `main` pins a commit on `engineering`. Conformance resolves that pin through `origin/engineering`, and `make invocation` holds `invocation.md`'s option table to the command's help output in both directions, so a row the option cannot yet produce fails the check as surely as a missing one.

**The design.**

- **The design gains one clause,** `Link classification and resource handling — The substitution mapping`, as the first rule of that section, beside the operators and the root. It states the form, that a key is the identity a reference normalizes to with a fragment key taking precedence over a document key, that both sides resolve from the captured invocation working directory and so are exempt from the writing source's root, that every reference is redirected — a positional entry, `-f`, and a compiled, deferred, dispatched or parallel-dispatched target alike — that a substitute is a source in every respect whose own links are matched rather than followed again, that the four malformed entries are invalid usage, and that a successful run states each substitution and each key that matched nothing.
- **The two names it introduces are contract terms.** *Substitution mapping* and *substitute* are italicised on first use, as every contract term is, and `docs/glossary.md` defines both. That is the vocabulary work: a definition the design does not use fails `make vocabulary`, and a term the design marks that the glossary does not define fails it too, so the clause and the rows land together.
- **`invocation.md` gains a bundling rule** in fewer words under `## Bundling`: what `-m ORIGINAL=ALTERNATIVE` names, that both sides resolve from the working directory, that the substitute is the target outright, and that the run states each substitution. No option row: `make invocation` compares the table to the help output in both directions, and the option W01 adds is the only thing that can produce the row.
- **The pin moves to the amendment.** `README.md`'s Design link pins the `engineering` commit carrying the clause, which is why the amendment lands first.
- **Two deliveries.** The design amendment lands on `engineering`, and the pin, the bundling rule and the glossary rows land on the task branch cut from the epic base.
