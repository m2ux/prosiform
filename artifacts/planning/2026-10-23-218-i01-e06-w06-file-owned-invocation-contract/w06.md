## Overview

The contract defines compilation from one file argument.

## Problem

Root and output choices require caller-supplied options.

## Proposal

Specify relative prosiform-root, bounded bootstrap capture, per-source link resolution and confinement, normalized entry filenames, fragment suffixes, combined destinations and removal of -r. Specify transitive parent-root inheritance, direct-entry defaults, explicit overrides and shared-file resolution under distinct inherited roots. Define invalid-root and source-access diagnostics and retain atomic publication.
