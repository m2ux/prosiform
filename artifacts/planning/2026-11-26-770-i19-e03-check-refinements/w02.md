**Component:** main:checks/independence.py, main:checks/tests/test_independence.py

## Overview

A section stating no behaviour contributes the terms it marks to the build words, not every word of its prose, so an ordinary word stops failing fixture descriptions.

## Problem

- **Every word of a silent section is a candidate.**
  A word only such a section carries is refused wherever a fixture states it, so "yet" was refused once a build sentence moved.
- **Prose there invites the next failure.**
  Each sentence a silent section gains can turn a common word into a build word, and the remedy is to reword the design.

## Proposal

A silent section names `pulldown-cmark` in code and says the choice of slugger crate remains open.

- **Marked terms only.**
  A build word is a term a silent section writes in a code span or as a bold defined term, matched in its forms as the contract's terms are.
- **Behavioural text still admits its words.**
  A marked term the behavioural text also states stays a contract word.
