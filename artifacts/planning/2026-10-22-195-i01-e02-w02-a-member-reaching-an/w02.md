**Component:** [src/prosiform/grouping.py](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/main/src/prosiform/grouping.py)

## Overview

The destination a whole-document reference to a merged member reaches, held to a heading no other member of the group shares.

## Problem

A member whose selected content all sits at the grouping depth takes no subsection, and a whole-document reference to it resolves to its first contribution. That contribution is a merged section, and a merged section is pooled by heading text, so two members contributing one heading text resolve to one fragment. A bundle emits `[Common](#style)` beside `[Agents](#style)`: two references, one destination, one of them naming content the reader never asked for.

The fault is silent. The bundle validates, the link resolves, and the reader lands on another document's section under a name that promised their own.

## Proposal

The arrangement already distinguishes the contributors of a pooled section. A merged section carrying body text from more than one document splits into variants, one heading per document, named by the run of that document's path which tells it from the others. The destination naming each member therefore exists in the bundle; what is missing is the member registering it as its own.

- **A whole-document reference to a subsection-less member resolves to the first heading the group emits from that member which no other member shares.** Where its contribution pools with another's, that is the variant heading naming the document inside the pooled section. Where it pools with none, that is the merged heading itself, which names one document already.
- **A heading names one member when every source heading it answers to, and every target it answers, is that member's.** A pooled heading answers to one origin per contributing document, so it names no single member; a variant heading answers one document's heading alone, so it does.
- **A structural ancestor is no destination.** It answers nothing by construction, so it is passed over whatever it names.
- **No two members of one group share a destination.** The subsection naming a member is already the member's alone, and the heading a subsection-less member now takes is unshared by the rule that selects it.

The merged sections, the grouping depth that selects them, the variant headings that distinguish contributors and the `--group` argument are unchanged.
