**Component:** [src/prosiform/grouping.py](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/main/src/prosiform/grouping.py)

## Overview

The destination a whole-document reference to a merged member reaches, held to a heading that member does not share.

## Problem

A member whose content all sits at the grouping depth resolves to its first contribution. Two members whose first contribution carries one heading text therefore resolve to one fragment, and a bundle emits `[Common](#style)` beside `[Agents](#style)`, sending two references to one heading.

## Proposal

A whole-document reference to a merged member reaches a heading that names that member. Where the member's first contribution is a heading other members share, the variant heading naming the document within it is the destination, so two members contributing one heading text resolve to two headings.
