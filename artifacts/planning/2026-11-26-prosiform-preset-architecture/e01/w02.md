## Overview

The complete pure import graph preserves substitution origins, role reach and manifest provenance.

## Problem

Nested levels/subst imports need explicit cycle/conflict boundaries and a complete public admission point.

## Proposal

Extend the delivered capture service with the reviewed bounded graph resolver, key-relative substitutions and equal/conflicting value handling. Wire imports metadata only when both supported setting kinds and all refusal cases work. Feed resulting settings into existing role resolution and the manifest rather than creating inheritance or a new substitution engine. First checkpoint90 minutes: a replacement of existing path/scope machinery or scope beyond the matrix requires retained-work replan; do not silently grow the task.
