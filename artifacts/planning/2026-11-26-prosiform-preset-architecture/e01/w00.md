## Overview

Explicit imports reuse pure settings at their declaring origin and receiving role.

## Problem

Cross-source reuse has no defined exact-target, impurity or conflict boundary.

## Proposal

Fix imports as a list of path/preset references. Reuse existing complete-name normalization for exact preset lookup; select only that preset metadata and count its declaration as selected, without implicit default or body selection. Permit only levels, subst and recursive pure imports. Equal values coalesce; incompatible imported/imported or imported/receiving-local values fail with both origins. Existing selected-over-default layering remains separate. Capture lexical origins, pure cycles, missing/impure targets, ordinary scope, entry maps, explicit entry stubs, dispatch and deferral cases.
