## Overview

The contract states the ledger as accepted from its specimen set: what it records, what it leaves out and when it is written.

## Problem

The bundler records source and selection facts, but the published ledger's contract and its complete feature coverage are not fixed.

## Proposal

- The contract states the ledger option, the YAML form and schema version, and the check-mode output, each as the accepted specimen shows it.
- A coverage table maps every existing feature to a ledger field or a stated exclusion.
- Runtime effort, provider and transport observation, obedience and secrets are stated as outside the ledger.
- The contract states publication only: verification is stated by the work that delivers it, so a build pinning this revision reports every code the revision declares.
- The deterministic matrix names each case a fixture will carry.
