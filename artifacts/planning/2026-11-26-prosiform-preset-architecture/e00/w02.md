## Overview

The choice-group set shows, as files, one document stating a component axis and a test axis, and what each selection, pick and refusal produces.

## Problem

Group identity, cardinality and picking are described in prose, so no reader can see the source an author writes or the bundle and refusal a caller receives.

## Proposal

- The proposed source names a component group and a test group on one document's alternatives, and states the same groups on a second document carrying no level, so an entry can name it directly. The 0.36.0 refusal of that source is retained as it is written.
- Hand-written bundles show an explicit selection of one member of each group, and a dispatch that fixes one group and leaves the other to the pick.
- Hand-written text shows the entry naming one pick for each open group, and each refusal: a group with no member, two members of one group, a dispatch leaving two groups open and a pick naming a member of a fixed group.
- Hand-written text shows the preset listing of the document: its alternatives by group with their choice lines, its ordinary presets and its stubs.
- A hand-written ledger names each group, the alternative chosen in it and where each was declared.
- The README links the five 0.36.0 controls the record holds as the nearest accepted builds, and states the open points: the fixed-group pick and the listing's format.
