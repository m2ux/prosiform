## Overview

An operator-facing manifest records compiler construction facts without claiming runtime behavior.

## Problem

The compiler already records source and selection data, but the public record contract and its complete feature coverage are not fixed.

## Proposal

Define the opt-in --manifest output and versioned schema. Map every existing feature to a recorded field or an explicit exclusion: capture/hashes, regions, ordered default/selected presets, layout groups, substitutions and lexical origins, entry stubs, alternatives, briefs, dispatch/deferral/picks, capability levels, resolved models and bundle hashes. Exclude runtime effort/provider/transport/obedience and secrets. Freeze the public synthetic controls before implementation.
