## Overview

The complete local-group feature preserves fixed bindings through an open pick, dispatch and deferral.

## Problem

Published routes must keep explicit group choices, and several open axes must not multiply into an implicit pick tree.

## Proposal

- The delivered group service is wired into source metadata and the existing selection and dispatch paths, over settings the import resolver has flattened.
- A `choice` mapping lacking `group` or `states`, or holding another key, is refused as a malformed `choice` is.
- Explicit groups, brief independence, the names a deferral carries and ledger origins are preserved.
- A dispatch publishes one open group through the existing pick mechanism and is refused with a remedy for each group where several are open.
- The entry's pick option is repeatable, one occurrence for each open group. Two picks naming one group, and a pick naming a member of a group the title fixes, are invalid usage.
- The README's design link moves to the contract revision stating groups in the same change, with the group fixtures and the codes page.
- A first checkpoint at ninety minutes stops for a replan before a general Cartesian picker or a broad dispatch rewrite.
