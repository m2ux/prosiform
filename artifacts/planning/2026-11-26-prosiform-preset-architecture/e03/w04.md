## Overview

One command prints a document's presets, its alternatives by choice group and its imports, publishing nothing.

## Problem

An author learns which alternatives a document declares only from the refusal a build without one raises.

## Proposal

- The listing option names one document and prints its ordinary presets, its alternatives by group with their choice lines, its stubs and its imports with origins, as the accepted specimen shows.
- It reads the document's metadata and the sources its imports name, bundles nothing and writes no folder.
- The contract gains the listing clauses in this work, and the README's design link moves to that revision in the same change.
- The public two-axis example's worker is the document the invocation contract lists.
