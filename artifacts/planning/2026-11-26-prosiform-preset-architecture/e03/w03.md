## Overview

The public component and test example and the guide pages state the delivered local selection mechanism.

## Problem

A grouped pick must stay understandable without operational policy moving into metadata.

## Proposal

- One synthetic example ships with component and test axes and optional recorded data as a separate stub.
- The pages state the one-open-group dispatch pick, the entry's pick for each open group and the several-open refusal.
- The existing brief behavior and authored operational text are stated as they stand.
