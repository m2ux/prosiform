## Overview

One local group-resolution service validates independent component and test cardinality.

## Problem

Exclusive axes need a focused owner independent of publication and dispatch.

## Proposal

- A normalized local group and cardinality service covers the contracted source cases, the single unnamed group among them.
- Ordinary additive presets and optional stubs stay distinct from grouped alternatives.
- The metadata is admitted publicly with the pick and dispatch seam, so no partly usable group form is released.
- A first checkpoint at sixty minutes returns a replan where a generic multi-axis planner or an implicit fallback would be needed.
