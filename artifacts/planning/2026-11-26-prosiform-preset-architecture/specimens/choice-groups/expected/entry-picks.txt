Hand-written specimen: pf 0.36.0 takes one --pick, and refuses the source's choice-group field.

At the entry the caller knows every answer, so --pick is repeatable: each
occurrence names one member of one group the entry's title leaves open. Each
command runs in `../proposed/`, on `direct.md`, which states the two groups and
no level, so it can be entered directly.

1. One pick for each open group

$ pf -o bundles '[Check](direct.md "?")' --pick alpha --pick small

Standard error is empty. Exit status 0. The run writes
`bundles/direct-alpha+small.bundles/direct-alpha+small.bundle.md`. The name
joins its members sorted by Unicode code point, whatever order the picks came
in, as pf 0.36.0 names a bundle today.

2. One group left open at the entry

$ pf -o bundles '[Check](direct.md "?")' --pick alpha
E_ALTERNATIVE_UNRESOLVED: direct.md needs one alternative of its group 'test' in force:
  small  The test is small.
  large  The test is large.
  source: direct.md
  remedy: rerun with a further --pick naming the alternative of 'test' that fits your task

Exit status 2. pf 0.36.0 builds this command on the ungrouped source and
bundles the test slot unselected, as `../proposed/diagnostics-0.36.0.txt`
records.

3. One group fixed in the title, the other picked

$ pf -o bundles '[Check](direct.md "alpha, ?")' --pick small

Exit status 0. The same bundle as case 1.

4. Two picks naming one group

$ pf -o bundles '[Check](direct.md "?")' --pick alpha --pick beta
E_INVALID_USAGE: --pick names 'alpha' and 'beta', both alternatives of the group 'component' of 'direct.md'
  reference: [Check](direct.md "?")
  remedy: name one alternative of each group the entry leaves open

Exit status 2.

5. A pick naming no alternative of the document

$ pf -o bundles '[Check](direct.md "?")' --pick gamma --pick small
E_ALTERNATIVE_UNRESOLVED: direct.md needs one alternative of its group 'component' in force:
  alpha  The requested component is alpha.
  beta   The requested component is beta.
  source: direct.md
  remedy: rerun with a further --pick naming the alternative of 'component' that fits your task

Exit status 2. An unknown name leaves its pick unmade, as it does today.

6. A pick naming a member of a group the title already fixes

$ pf -o bundles '[Check](direct.md "alpha, ?")' --pick beta
E_INVALID_USAGE: --pick names 'beta', an alternative of the group 'component', which the entry's title fixes as 'alpha'
  reference: [Check](direct.md "alpha, ?")
  remedy: name an alternative of 'test', the group the entry leaves open

Exit status 2.

7. A deferred link

A deferred link is bundled by a later run, whose entry it becomes. That entry
names one pick for each open group, so a deferral may fix some groups and
leave any number open:

  [¬ Check](direct.md "alpha, ?")    publishes  [Check](../../direct.md "alpha, ?")
  [¬ Check](direct.md "?")           publishes  [Check](../../direct.md "?")
  [¬ Check](direct.md "alpha")       is refused by the deferring run:

  E_UNSUPPORTED_CONSTRUCT: the deferred link 'direct.md' names no alternative of the group 'test'; direct.md declares 'small', 'large' in it, and the link must name exactly one alternative of each group or carry '?' for the groups it leaves open

The later run bundles the first with `--pick small`, and the second with
`--pick alpha --pick small`.

pf 0.36.0 refuses the first today, on the same source with its choice-group
lines removed:

  E_UNSUPPORTED_CONSTRUCT: the deferred link 'direct.md' names 'alpha' beside the pick token '?'; direct.md declares the alternatives 'alpha', 'beta', and the link must name exactly one of them or carry '?' alone

pf 0.36.0 holds a deferred link to naming exactly one alternative, or the pick
token alone. The form holds it to naming exactly one alternative of each
group, or the pick token, which leaves open every group the link names no
alternative of. A dispatch still leaves at most one group open, since its pick
is made by the dispatching agent from one published list.
