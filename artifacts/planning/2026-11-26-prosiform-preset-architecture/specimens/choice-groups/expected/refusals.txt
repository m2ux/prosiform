Hand-written specimen: pf 0.36.0 refuses the source's choice-group field.

Each case states the title `../proposed/entry.md` gives its dispatch of
`worker.md`, and what the build then writes to standard error.

1. No member of the component group: "small"

E_ALTERNATIVE_UNRESOLVED: worker.md needs one alternative of its group 'component' in force:
  alpha  The requested component is alpha.
  beta   The requested component is beta.
  source: worker.md
  remedy: name the alternative that fits your task in the title or tooltip of each link reaching it, a link entry's among them

Exit status 2. pf 0.36.0 refuses the same title today, on the control
`../../missing-component`.

2. No member of the test group: "alpha"

E_ALTERNATIVE_UNRESOLVED: worker.md needs one alternative of its group 'test' in force:
  small  The test is small.
  large  The test is large.
  source: worker.md
  remedy: name the alternative that fits your task in the title or tooltip of each link reaching it, a link entry's among them

Exit status 2. This is the behaviour the form changes: pf 0.36.0 builds this
title on the control `../../missing-test-axis`, exit status 0, with the test
slot unselected.

3. Two members of one group: "alpha, beta, small"

E_ALTERNATIVE_UNRESOLVED: worker.md needs one alternative of its group 'component' in force, and the link names two:
  alpha  The requested component is alpha.
  beta   The requested component is beta.
  source: worker.md
  remedy: name the alternative that fits your task in the title or tooltip of each link reaching it, a link entry's among them

Exit status 2.

4. A dispatch leaving both groups open: "?"

E_ALTERNATIVE_UNRESOLVED: worker.md needs one alternative of each of its groups 'component' and 'test' in force, and a dispatch leaves one group to the pick:
  component
    alpha  The requested component is alpha.
    beta   The requested component is beta.
  test
    small  The test is small.
    large  The test is large.
  source: worker.md
  remedy: name an alternative of 'component' beside the pick token to leave 'test' to the pick, or one of 'test' to leave 'component'

Exit status 2. The dispatching agent picks within one group. Two open groups
would publish one bundle for each pair of members, and no such set is written.

5. A group no selected link depends on

A document whose selected content names no key of a group needs no member of
it, as pf 0.36.0 needs no alternative for content none of them changes. The
proposed worker has one section, which names both slots, so this set does not
exercise the rule: a second section writing neither routed link, composed by
`[↳ Hand-off](worker.md#hand-off)`, would bundle with no alternative named.

6. An alternative naming no group

A document whose alternatives state no `choice-group` behaves as it does under
pf 0.36.0: they form its one unnamed group, and its diagnostics read as today's,
with no group named.

Every refusal reports E_ALTERNATIVE_UNRESOLVED, the code a missing or
conflicting pick reports today. The form adds no code.
