## Overview

The contract states imports as accepted from their specimen set: pure settings reused at their declaring origin and receiving role.

## Problem

Cross-source reuse has no defined exact-target, impurity or conflict boundary.

## Proposal

- The contract states imports as a list of path and preset references, with exact preset lookup by the existing complete-name normalization.
- Only that preset's metadata is selected and counted as selected; no implicit default or body is.
- Only levels, substitutions and recursive pure imports are admitted.
- Equal values coalesce; incompatible values, imported or local, fail with both origins. Existing selected-over-default layering stays separate.
- The matrix covers lexical origins, cycles, missing and impure targets, ordinary scope, entry maps, entry stubs, dispatch and deferral.
