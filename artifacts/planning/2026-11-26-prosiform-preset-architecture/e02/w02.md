## Overview

The complete pure import graph preserves substitution origins, role reach and ledger origins.

## Problem

Nested level and substitution imports need explicit cycle and conflict boundaries and one complete public admission point.

## Proposal

- The delivered capture service gains the bounded graph resolver, key-relative substitutions and equal or conflicting value handling.
- The imports metadata is admitted when both setting kinds and every refusal work.
- Resulting settings feed the existing role resolution and the ledger; no inheritance and no second substitution engine is created.
- The README's design link moves to the contract revision stating imports in the same change, with the import fixtures and the codes page.
- A first checkpoint at ninety minutes stops for a replan before existing path or scope machinery is replaced.
