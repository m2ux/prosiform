## Overview

A pure import-capture service reads one exact named preset declaration with its source identity and purity.

## Problem

Resolution needs a trusted captured declaration boundary before role integration and nested substitution handling.

## Proposal

- A bounded capture and lookup service reads real source files through the existing complete-name normalization.
- It returns the exact preset metadata and its lexical origin, and accounts for the selection without a body or default.
- Direct levels exercise the seam end to end.
- The imports metadata is admitted publicly only with the complete resolver, so no partly supported form is released.
- A first checkpoint at sixty minutes returns a replan where a broad source-reader or normalization redesign would be needed.
