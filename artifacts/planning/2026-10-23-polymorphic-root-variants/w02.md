**Component:** src/prosiform/cli.py

## Overview

One physical file reached under two effective roots is captured once and emitted once for each root. Each emission resolves that file's links under its own root.

## Problem

A file is stored and expanded under its filesystem path. A second parent that reaches the same bytes under another root adds no second rendering, and the file's links are resolved once.

## Proposal

The scene is A reaching B under one root and C under another, with B and C both reaching D, and D omitting its root.

- D's bytes are captured once.
- D is emitted once under B's effective root and once under C's effective root.
- The emission under B's root resolves D's links under that root. The emission under C's root resolves them under C's root.
- When B and C resolve to the same effective root, D is emitted once.
