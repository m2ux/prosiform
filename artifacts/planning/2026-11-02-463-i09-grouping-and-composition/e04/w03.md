**Component:** main:src/prosiform/composition.py

## Overview

Inserted content keeps its own source: its links and reference definitions resolve against the fragment's document, and each marked occurrence contributes its own content.

## Problem

- **Inserted links would resolve against the caller.**
  A relative link inside a composed fragment names a path relative to its own document.

## Proposal

**The scene.** W02 composes sections into their scope.

- **Source-relative resolution.**
  Links and reference definitions inside a fragment resolve against its source, and diagnostics inside it name that source.
- **Every occurrence contributes.**
  Repeated marked references each contribute their own nested content.
