**Component:** main:src/prosiform/composition.py

## Overview

A composed section becomes a child of the nearest enclosing heading, references sharing a title in that scope share one destination section, and each contribution sits under its source parent's name with its structure kept.

## Problem

- **A recognised directive places nothing.**
  Recognition alone leaves the target bundled as an ordinary reference.

## Proposal

**The scene.** W01 recognises the directive.

- **The destination scope.**
  The nearest enclosing heading of the occurrence is the scope; a referenced section becomes its child.
- **Shared titles merge.**
  References carrying one title within the scope share one destination section.
- **Contributions keep their parent.**
  Each contribution sits beneath a heading naming the immediate parent of its source section, with its blocks and relative depth kept.
