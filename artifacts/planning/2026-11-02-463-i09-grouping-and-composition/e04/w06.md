**Component:** main:src/prosiform/composition.py

## Overview

A composition target must declare `prosiform`, a deferred occurrence keeps its destination and composes when selected, and composing a contract leaves a deferred implementation absent.

## Problem

- **An ineligible target has no refusal.**
  A directive naming a target that declares no `prosiform`, or an external resource, asks for composition nothing can answer.
- **A deferred occurrence has no retained destination.**
  Activation on a later compilation needs the scope the occurrence named.

## Proposal

**The scene.** W05 bounds recursion; deferral withholds a target until selected.

- **The eligible target.**
  A target declaring no `prosiform`, or an external resource, is refused with a source-located diagnostic.
- **Deferred composition.**
  A deferred occurrence retains its destination section; activating it composes and validates it there, preserving nested deferrals.
- **Contracts without bodies.**
  Composing a contract section leaves its deferred implementation body absent.
