**Component:** main:src/prosiform/composition.py

## Overview

Composition ordering and title matching are deterministic, and each structural failure is a source-located diagnostic.

## Problem

- **Order and matching are unstated in the build.**
  Generated sections need one order and one matching rule so a bundle is reproducible.

## Proposal

**The scene.** W03 composes contributions with their source.

- **Ordering.**
  Generated children sit after the containing section's own content and before its existing children, in first-reference order; contributions follow reference order.
- **Matching.**
  Titles match exactly after decoding, with no case folding or normalisation.
- **Diagnostics.**
  A missing containing heading, missing source or parent section, colliding child heading or unrepresentable depth is a source-located diagnostic.
