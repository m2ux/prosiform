**Component:** main:src/prosiform/cli.py

## Overview

A composed reference obeys the rules every link obeys: its containing heading's title selects the target's presets, and the writing document's `subst` redirects it.

## Problem

- **Composition could bypass the link rules.**
  A directive is a link, and a reader expects presets and `subst` to apply to it as to any other.

## Proposal

**The scene.** Preset selection reads the title of the section holding a link; `subst` redirects the references the declaring document writes.

- **Presets.**
  The containing heading's title selects the target's presets.
- **Substitution.**
  The writer's `subst` redirects a composed reference before its target is read.
