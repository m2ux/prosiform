**Component:** main:src/prosiform/cli.py

## Overview

Refuses two composition plans that contradict themselves: a deferred directive whose scope selects other presets of its target than the target's own heading selects, and a scope that composes a section and also defers it.

## Problem

- **A deferral could compile otherwise than composed.**
  A directive's scope title selects its target's presets, but the run activating a deferred directive compiles the target as a section entry, which selects by the target's own heading, and no option names the referrer. A step `Fetch the page offline` deferring `Implementation` would have its body compiled without `offline`.
- **One scope could place a section twice over.**
  `[→](x.md#s)` and `[¬→](x.md#s)` in one scope state that the section is placed now and left to a later run at once.

## Proposal

**The scene.** `refusals_of_directives` in `cli.py` refuses each directive no section holds or answers, per source; `withheld` yields each deferred target, and the closure reads every deferred target once the frontier is exhausted, confirming its heading.

- **Two placements of one section.**
  Each `Use` carries the line of the heading holding it. `refusals_of_directives` keys each eligible directive by that heading, the target's path and fragment, and whether it defers; meeting the opposite placement refuses the later directive under `E_UNSUPPORTED_CONSTRUCT`, naming both labels and locations.
- **A deferral held to its target heading.**
  `withheld` gives each target the title of the section the link is written in, and marks a deferred directive's as composing. Once the target is read, `refusals_of_deferrals` compares the presets that title selects with those the target's heading selects (`entry_title`, `keywords`), and refuses a difference under `E_UNSUPPORTED_CONSTRUCT`, naming the link and both selections. An immediate directive is not checked.
- **Design.**
  *Presets and `subst`* states the deferral rule and *Deferred composition* the paired-directive rule, each linking the other where it needs it; the Composition fixture list names both. The README pin and the glossary follow.
