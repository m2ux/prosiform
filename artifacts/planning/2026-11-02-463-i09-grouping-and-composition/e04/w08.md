**Component:** main:src/prosiform/cli.py

## Overview

A target reached only by composition is not also emitted as a separately grouped copy, while one also reached by an ordinary dependency keeps its ordinary inclusion.

## Problem

- **A composed target is also bundled.**
  Assembly places every reached document in a group, so a section composed in place would appear twice.

## Proposal

**The scene.** W02 composes sections into their scope; assembly groups every selected document.

- **Inline-only targets.**
  A document reached by directives alone joins no group.
- **Mixed reach.**
  A document also reached by an ordinary link keeps its ordinary inclusion.
