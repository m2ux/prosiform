**Component:** main:src/prosiform/composition.py

## Overview

Recursive composition reports a cycle on the active path with its chain, and materialised content counts against the declared budgets.

## Problem

- **Nested directives can loop.**
  A composed fragment may itself carry a directive, and a cycle would expand without bound.
- **Materialised content is uncounted.**
  A fragment composed several times multiplies the output the budgets bound.

## Proposal

**The scene.** W04 orders and diagnoses composition; the limits module declares the budgets.

- **Cycles.**
  A directive reaching a section already on the active path is refused with the chain.
- **Budgets.**
  Each materialised fragment counts against the expansion and output budgets.
