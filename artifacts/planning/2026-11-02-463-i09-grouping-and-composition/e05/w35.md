## Overview

The eval page states each `scenarios.toml` key, and the `undeclared/` directory, as a paragraph of its own.

## Problem

- **Two statements render as one paragraph.**
  The `undeclared/` statement and the `warnings` key sit on consecutive lines, so the page renders them as one paragraph, and the key reads as part of what the `undeclared/` directory does.

## Proposal

- **One paragraph per statement.**
  The `warnings` key follows the `entry` key it extends, and the `undeclared/` statement stands after it, separated by a blank line.
