**Component:** main:src/prosiform/candidate.py

## Overview

Candidate validation refuses a candidate in which one emitted heading answers the targets of two members of one group, so the rule that a reference reaches the document it named holds by the build rather than by construction alone.

## Problem

- **Target integrity reads one target at a time.**
  It asks whether a destination's heading answers the target assigned to it, so a heading answering two members' targets passes for both.
- **A construction regression stays green.**
  A merged heading recorded as answering every contributor's fragment is what commit 02ec51b1 repaired, and no check would have caught it.

## Proposal

**The scene.** Each emitted heading records the targets it answers. A merged group's merged heading answers a fragment only where one document contributes it, a variant heading answers its own document, and a member subsection answers that member.

- **One member per heading.**
  Within a merged group, a heading whose answered targets name two documents fails validation under the one-intended-destination invariant, naming the heading and both documents.
- **The failure is the candidate's.**
  It surfaces as candidate validation does, with its named code, and nothing is published.
