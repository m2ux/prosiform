## Overview

## Problem

- **The rule is a consequence, not an invariant.**
  *A heading for every member* concludes that no two members of one group reach one destination, and *Post-compilation invariants* lists no check holding it.
- **The check needs a clause to cite.**
  A fixture or test for the refusal names a clause, and none states that validation holds the rule.

## Proposal

**The scene.** *Post-compilation invariants* lists one intended destination and destination stability; *Merged section references* and *A heading for every member* state where a pooled or whole-document reference resolves.

- **One destination answers one member.**
  Within a merged group, an emitted heading answers the targets of at most one member, and candidate validation checks it against the provenance each heading records.
- **The pin moves.**
  The README's design link moves to the engineering commit carrying the amendment.
