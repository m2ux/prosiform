**Component:** main:invocation.md

## Overview

The invocation guide states that a file's `group` pair places its own sections wherever a parent bundles it.

## Problem

- **The guide states pairs without placement.**
  It states which groups a document joins and at what depth, and not whose pairs place a linked file's sections.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The guide's declaration paragraph states presets and `group` pairs.

- **One sentence on placement.**
  A file's pairs place its own sections in the result its parent merges; a parent's pair applies when it is bundled in turn.
