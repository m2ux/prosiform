## Overview

The hierarchy page states the heading rule the build emits for a titled group member, and names no retired group-heading rule.

## Problem

- **The page states member names without the title rule.**
  *Member names* states the distinguishing name and omits that a member's H1 is absorbed into its subsection where their slugs agree, and where a reference to it resolves.

## Proposal

**The scene.** *Groups* and *Member names* state membership, variants and the subsection a member takes.

- **The titled member's heading.**
  A member's subsection absorbs its own H1 where their slugs agree, and a whole-document reference to it resolves there.
- **Nesting is stated.**
  An intermediate file's group sits inside the result its parent merges.
