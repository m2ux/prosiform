**Component:** src/prosiform/dispatch.py

## Overview

A run whose dispatches cannot be followed publishes nothing: a cycle, a dispatch with no heading level left for its sub-section, and an authored heading that would collide with one pf emits are each refused. The warning keyed on numbered steps is retired, since a dispatched target's shape is the corpus's convention.

## Problem

- **A cycle publishes.**
  [`analysed`](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/45acf3e1dd6228a3d99ea12406c9926cbfcadd4e/src/prosiform/dispatch.py#L202-L247) warns `W_DISPATCH_CYCLE` on a chain that would start sub-agents without end, and the bundle is written.
- **A procedure is keyed on ordered lists.**
  [`procedural`](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/45acf3e1dd6228a3d99ea12406c9926cbfcadd4e/src/prosiform/dispatch.py#L250-L267) warns `W_DISPATCH_NOT_PROCEDURAL` on a target carrying no numbered step, so a procedure written as step sub-sections warns.
- **Generated headings can collide.**
  Once pf emits `Dispatch <link text>` and `Prosiform` headings, an authored heading of the same text would give two sections one name.

## Proposal

A source whose `review.md` dispatches `plan.md` and whose `plan.md` dispatches `review.md` ends with exit 1, naming the chain `review.md → plan.md → review.md`, and the output folder is untouched.

- **A cycle is refused.**
  A chain of bundles returning to one already on it publishes nothing under `E_DISPATCH_CYCLE`, naming the chain; a dispatch of the bundle's own selection is the shortest cycle.
- **No heading level left.**
  A dispatch in a level-6 section is refused under `E_HEADING_DEPTH_OVERFLOW`, naming the link: its sub-section would be emitted beyond H6.
- **pf's headings are its own.**
  An authored heading whose text equals one pf emits in the same bundle — `Prosiform`, `Dispatch <link text>` or `Dispatch in parallel` — is refused under `E_HEADING_COLLISION`, naming both.
- **Kept warnings.**
  A chain deeper than three still warns, and the self-dispatch warning is kept for a dispatch that closes no cycle.
- **The step-shape warning is retired.**
  `W_DISPATCH_NOT_PROCEDURAL` and `procedural` are removed.
- **The contract moves with the build.**
  The design's Dispatch clauses and declared codes, the pin and `invocation.md` state the refusals and the retired warning.
