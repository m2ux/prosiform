**Component:** main:src/prosiform/bundle.py

## Overview

Deferred links carry the preset selection their later commands need. Observe publication and immediate/deferred selection parity through the real compiler and exact-output corpus.

## Problem

The merged compiler preserves title tags, but the dedicated acceptance evidence must connect a published deferred link to its executed later command and compare its selected content with immediate composition under unrelated headings.

## Proposal

**The scene.** Bundle rewriting retains ordinary deferred titles, and composition emits deferred activation lines with their decoded title. The [design](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/795bbfb67c66b02193f0313138f59f18aa6c5274/artifacts/planning/2026-10-17-prosiform-v1/design.md) binds both forms to the same explicit preset command.

Add exact-output fixtures for rebased plain deferral, generated composition handoff, immediate composition and the prescribed activated command. Exercise inline and reference-style links, complete multiword names, case/repetition/unknown requests, Unicode and empty selections. Execute the command derived from the actual published destination/title and compare selected payloads with immediate compilation; a command omitting meaningful tags must produce the default payload. Preserve the existing source implementation unless an observing failure requires a repair.
