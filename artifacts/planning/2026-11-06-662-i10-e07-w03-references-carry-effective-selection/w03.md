**Component:** main:src/prosiform/closure.py

## Overview

References retain occurrence-local preset requests through resolution and substitution. A separate immutable bundle identity contains the canonical destination and its effective named presets.

## Problem

The [reference model at the unit base](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/227069a8fed00554deb7f044da37f396f35a3208/src/prosiform/closure.py#L131-L174) carries destination and heading context without the title tags the source reader exposes.

## Proposal

**The scene.** Parsed source occurrences supply normalized title tags under the [preset-selection contract](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/c67cf78f020923c4e5bd25d95497ced206be7161/artifacts/planning/2026-10-17-prosiform-v1/design.md#presets).

`Reference.tags` retains the request through every resolution route and substitution. Immediate, deferred and dispatch traversal keeps distinct tag sets while physical capture remains keyed by source path. `matching_presets` answers complete normalized names in declaration order; `bundle_selection` produces a frozen destination/fragment/effective-name key retaining declared spelling. Unknown names and implicit default add no named selection. Runtime activation and variant scheduling consume these values in their own components.
