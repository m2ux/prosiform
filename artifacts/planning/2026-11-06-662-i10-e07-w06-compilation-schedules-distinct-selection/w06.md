## Design

`Compiled.selection` identifies one canonical document, fragment and matched declared preset set. `Compiled.hops` maps each effective outgoing selection to its first published occurrence. Scheduling, memoization and dispatch analysis use those keys; filenames remain the design's independent deterministic serialization. Capture and input revalidation remain physical-source facts.

Each compilation passes captured target conversions to heading/group helpers. Shared substitution records distinguish declared alternatives, including two fragments of one file; unused-pair checks retain the declaring preset and alternative.
