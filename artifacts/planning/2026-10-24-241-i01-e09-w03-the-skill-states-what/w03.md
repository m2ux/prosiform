**Component:** skills/work-planner/SKILL.md

## Overview

A session reading a bundle of the work-planner skill meets the dispatch rule as part of the bundle's opening instruction, rather than in a mode's prose where it applies to every reader.

## Problem

The skill's entry point opens with `name` and `description` alone, so a bundle of any of its modes carries no rule for reading a dispatched link. Deliver mode states in prose that a sub-agent works in the unit's worktree and reads the brief, but the brief and the work item it names are ordinary links, indistinguishable from a link the reading agent compiles for itself.

## Proposal

Declare `prosiform.dispatch` in `skills/work-planner/SKILL.md` stating what a `»` link means for this skill: the reading agent compiles it and gives the result to a sub-agent. Because the entry document is the source of the value, every mode bundle opens with that instruction after the entry sentence.

Mark the two links a dispatched session compiles — deliver mode's links to the delivery brief and to the work item — with `»`, so a bundle says of those two that their material belongs to a dispatched session rather than to the session reading the bundle. Neither target contributes content to the bundle.

Keep the prose sentence naming the dispatch in the mode that performs it: the metadata states what the mark means, and the mode states what a session does.
