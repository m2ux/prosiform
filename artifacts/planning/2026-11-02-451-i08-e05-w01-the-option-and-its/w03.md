**Component:** main:fixtures/limits/a-file-count-budget-crossed/manifest.toml

## Overview

Every corpus fixture that passed a lettered budget passes its name, and every expected result stands as it was.

## Problem

Twenty-one manifests carry a letter, and the corpus is what proves the ceilings still bound.

- **Twenty-one fixtures pass one.** `-F` in six, `-N` in four, `-B` in three, `-L` in three, `-E` in two, and `-I`, `-O` and `-T` in one each, across `limits`, `inclusion`, `dispatch`, `file-wide-safety` and `parser-limit`.
- **Each declares an expected result.** A fixture crossing a ceiling expects `E_RESOURCE_LIMIT`; one staying under it expects a bundle; a refused override expects `E_INVALID_USAGE`. None changes when the spelling does.
- **The figures stay with their ceilings.** `-N` bounds container and YAML nesting independently and `-L` counts authored occurrences, so a figure moves with its ceiling and not with another.

## Proposal

**The scene.** A manifest declares `arguments` as a list, one element per argv entry.

**The design.**

- **Each letter becomes its name.** `["-F", "3"]` becomes `["--budget", "files=3"]`, and the joined `"-F=-1"` becomes `"--budget=files=-1"`, so the value travels with the name.
- **No expected result changes.** Every manifest's `expected` block is untouched, which states the behaviour did not move.
- **The refused overrides name their clause.** The zero and negative override fixtures declare `Resource limits — Malformed entry`, the clause the pinned design states for a value that is no positive decimal integer.
- **A new fixture bounds a run by a name.** `fixtures/limits/a-named-budget-bounds-the-run` passes `--budget files=1` over a tree opening two sources.
