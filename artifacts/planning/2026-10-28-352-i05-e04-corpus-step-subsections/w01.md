**Component:** example/work-planner/guidelines.md

## Overview

The example corpus's guidelines state the step convention: a procedure writes each step as a heading-rooted sub-section, so a link can name one step precisely. Every later conversion follows this rule, and the shared guidelines define a procedure by its step sub-sections.

## Problem

- **A step roots no anchor.**
  A numbered list item has no heading, so a link can name a procedure but not its fourth step, and the corpus writes `step 4` as prose.
- **The rules require the numbered form.**
  [`guidelines.md`](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/9851615028ea489c48f6b3fa5ddbe0d917438cf2/example/work-planner/guidelines.md#L33-L44) states Numbered steps, Procedure steps and Operation steps as a numbered list, and the shared [`guidelines.md`](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/9851615028ea489c48f6b3fa5ddbe0d917438cf2/example/guidelines.md#L57) defines a procedure as a section carrying a numbered step.

## Proposal

A reader of plan mode's guidelines finds that the publish step is `### 6. Publish` under `## Procedure`, linked as `plan-mode.md#6-publish`.

- **One sub-section per step.**
  One heading level below the procedure's heading, in the order performed, ahead of the procedure's child sections.
- **Numbered and titled.**
  The heading is the step's position, a full stop and its action in title case; a mode or protocol step's bold label becomes the action, and an artifact step gains one.
- **Inserting a step renumbers.**
  The anchors of the steps below an inserted step change; `pf`'s unresolved-link check reports a link left naming an old one.
- **Body.**
  The instruction opens the sub-section; its bullets, branches and command blocks follow as they do under a numbered step today.
- **A procedure is its steps.**
  The shared guidelines define a procedure as a section whose step sub-sections it holds; a single-step procedure stays one paragraph or bullet.
- **Rules restated.**
  Numbered steps, Sub-bullets, Separate actions, Procedure steps and Operation steps state the sub-section form.
