**Component:** example/work-planner/modes

## Overview

Every mode procedure writes its steps as sub-sections, so a dispatching session, a guideline or a reviewer can link the one step it means.

## Problem

The ten modes hold 86 numbered steps across 12 procedures; `deliver-mode.md` alone holds 22, beside two `###` sub-procedures whose own steps are numbered too.

## Proposal

`plan-mode.md`'s `6. **Publish.**` becomes `### 6. Publish` under `## Procedure`, its instruction and bullets beneath.

- **Labels become headings.**
  Each bold step label becomes its sub-section's action, after the step's number, in title case.
- **Sub-procedures.**
  A `###` sub-procedure such as Run Delivery Session follows the steps, and its own steps become `####` sub-sections.
- **The worked example.**
  `example/README.md`'s excerpt of plan mode's publish step is the converted text.
- **Fixtures.**
  Each usage-example fixture whose entry reaches a mode takes the expected bundle the converted corpus compiles to.
