**Component:** example/work-planner/artifacts

## Overview

Every artifact operation writes its steps as sub-sections, each headed by its number and an action, so a mode or protocol can link the one step of an operation it means.

## Problem

The 31 artifacts hold 423 numbered steps across 118 operations, the bulk of the corpus, and their steps carry no label a heading could take: the guidelines' Operation steps rule forbids one.

## Proposal

In `issue.md`, Save Tracker Issue's `3. For a new issue, run Create Tracker Issue …` becomes `#### 3. Create New Issue` under `### *Save* Tracker Issue`.

- **An action per step.**
  Each step gains a short action, after its number, written from its leading instruction.
- **Existing sub-sections.**
  `issue-body.md` Verifiable and the Board Reads steps keep their place, after the operation's steps.
- **Fixtures.**
  Each usage-example fixture whose entry reaches an artifact takes the expected bundle the converted corpus compiles to.
