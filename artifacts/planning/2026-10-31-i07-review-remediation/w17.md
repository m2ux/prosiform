**Component:** fixtures

## Overview

The publication and title-dispatch refusals W02 delivered, which no page, catalogue row or corpus fixture states.

## Problem

W02 refuses a non-folder at `<entry>.bundles` with `E_PUBLICATION_FAILED`, and a dispatch in a heading the bundle emits as text with `E_UNSUPPORTED_CONSTRUCT`. `docs/bundles.md` still says a path there is renamed aside, `docs/dispatch.md` names no title-dispatch refusal, and the diagnostics catalogue lists neither case. Only `src/tests` prove them, while initiative AC1 reads the corpus run and the catalogue.

The finding was raised by the review of the merged I07:E00 work, against W02's delivery.

## Proposal

**The scene.** An author meets the refusal and looks it up. The catalogue row, the page and a corpus fixture each state it.

**The design.**

- **Each refusal has a corpus fixture.** A fixture over a file at the run folder's path, and fixtures over an absorbed member title and a title above the selection.
- **The catalogue and the pages agree with the build.** The two catalogue rows name the cases, and the pages state refusal where they stated renaming aside.
