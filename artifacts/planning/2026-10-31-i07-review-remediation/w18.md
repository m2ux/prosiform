**Component:** fixtures/README.md

## Overview

The scalar ceiling W06 delivered, which the diagnostics catalogue and the validation page describe inaccurately.

## Problem

The catalogue says `E_RESOURCE_LIMIT` means a declared budget the run crossed, and the scalar ceiling is not a declared budget. `docs/validation.md` says every scalar within the ceiling costs a pass over its own digits, while the cost is bounded rather than linear. The docstring at `metadata.py` documents `PATTERNS` where it describes `PREFIXED`, and the refusal message calls a float with a short exponent quadratic.

The finding was raised by the review of the merged I07:E00 work, against W06's delivery.

## Proposal

**The scene.** A reader meets `E_RESOURCE_LIMIT` from a long scalar and looks it up. The catalogue names the ceiling as well as declared budgets.

**The design.**

- **The catalogue names both limits.** The `E_RESOURCE_LIMIT` row names the scalar ceiling beside declared budgets.
- **The page and the code say the same.** The validation page states the bounded cost, and the docstring sits on the constant it describes.
