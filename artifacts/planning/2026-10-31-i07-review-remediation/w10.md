**Component:** fixtures

## Overview

Two form faults where a fixture cannot measure what it claims: a bundle Mermaid cannot parse, and a linked entry carrying the labels it exists to avoid.

## Problem

The AC11 fixture ships a bundle Mermaid cannot parse, and the render check omits that fixture. The linked form ships its own flowchart carrying the branch labels, so it cannot measure link-following.

The finding is recorded as findings 21 and 22 in the [merged-epic review](../../2026-10-31-merged-epic-review/findings.md), with its site and the case that failed.

## Proposal

**The scene.** Each defect above was found by the review of fifteen merged epics, and each has a case that reproduces it. The task repairs one scope area and carries that case, so the defect cannot return unnoticed.

**The design.**

- **The repair follows the finding.** Correct the fixture and list it in the render check; remove the branch label from the linked form's entry document.
- **The case ships with the fix.** The failing case the finding records becomes a check the repository runs, so a regression fails a target rather than waiting for another review.
- **No other task's area is touched.** Each task repairs one scope and carries its own failing case, so the faults land as separate pull requests.
