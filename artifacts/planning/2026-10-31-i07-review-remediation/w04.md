**Component:** src/prosiform/heading.py

## Overview

Two naming faults that publish headings an author cannot tell apart.

## Problem

Two distinct group names title-case to the same empty heading and publish as indistinguishable H1s. A member heading can be empty, emitting an empty heading and an empty-fragment destination with no diagnostic.

The finding is recorded as findings 37 and 39 in the [merged-epic review](../../2026-10-31-merged-epic-review/findings.md), with its site and the case that failed.

## Proposal

**The scene.** Each defect above was found by the review of fifteen merged epics, and each has a case that reproduces it. The task repairs one scope area and carries that case, so the defect cannot return unnoticed.

**The design.**

- **The repair follows the finding.** Refuse a group name whose title case is empty, and let a member whose name title-cases to nothing take its path text or be refused.
- **The case ships with the fix.** The failing case the finding records becomes a check the repository runs, so a regression fails a target rather than waiting for another review.
- **No other task's area is touched.** Each task repairs one scope and carries its own failing case, so the faults land as separate pull requests.
