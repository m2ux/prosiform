**Component:** src/prosiform/heading.py

## Overview

Three arrangement faults: two title-case conventions in one bundle, a titled parent's whole-document reference landing in its first section, and an overview wrapper with nothing to carry.

## Problem

One helper destroys separators where the other keeps them, so one bundle carries two title-case conventions. A titled parent's whole-document reference resolves inside its own first section at grouping depth 1. The overview wrapper is emitted with no above-depth content to carry.

The finding is recorded as findings 28, 38 and 40 in the [merged-epic review](../../2026-10-31-merged-epic-review/findings.md), with its site and the case that failed.

## Proposal

**The scene.** Each defect above was found by the review of fifteen merged epics, and each has a case that reproduces it. The task repairs one scope area and carries that case, so the defect cannot return unnoticed.

**The design.**

- **The repair follows the finding.** Hold every emitted heading to one title-casing convention that preserves separators, resolve a titled parent's whole-document reference to its own title heading at every depth, and emit the wrapper only where there is above-depth content.
- **The case ships with the fix.** The failing case the finding records becomes a check the repository runs, so a regression fails a target rather than waiting for another review.
- **No other task's area is touched.** Each task repairs one scope and carries its own failing case, so the faults land as separate pull requests.
