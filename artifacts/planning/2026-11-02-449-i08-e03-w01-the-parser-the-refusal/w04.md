**Component:** main:invocation.md

## Overview

The option table loses its row, and the guide states `document#fragment` as the one spelling for a section.

## Problem

The option is documented in the guide and named beside it.

- **The table carries the row.** `invocation.md` states `-f DOC`, and `make invocation` holds that table to the help output in both directions.
- **The positional row states three forms.** `REF` is given as `document`, `document#fragment`, or `#fragment with -f`.
- **The mapping prose names a referring document** among the references a mapping redirects, in `invocation.md` and in the glossary's entry.
- **The authoring check states the row as owned by the guide** and lists `-f` among the options taking a value.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The table's rows are clauses `make invocation` compares against the build's own help, and the authoring check reads the guide's worked commands.

**The design.**

- **The row is removed,** and the positional row states `document` and `document#fragment` alone, mirroring the help string `cli.parser` gives.
- **The guide states one form.** Its prose names a section as `document#fragment`, and no example passes the removed option.
- **The mapping prose drops the referring document,** in the guide and the glossary.
- **The authoring check loses the `-f` statement and value-taking entry.**
