## Overview

The v1 design states that a Mermaid click's tooltip carries its link's operator and title before the build reads it, so the fixtures can name the clauses they encode and the documentation can pin the commit stating the rule.

## Problem

- **The design states the opposite rule.**
  Presets' **Selection belongs to the link** says a Mermaid tooltip selects no named preset and a followed Mermaid destination uses its target's `default`; the Presets fixture family lists Mermaid tooltips selecting no named presets.
- **Link classification gives a diagram link no operator.**
  Its **Examples** rule follows and rewrites a diagram destination as a link but states nothing of an operator.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The v1 design on the engineering branch is the contract the fixtures cite by clause and the documentation pins by commit.

- **Presets.**
  A click's or class diagram link's tooltip, less its opening marks, selects presets as a link title does, `?` included; the tooltip-selects-nothing statement leaves.
- **Link classification.**
  Marks opening such a tooltip, followed by a blank or its end, state the link's operator as marks opening a link's text do, accepted and refused alike; a mark followed directly by text opens none; the destination is published as the operator states and the tooltip carried as written.
- **Fixture families.**
  The Presets family lists tooltips selecting presets in place of selecting none, and the operator families list a click carrying each operator.
