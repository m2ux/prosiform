## Overview

The documentation states the click tooltip rule once, in Diagram links, and the pages that said a diagram link selects nothing link it.

## Problem

- **Diagram links states the old rule.**
  Its last paragraph says a diagram link carries no operator and Mermaid tooltips supply no preset tags.
- **Two pages repeat it.**
  The glossary's pick entry and Selection's exactly-one-alternative rule say a diagram link selects no preset.

## Proposal

**The scene.** Diagram links in `docs/references.md` is the home of every rule about diagram destinations.

- **Diagram links states the rule.**
  The tooltip's opening marks state the operator under Operators, the rest is the link's title, the destination is published as the operator states and the tooltip carried as written, with `click Intake href "activities/intake.md" "⇉"` as its example.
- **Other pages link it.**
  The glossary and Selection drop the no-selection claim and link Diagram links where they need the rule.
- **Design links are pinned.**
  Links to the design name the commit that states the rule.
