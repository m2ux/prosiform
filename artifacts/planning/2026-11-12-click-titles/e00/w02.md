## Overview

The corpus states the click tooltip rule, one fixture per rule, and the fixture encoding the retired rule leaves.

## Problem

- **One fixture encodes the retired rule.**
  `diagram-tooltips-supply-no-preset-selection` expects a tooltip naming a preset to select nothing and the preset to warn unused.
- **No fixture puts an operator on a click.**
  Every click fixture follows an ordinary link.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The corpus is the contract any build is held to; each fixture names the design clauses it encodes.

- **One fixture per rule.**
  A click dispatching with `"⇉"`, a click deferring with `"¬"`, a click tooltip selecting a preset, a click tooltip picking an alternative, and a refused combination, each naming the clauses the design now states.
- **The retired fixture leaves.**
  `diagram-tooltips-supply-no-preset-selection` is removed with its sources and expected bundle.
- **Stale descriptions are corrected.**
  A manifest description saying a diagram link selects no preset, such as `a-diagram-link-alone-reaching-alternatives-fails`, states its case as a click with no tooltip.
