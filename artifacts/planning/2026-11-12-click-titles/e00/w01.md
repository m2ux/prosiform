## Overview

A click's tooltip is read as its link's title: opening marks state the operator, the rest selects presets, and the bundle publishes the click as that operator states.

## Problem

- **The tooltip is matched and dropped.**
  `QUOTED` matches an optional tooltip after the destination without a group, so `Click` holds none.
- **Every click is an ordinary link.**
  `uses` yields each click with an empty opening and no tags, and `carried` does the same for candidate validation, so the operator and selection machinery never sees a tooltip.
- **Docstrings state the old rule.**
  `read_diagram`, `Use` and the `profile.py` module docstring describe a tooltip as text that carries nothing.

## Proposal

**The scene.** A Markdown link reaches the operator reader through its `opening` and the preset reader through its title tags; a click reaches both with nothing.

- **The tooltip is captured.**
  `QUOTED` captures the tooltip, and `Click` carries it beside the destination for `click` and class diagram `link` lines.
- **The tooltip is split once.**
  Marks opening it, followed by a blank or its end, become the use's opening, read by the same operator reader as a link's text; the remainder goes through `read_tags`. A mark followed directly by text leaves the whole tooltip to `read_tags`.
- **Every consumer takes the split.**
  `uses`, `carried` and `bundle.py`'s destination rewriting take the opening and tags from the click, so dispatch, deferral, parallel dispatch and composition, their refusals and the pick token apply unchanged.
- **Docstrings state the rule.**
  The docstrings that describe a tooltip as carrying nothing state the new rule.
