## Overview

A preset holding a non-empty one-line `choice` is read as an alternative, and the forms that cannot be one are refused while the document's metadata is decoded.

## Problem

- **A preset holds two keys.**
  `metadata.py` accepts `group` and `subst` in a preset and fails any other key as an unknown field, so `choice` is refused today.

## Proposal

**The scene.** `_preset` decodes each preset's mapping into a `Preset`, and `Conversion` holds the document's presets in declaration order.

- **`choice` is a third key.**
  Its value is a non-empty string holding no line break, carried on the `Preset` as the alternative's choice line.
- **`default` is no alternative.**
  A `choice` in `default` fails with `E_INVALID_CONVERSION_METADATA`, naming the field.
- **`?` names no preset.**
  A preset named `?` fails with `E_INVALID_CONVERSION_METADATA`, since `?` is the pick token.
- **The document exposes its alternatives.**
  `Conversion` lists its alternatives in declaration order for the selection rules to read.
