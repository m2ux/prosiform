## Overview

Candidate validation mode and the invariants it checks against recorded provenance.

## Problem

A reparse that agrees with itself establishes that the output parses, not that each region came from the source it claims, nor that a region is missing.

## Proposal

The candidate is reparsed under the same grammar in a policy context that expects generated filename headings, shifted headings and structural ancestors, and that does not apply frontmatter stripping, dependency discovery or generated-input rejection. The invariants the contract names are checked against provenance ranges and distinct generated-structure records. Nothing is published while a check fails.

## What the Provenance Records

Two record kinds, held apart so that neither can answer the other's question.

- **A range** names one selected source region by the half-open line range of its source's content it occupies, with the definition lines dropped inside it. Two regions carrying identical prose do not share that identity, which is what makes repeated prose distinguishable from duplication without counting strings.
- **A generated record** names a heading the bundle wrote itself and carries no source range, so a filename wrapper cannot stand in for a missing selected region.

The record sits on the group it describes rather than beside it, because a record travelling separately can be paired with the wrong group.

## The Invariants

Eleven of the thirteen the contract names are checked: target integrity, exactly-once coverage, no accidental omissions, no accidental expansion, no new links, no definition leakage, reference preservation, outline preservation, depth safety, group isolation, entry line integrity, and one intended destination per rewritten link.

The record is held to itself before the candidate is read, so a record claiming a heading beyond the sixth level is reported as the depth it lost rather than as a heading the parser cannot find.

## What This Row Does Not Check

- **Ownership stability** is a property of the construction, not of the candidate. Link ownership is read from a source's own token stream and a position in it, and the selection never enters the answer, so consolidation cannot change it. A before-and-after comparison would call one function twice with the same arguments, and a check that cannot fail reports a guarantee nobody has.
- **Block-syntax preservation** belongs to [I00:E08:AC1](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/issues/59). The pairing here holds the candidate's regions to the provenance one for one and says nothing about what a boundary merged inside one.
- **The structural-ancestor half of outline preservation** waits on [I00:E09:W02](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/issues/60), which is what emits a structural ancestor. What is checked is that each carried heading sits at its source level plus its group's shift, and that carried headings follow their source's order.
