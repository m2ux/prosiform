## Overview

The *Conversion metadata* clause states once what a declaration's presence means, and the pin names the revision carrying it, so the design's account of an empty `prosiform` mapping is one statement a reader and a fixture can cite.

## Problem

The pinned design's *Conversion metadata* clause states presence twice, and the two statements disagree.

- **The paragraph says an empty mapping declares nothing.**
  The paragraph under the field table lists "an absent or empty `prosiform` mapping" among the cases that declare nothing, so the document is text wherever a link names it.
- **The bullet says an empty mapping declares.**
  *An empty block declares* states that an empty `prosiform` mapping qualifies its document, which is what the build does and what `fixtures/inclusion/an-empty-block-declares` proves.
- **"Empty block" names two things.**
  The paragraph's "an empty block" is an empty frontmatter block, which *YAML grammar* reads as absent metadata; the bullet's is an empty `prosiform` mapping.

## Proposal

**The scene.** A reader asking whether `prosiform: {}` declares finds one answer at *An empty block declares*, and no sentence of the clause says otherwise.

**The design.**

- **One statement of presence.** The paragraph drops its presence sentence; *An empty block declares* carries every case: a present `prosiform` mapping declares, an empty one declares with no settings, and absent frontmatter, an empty or comments-only frontmatter block, and frontmatter with no `prosiform` key declare nothing.
- **The bullet keeps its title.** Fixtures cite `Conversion metadata — An empty block declares`, so the title stands and the citations keep resolving.
- **The pin moves.** `README.md`'s design link names the engineering commit carrying the edit.
