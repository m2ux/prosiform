## Overview

The independence check reads the contract's vocabulary from the design's behavioural sections and reports a word only a section stating no behaviour carries, whatever its shape. A build's words then stay out of the corpus however they are written.

## Problem

- **A build's words reach the corpus's vocabulary.** The design's status, verification and upstream sections state what the project decided about its builds, and their prose was read as the contract's words.
- **Case was the only thing holding them out.** Only a shaped term was checked, so a build word written in lower case passed in any declaration.

## Proposal

The conformance report already declares which design sections state no behaviour; the independence check reads the design through that declaration.

- **The design is partitioned once.** `conformance.partition` divides the design into the text its behavioural sections state and the text of each silent section, by the headings `clauses` reads. Every heading stays behavioural text, since the report names each section by it.
- **Build words are refused whatever their shape.** A word a silent section carries and the contract states nowhere else is reported in a fixture declaration or a report entry, naming the section that carries it.
- **A fixture's tree keeps its own words.** A word naming a file the fixture carries is the tree's, so a carried message quoting that tree is not reported.
- **The report's own entries use the contract's words.** The reasons conformance gives for its silent sections are worded without the words only those sections carry.
