## Overview

A dispatch target's preset can declare a `brief`: a section of its own document that a dispatch of it carries into the dispatching bundle, compiled as a composition and placed in a sub-section lifted from the dispatch around the dispatch line, so the dispatching agent reads it before it hands the bundle on.

## Problem

- **The dispatching document restates its target's needs.**
  A target's own inputs reach the dispatching agent only where every dispatching document composes that section again beside each dispatch of it.
- **The restatement lands after the dispatch.**
  A composed section follows its scope's content, so an agent can meet the dispatch before the text that governs it.
- **Nothing ties the two together.**
  A dispatching document can drop or misname the composed section and the dispatch still publishes.

## Proposal

- **A preset declares a brief.**
  `brief: "#contract"` names a section of the declaring document; a dispatch whose presets in force name one carries it.
- **A briefed dispatch is lifted into a sub-section.**
  A `⇉` link, or a set of one, publishes a `⇉ <text>` sub-section holding the brief's lead content, the dispatch line, then the brief's child sections one level below; a pick's sub-section holds the brief around its pick lines, and a set holding a briefed member lifts every member into a sub-section of its own.
- **The brief compiles as a composition.**
  Its links join the dispatching bundle's closure, each copy is complete and its own, its links to its own children land in the copy, and the target's own bundle keeps the section unchanged.
- **An eval measures what a brief changes.**
  A `dispatch-brief` workflow in a briefed and an unbriefed form, around a Contract brief, scored by a check failing a session that dispatches before acting on the brief.
