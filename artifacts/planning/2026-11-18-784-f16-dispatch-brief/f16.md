## Overview

A dispatch target's preset can declare a `brief`: a section of its own document that a dispatch of it carries into the dispatching bundle, compiled as a composition and placed in a sub-section lifted from the dispatch, ahead of the dispatch line, so the dispatching agent reads it before it hands the bundle on.

## Problem

- **The dispatching document restates its target's needs.**
  A target's own inputs reach the dispatching agent only where every dispatching document composes that section again beside each dispatch of it.
- **The restatement lands after the dispatch.**
  A composed section follows its scope's content, so an agent can meet the dispatch before the text that governs it.
- **Nothing ties the two together.**
  A dispatching document can drop or misname the composed section and the dispatch still publishes.

## Proposal

- **A preset declares a brief.**
  `brief: "#given"` names a section of the declaring document; a dispatch whose presets in force name one carries it.
- **A briefed dispatch is lifted into a sub-section.**
  A `⇉` link, or a set of one, publishes a `⇉ <text>` sub-section holding the brief and then the dispatch line, the authored link pointing at it; a pick's sub-section and a set's sub-section hold the brief above the line it governs.
- **The brief compiles as a composition.**
  Its links join the dispatching bundle's closure, and the target's own bundle keeps the section unchanged.
- **An eval measures what a brief changes.**
  A `dispatch-brief` workflow in a briefed and an unbriefed form, scored by a check failing a session that dispatches before acting on the brief.
