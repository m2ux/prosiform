## Problem

## Proposal

- **One entry.**
  The symbol grammar gains `?`: pass the title as `--presets` as usual, read the alternatives `pf` lists, and rerun with `?` replaced by the one that fits.
- **A router fixture.**
  A kind router declaring three alternatives with routing pairs, a mode linking one of its sections with `¬` and `?`, the mode's bundle, the listing that bundle's command draws, and each pick's bundle with no warning.
