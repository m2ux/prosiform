## Proposal

- **Sources.**
  `choice` joins the frontmatter table, with its refusals.
- **Selection.**
  Presets in force states alternatives and the exactly-one rule; The substitution mapping states routing pairs and their warnings.

- **Glossary and validation.**

- **Pins.**
  Each design link moves to the commit stating these rules.
