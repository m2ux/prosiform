## Overview

The documentation states alternatives, the exactly-one rule, its code and routing pairs, and pins the v1 design commit that states them.

## Problem

- **The pages describe presets without alternatives.**
  The frontmatter table names `group` and `subst` alone, and Presets in force states no rule requiring one of a set.

## Proposal

**The scene.** Each page states its rules once and links the v1 design at a pinned commit.

- **Sources.**
  `choice` joins the frontmatter table, with its refusals.
- **Selection.**
  Presets in force states alternatives and the exactly-one rule; The substitution mapping states routing pairs and their warnings.
- **Invocation.**
  Conversion metadata names `choice` and the failure a missing pick raises.
- **Glossary and validation.**
  The glossary's alternative, choice line, pick and routing pair rows, which W02 ([#715](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/pull/715)) added with the README's design pin, link the pages that state each rule; `E_ALTERNATIVE_UNRESOLVED` is listed with its exit status.
- **Pins.**
  Every design link the README's pin does not cover moves to the commit stating these rules.
- **One meaning of alternative.**
  The substitute side of a `subst` pair, `Subst.alternative` in `metadata.py` and its uses in `cli.py`, is named `substitute`, so "alternative" names a preset holding `choice` alone.
