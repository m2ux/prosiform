**Component:** `src/prosiform/grouping.py`

## Overview

A merged group names every member it carries. A member whose whole content is the H1 its overview subsection heading absorbs takes that subsection like any other member, so the document reaches the bundle and the references naming it resolve to the heading that carries its title.

## Problem

- **A member of one heading is emitted nowhere.**
  The member's own H1 is the title the overview subsection heading absorbs, so it is no item of the member's own. Carrying nothing else above the grouping depth the member takes no subsection, and carrying nothing at that depth it takes no merged section, so the group emits the member's name nowhere.

- **The arrangement decides what the bundle contains.**
  The same document bundled without a grouping rule is its own group, headed by its path with the title absorbed into it. Under `--group docs:2` it is absent, so the rule decides whether content is carried rather than only where it sits.

- **The references to it are redirected.**
  Both destinations the member answers to — the document itself and the title its H1 carries — fall through to the group's own heading, so a reader following a link to the member lands on the group and finds the member's content nowhere beneath it.

## Proposal

The overview subsection is what a merged group emits a member's name as. A member contributing nothing is the case where that naming is the whole of its contribution, not the case where it is dispensed with.

- **A member the group emits nothing else from takes its overview subsection.**
  The `Overview` section carries a subsection for each member with selected content above the grouping depth, and for each member the arrangement emits nothing else from. The subsection heading is the member's stem in title case, as it is for every other member, and it absorbs the member's own H1 under the rule that already governs absorption.

- **The subsection answers both destinations.**
  The whole-document destination and the destination of the title it absorbed are the subsection heading's, as they are for every other member, so the fall-through to the group's own heading is reached only by a member no group emits a heading for — which, after this, is no member at all.

- **Grouping decides where content sits, never whether it is carried.**
  Every document the closure selected reaches the bundle under every arrangement. That is the invariant the row establishes, and the corpus is where it is stated.

- **The design's clause states the same thing.**
  The Grouping clause's sentence giving a member contributing nothing the group's own heading describes the behaviour this work ends, and is restated as the subsection every member takes.
