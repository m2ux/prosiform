**Component:** `src/prosiform/bundle.py`

## Overview

An emitted region carries the newline spelling its source wrote, on the line that ends it as on the lines within it. The separation between two regions, the generated entry line and the generated headings stay the newline the bundle generates.

## Problem

- **The terminator of a region's last line is dropped.**
  A region is emitted as its lines with the terminators stripped from the end of it, and the assembly writes the newline back as its own. A region of one line therefore reaches the bundle with the bundle's newline wherever its source wrote another, which is every line of a source terminated with CRLF.

- **One document carries two spellings.**
  The bytes inside a fenced block survive as the source wrote them where the block runs to more than one line, so a run that deliberately changed neither leaves a document carrying CRLF within its blocks and LF at the end of each of them.

- **The run says nothing about it.**
  The declared transformations are a closed list and normalizing line endings is not among them, so the bundle differs from its source in a way the exit status and the diagnostics do not name.

## Proposal

The terminator belongs to the line it terminates, which is content, and the blank line between two emitted parts belongs to the bundle, which generates it.

- **An emitted part ends with the terminator its last line carries.**
  A region emitted from a source ends with the spelling that source wrote, and a part the bundle generates — the entry line, a file heading, a group heading — ends with the newline the bundle generates. A source whose last line carries no terminator is emitted with the bundle's own, since there is no spelling to carry.

- **The separation is written once and is the bundle's.**
  One blank line stands between two maximal emitted parts and is spelled with the newline the bundle generates, as the design's clause admits for generated separators. The geometry the candidate reads back is the geometry the assembly writes, so the line each part opens on is unchanged by what spelling a part ends with.

- **The corpus states it in bytes.**
  A fixture declares a CRLF source whose link is rewritten and whose paragraphs are not, and the expected bundle is compared byte for byte, so the rule is read from the bundle rather than from one build's behaviour.
