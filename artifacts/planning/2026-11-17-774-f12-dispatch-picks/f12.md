## Overview

A dispatch link whose title carries `?` compiles its target once for each alternative and publishes each bundle beside its choice line, so the agent that dispatches hands on the one its work calls for. An author writes one link where each alternative took a link of its own.

## Problem

- **A dispatch refuses the pick token.**
  With `pf` 0.25.0, `[⇉ Stage](stage.md "?")` fails with `E_UNSUPPORTED_CONSTRUCT`, since the run compiles the target and no agent is left to pick.
- **The author lists every alternative by hand.**
  A route that dispatches one stage under each of its alternatives writes a dispatch link for each, and restates beside it the choice the stage already declares.
- **A deferral gives up the up-front compile.**
  Writing `¬` leaves the pick to a later run, and a dispatch is compiled up front, so the two marks do not combine.

## Proposal

The confirmed design of issue #774.

- **A dispatch may carry `?`.**
  `[⇉ Stage](stage.md "?")` compiles the target once for each alternative it declares, each a bundle of its own, as a link naming that alternative does.
- **The link publishes as a listing.**
  The section writing it gains one dispatch line for each alternative, beside its choice line and in declaration order, in a generated sub-section as a dispatch set's members are.
- **The guide states the pick.**
  It tells the reader of a `⇉` link carrying `?` to hand on the one bundle whose choice line fits its work.
- **Other names still select.**
  A name beside `?` selects the target's ordinary preset in every alternative's bundle.
- **A dispatch set is untouched.**
  A `⫽` link carrying `?` stays refused.

## Design Points

Points the issue leaves open, decided by their simplest reading.

- **The sub-section is headed `⇉ Dispatch Pick`.**
  It mirrors `⫽ Dispatch Set`: the mark leads and the words name the structure. It sits one level below the section, after the section's own content and any dispatch-set sub-section, before its child sections. Each distinct `?` dispatch in a section takes its own sub-section, in written order.
- **Each line is a dispatch followed by its choice line.**
  `[⇉ <link text>](<bundle file>) — <choice line>`, the link text and choice line escaped as a set member's text is.
- **The link drops its mark and keeps its title.**
  As a set's links do, the in-place link points at the sub-section without its `⇉`, and its title, `?` among it, is carried as authored.
- **A `?` dispatch is checked as a deferral is.**
  A `?` dispatch naming an alternative beside `?`, or naming a target that declares no alternative, fails with `E_UNSUPPORTED_CONSTRUCT` at the link, with the message the deferral check gives, naming it a dispatch.
- **The sub-section is placed under the set's rules.**
  A level-6 section leaves it no level (`E_HEADING_DEPTH_OVERFLOW`), and an authored heading equal to it collides (`E_HEADING_COLLISION`).
- **The design pin is unchanged.**
  The design change is committed to `engineering`; the README's design pin stays at `005b0ff1d`, since moving it would carry other units' in-flight design changes. The fixtures cite clauses the pinned design already names.
