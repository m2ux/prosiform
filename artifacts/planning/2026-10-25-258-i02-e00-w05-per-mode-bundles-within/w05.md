**Component:** example/work-planner/

## Overview

W04 gave the tree one effective root, so each mode's bundle now carries the artifacts its links name. That also brought the example's own `guidelines.md` into scope: seven of ten mode bundles carry its text. Six carry the 4,323-byte `### Modes` section and its H1; `revise-mode` carries its text whole, 23,797 bytes of it. This row defers every in-root guidelines link, so a mode's bundle carries its procedure and the artifacts it runs without the guidance document riding along.

## Problem

A guidelines link selects the whole document rather than the section the caller reads.

- **Seven bundles carry the guidance text.**
  `advance`, `deliver`, `hoist`, `plan`, `revise`, `standup` and `sync` each include `# Guidelines` and its sections. Six of them carry the H1 and the `### Modes` section, 4,323 bytes each; `revise` carries the document's text whole, 23,797 bytes. The largest bundle, `deliver`, sits at 247,540 bytes — 94% of the 262,144-byte budget — 4,323 of them guidance.

- **A mode reads guidance as a reader's reference, not as procedure.**
  A mode's Procedure says "as the interview rules state" and names the section. The section is guidance the reader consults beside the bundle, not content the bundle must carry.

- **The guidance is already in scope without the deferral.**
  The example's `guidelines.md` sits inside the example root, so the compiler follows a plain link to it and includes the whole file. The deferral marker is what tells a run the destination must exist but its content stays out.

## Proposal

- **Every in-root link to `guidelines.md` carries the deferred marker.**
  `[Interview](guidelines.md#modes)` becomes `[¬ Interview](guidelines.md#modes)`. The destination still resolves and still has to exist; the content does not come through.

- **Out-of-root guideline links stay exactly as authored.**
  `guidelines.md`'s own `../guidelines.md` links and `modes/revise-mode.md`'s `../../guidelines.md` links name the shared skill guidelines, outside the example root. They already stay links and are not marked.

- **No content changes.**
  Only link text changes. Every document's prose is identical to the base's.

## Known finding to record rather than fix

`revise-mode`'s bundle falls from 44,948 bytes to 3,482 once its guideline links are deferred, because all four of its in-root links point at guidelines and its only other links are same-file anchors. The bundle is then its Procedure alone. That is the deferral working as designed, and it is also a design signal about the mode: a mode whose only references are guidance has nothing else to bundle. Record it as a finding; do not add artifact links to the mode in this row.
