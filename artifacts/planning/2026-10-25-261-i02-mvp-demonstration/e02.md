## Overview

This epic holds the work the demonstration's delivery defers. A unit that meets something the demonstration does not need records it here when it would warrant a work item of its own, and fixes it in place when it blocks that unit's own criteria. The epic runs after every epic planned with the initiative, so the clear-up competes with none of them.

## Problem

Work the demonstration meets and does not need has nowhere to go that is neither a widened epic nor an orphan.

- **Widening the epic in hand changes what it was planned to be.**
  A unit that absorbs every defect it notices delivers something other than the work its epic scoped, and the criteria it is measured against stop describing it.

- **A standalone issue with no home is a decision postponed.**
  An orphan waits for a later pass to find it a place, and until then nothing says when it is fixed or what fixing it makes true.

- **Nothing records that the demonstration is carrying debt.**
  An initiative whose epics are all closed reads as complete even when its pass left known work behind, because no row names it.

## Proposal

- **One epic takes the whole of it.**
  Each deferred item becomes a row here, and the initiative stays open while any row is undelivered, so the debt is visible as outstanding work rather than as prose in a planning record.

- **Each row carries the criterion its fix makes true.**
  The statement is what the fix establishes, not the defect it removes, so the row is verified the way any other row is.

- **The clear-up absorbs what it uncovers.**
  Debt a unit of this epic finds in resolving its own row becomes a further row here.

- **It is raised with its first row and grows as delivery proceeds.**
  Its rows are not planned ahead of the work that finds them.
