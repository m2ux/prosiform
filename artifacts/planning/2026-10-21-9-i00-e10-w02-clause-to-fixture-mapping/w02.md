**Component:** `checks/conformance.py` — the conformance report.

## Overview

The mapping from each behavioural clause of the design to the fixtures exercising it, emitted by a report that reads the design and the corpus, lists the clauses no fixture reaches, and states the declared surface the corpus covers.

## Problem

Fixtures accumulate alongside the work that needed them, so a clause nobody implemented has no fixture and no record that it has none.

A fixture already states the design clauses it encodes, and 215 manifests carry 696 such declarations between them. Nothing reads them against the design, so a declaration naming a clause the design does not state is indistinguishable from one that lands, and a clause no manifest names is invisible. The declared surface is in the same position: every error code, warning code and exit status is declared by a fixture somewhere, and nothing says which of them a run of the corpus actually reaches.

## Proposal

- **A clause is an enumerated rule of the design.**
  A bold-led bullet, a step of a numbered sequence, or a row of a table, named by the heading it sits under and the rule under it: `Heading-only structural ancestors — Depth`. A section stating its rule in prose alone is named by its heading.

- **A manifest names a clause exactly, and may add the case it takes.**
  The clause name is the identifier; a comma and free text after it state which case of that clause the fixture encodes. A declaration matching no clause is a reported failure, so the mapping is only as good as the declarations it reads and says when it is not.

- **The mapping is generated from the design and the corpus.**
  The report reads the design from the branch and path the README's Design link names, as the vocabulary check reads it, and every manifest from the corpus. It maintains no list of its own.

- **A clause no fixture names is listed as uncovered.**
  The uncovered list is the report's measurement rather than its verdict: a gap is work for the epic that owns the clause, and the report states it every run.

- **The declared surface is read from the corpus.**
  Each error code, warning code and exit status the contract declares is listed with the fixtures reaching it. One that only a fixture awaiting later work declares is unfixturable, and the work that fixture names is the reason. One no fixture declares at all has no reason to state, and fails the report.
