## Overview

A stage declares the capability level its work needs, the run's entry maps each level to a model, and `pf` publishes the resolved model on every dispatch, so each sub-agent starts on the model its stage needs.

## Problem

- **A sub-agent runs on whatever model starts it.**
  The guide's `⇉` entry names the bundle's path and its inputs, and nothing about the model, so one model runs every stage.
- **No source can name a model.**
  `pf` 0.29.2 refuses a `levels` map as an unknown field.

## Proposal

- **`level` and `levels` are preset fields.**
  `level` is one single-line string; `levels` maps single-line strings to single-line strings. Anything else fails with `E_INVALID_CONVERSION_METADATA`.
- **The entry's presets in force give the run's level map.**
  Read once from the run's entry, layered by its title tags, and carried to every dispatched bundle as the entry's stub names are.
- **A published dispatch carries its model.**
  Every `⇉` link to a bundle file, set line, pick line and lifted brief's dispatch line whose target has a level gains ` on <model>`; a diagram click carries none.
- **Refusals.**
  An unmapped level fails with `E_LEVEL_UNMAPPED` at its dispatch; a level in force for the entry with `E_LEVEL_ON_ENTRY`; two selected presets disagreeing on a level or a mapping with `E_INVALID_USAGE`.
- **The guide.**
  "Where on <model> follows a ⇉ link, start its sub-agent on that model; where nothing follows, start it as your harness does by default."
