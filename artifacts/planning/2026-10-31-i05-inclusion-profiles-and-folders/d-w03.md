**Component:** src/prosiform/cli.py

## Overview

The carve applies only where a reference names its target whole. A reference carrying a fragment selects one section, so explicit selection is the more specific statement and the preset's `exclude` does not apply.

## Problem

Selection and exclusion describe one shape, so they can name the same section from two directions.

- **A fragment is an explicit statement.** `[Rules](rules.md#Tests)` says which part of `rules.md` the author wants. A preset's `exclude` list is written for the general case, and applying it here would overrule a section the author named by hand.
- **A whole-file reference states no part.** `[Rules](rules.md)` names the document and leaves the carve to the governing preset, which is exactly the case `exclude` exists for.
- **Both readings would be defensible, and one must be chosen.** Applying the carve over an explicit fragment and skipping it are both coherent, so the rule is stated once in the clause rather than discovered from behaviour.
- **The two references must differ.** A document where one section links the target whole and another links a section of it exercises both paths, and the difference is the whole of what this row makes observable.

## Proposal

**The scene.** `traverse` resolves each reference; `selection` settles the regions it carries; the governing preset's exclusions narrow that settlement.

**The design.**

- **A fragment-bearing reference consults no `exclude`.** Whether the fragment names a document-level heading or a nested one, the settlement is the section it resolves to and the excluded list is not read. No diagnostic accompanies that.
- **A whole-file reference is carved.** The settlement is every region the target holds less the excluded ones, which W01 states.
- **An operator's target follows its own spelling.** A `⇉` or `⫽` link carrying a fragment settles that section and consults no `exclude`; one naming the target whole is carved by the preset its containing section selected, exactly as a compiled reference is.
- **A reference naming a target that does not exist is unaffected.** Nothing is settled, so nothing is carved, and [I08:E00](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/issues/434)'s rule for an absent target decides the outcome.
- **The rule is stated where the entry is.** The clause and the source page state both halves together, so a reader meets the scope of `exclude` at the entry rather than in a resolution stage.
