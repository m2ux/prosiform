**Component:** fixtures/cli

## Overview

The three fixtures the option carried are withdrawn or rewritten, and no manifest in the corpus names `-f`.

## Problem

Three fixtures reach the option, and two of them test nothing else.

- **`cli/fragment-only-reference-without-from` tests the refusal.** Its whole subject is that a bare `#a-section` is refused when `-f` is absent, which the removal replaces with a different refusal.
- **`extraction/a-fragment-alone-selects-from-the-referrer` tests the capability.** It passes `-f guide.md '#stop'` and expects `## Stop`, which `guide.md#stop` now states.
- **`usage-example/a-bare-fragment-and-its-referring-document` does the same over the real example tree.** Its source is a symbolic link to that tree, so its invocation is the part that changes.
- **E09's redirected-referrer fixture tests a delivered substitution.** `fixtures/mock/a-redirected-referrer-carries-its-entry` asserts that a referrer is redirected, and no referrer survives.

## Proposal

**The scene.** A fixture declares its invocation as `arguments`, and `runner.py` passes them as the process's working directory and argv.

**The design.**

- **The refusal fixture is rewritten.** `cli/fragment-only-reference-without-from` becomes `cli/a-bare-fragment-is-invalid-usage`, declaring exit status `2` and the message naming `document#fragment`.
- **The capability fixture is rewritten to the spelling that survives.** `extraction/a-fragment-alone-selects-from-the-referrer` becomes `extraction/a-section-entry-selects-its-section`, passing `guide.md#stop`, and its expected bundle is unchanged because the same section is selected.
- **The example fixture is rewritten the same way,** so the `usage-example` family still exercises a section entry over the real tree.
- **The substitution fixture is withdrawn.** `fixtures/mock/a-redirected-referrer-carries-its-entry` goes with the capability it tests, and E09's AC13 is marked superseded.
- **No manifest names the option.** A corpus-wide check that no `arguments` list holds `-f` is the statement that the removal is complete.
