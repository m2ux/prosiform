**Component:** invocation.md

## Overview

The documented table names no `-m`, and the source page states a preset as the only channel with its pair resolved from the declaring file.

## Problem

The option is documented in two places and its absence is stated nowhere.

- **The table carries the row.** `invocation.md` states `-m, --mock ORIGINAL=ALTERNATIVE` with its effect, reach and repeatability.
- **The bundling paragraph states its scope.** It states that a mapping entry redirects a reference and that both sides read from the working directory.
- **The source page states the two kinds of `prosiform` key.** With one kind left, that section states presets alone and the pair's path basis beside them.
- **A reader needs the absence stated.** A document with no preset now substitutes nothing, which is a fact an author meets at the page rather than infers.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The option table's rows are clauses `make invocation` compares against the build's help output.

**The design.**

- **The row is removed.** Five option rows become four, and the table names no channel for a substitution.
- **The bundling paragraph states the preset's reach.** It states that a preset covers its compilation's closure, that a pair resolves from the declaring file, and that a compilation selecting no preset substitutes nothing.
- **The source page states one kind of key.** `prosiform` holds presets; the pair's path basis and its `=` form are stated beside the other entries.
- **The examples move to presets.** A worked example passing the option states the same pair in the entry document's `default`.
- **The two-way comparison proves the removal.** An option the build offers and no row names fails, and so does a row naming an option the build lacks.
