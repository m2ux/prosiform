## Overview

The clause that makes `prosiform` hold presets and nothing else, and what a preset governs.

## Problem

The design states a closed key set and a run-wide option surface, and a preset is neither.

- **Only one open key is stated.** The conversion-metadata clause names `root`, `group` and `group-depth` and refuses any other, which [Declared Inclusion](epic-a-declared-inclusion.md) narrows further. A preset has no clause to stand on.
- **A compilation is not a term the design defines.** The design states that each dispatched target is compiled as its own entry under every rule an entry is, so the behaviour exists; what it lacks is a name and a statement that a preset attaches to it.
- **A section name is stated two ways already.** A fragment names a heading in the document that carries it, and a heading carries text; the design says the lookup is "exactly as spelled". Matching a preset by the resolved text rather than by the spelling is a decision, and the clause is where it is recorded.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The conversion-metadata clause states the admitted keys; the substitution clause states what a mapping redirects and how its sides resolve; the dispatch clause states that each target is compiled as its own entry.

**The design.**

- **One clause, stated beside the key set.** Every `prosiform` key is a preset name whose value is a mapping, and a key whose value is anything else is refused. There is no top-level parameter: a file-wide setting cannot vary with what a caller selected, which is what a preset exists to do. The clause states the refused key as the misspelled field it usually is and as the retired top-level parameter it may be.
- **The term is defined where it is used.** A **bundle compilation** is one entry reference and the closure it reaches. The clause states that a preset is the whole setting of the compilation that selects it and covers every document that compilation reaches, so a document reached as a dependency is compiled under the entry's preset rather than its own.
- **The word *profile* keeps its meaning.** `skill-markdown-v4` is the profile, reported by `-v`, and the glossary's one sense of the term is the grammar. The per-compilation setting is a **preset**, and the clause states the distinction beside the key rule so a reader meets the two words where they first differ.
- **The selection rule is stated once.** A preset applies where the section its compilation's entry reference names has a heading text equal to the preset's name, and the clause states why the resolved text is matched rather than the authored fragment: the two live in different documents and a fragment may be percent-escaped.
- **The codes are stated with their reach.** `W_PRESET_UNUSED` is named where the substitution clause states the warnings, and the clause states that a preset reached only through a deferral is not unused, and that a document's own preset is inert where that document was reached as a dependency.
- **This row does not move the pin.** [I08:E00:W00](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/issues/434) moves it once for every clause in this initiative, so this amendment lands beside that one and the pin names the revision carrying all of them.
