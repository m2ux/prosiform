## Overview

The clause that admits named profiles, the rule deciding which key is which, and what a profile governs.

## Problem

The design states a closed key set and a run-wide option surface, and a profile is neither.

- **Only one open key is stated.** The conversion-metadata clause names `root`, `group` and `group-depth` and refuses any other, which [Declared Inclusion](epic-a-declared-inclusion.md) narrows further. A profile has no clause to stand on.
- **A compilation is not a term the design defines.** The design states that each dispatched target is compiled as its own entry under every rule an entry is, so the behaviour exists; what it lacks is a name and a statement that a profile attaches to it.
- **A section name is stated two ways already.** A fragment names a heading in the document that carries it, and a heading carries text; the design says the lookup is "exactly as spelled". Matching a profile by the resolved text rather than by the spelling is a decision, and the clause is where it is recorded.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The conversion-metadata clause states the admitted keys; the substitution clause states `-m` and its reach; the dispatch clause states that each target is compiled as its own entry.

**The design.**

- **One clause, stated beside the key set.** `prosiform` admits, beside its fields, a key naming an argument and a key naming a profile. A key whose value is a mapping is a profile; a key naming an argument holds that argument's value; any other key is refused, which is the misspelled-field refusal the clause already carries.
- **The term is defined where it is used.** A **bundle compilation** is one entry reference and the closure it reaches, and the clause states that a profile applies to one. The substitution clause gains a sentence stating that a profile's entries join the mapping for its compilation alone.
- **The selection rule is stated once.** A profile applies where the section its compilation's entry reference names has a heading text equal to the profile's name, and the clause states why the resolved text is matched rather than the authored fragment: the two live in different documents and a fragment may be percent-escaped.
- **The two codes are stated with their reach.** `W_PROFILE_UNUSED` and the existing `W_MOCK_UNUSED` are named where the substitution clause states the substitution warnings, and the clause states that a profile reached only through a deferral is not unused.
- **The pin moves after the clause merges**, as every epic in this record does.
