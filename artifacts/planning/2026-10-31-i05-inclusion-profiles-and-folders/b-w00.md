## Overview

The clause that makes `prosiform` hold presets and nothing else: what selects one, what `default` does, and what a match governs.

## Problem

The design states a closed key set and a run-wide option surface, and a preset is neither.

- **Only one open key is stated.** The conversion-metadata clause names `root`, `group` and `group-depth` and refuses any other, which [Declared Inclusion](epic-a-declared-inclusion.md) narrows further. A preset has no clause to stand on.
- **A reference's governing settings have no name.** The design states that a link resolves from its own source's directory and that its target's declaration decides inclusion; what it does not state is that the source's own section decides *how* that reference is treated.
- **A keyword rule is a decision, and the clause is where it is recorded.** Matching a preset name against a heading's text as words rather than as an equality is a choice about authored prose meeting declarative settings, and a reader meeting it at the implementation would not know it was deliberate.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The conversion-metadata clause states the admitted keys; the substitution clause states what a mapping redirects and how its sides resolve; the dispatch clause states that each target is compiled as its own entry.

**The design.**

- **One clause, stated beside the key set.** Every `prosiform` key is a preset name whose value is a mapping, and a key whose value is anything else is refused. There is no top-level parameter: a file-wide setting cannot vary with the section that asked for it, which is what a preset exists to do. The clause states the refused key as the misspelled field it usually is and as the retired top-level parameter it may be.
- **Selection is by keyword, against the writing section's title.** The presets a document declares are read where that document's content is compiled, and a reference is governed by those whose names appear as words in the title of the section containing it. The clause states that matching is case-insensitive and whole-word, so `Checks` matches `Checks and Draft` and not `Checklist`, and that several matches union.
- **`default` is always in force.** The clause states that a document's `default` applies wherever that document is compiled or writes a reference, that a keyword match layers above it, and that its name is reserved so no heading selects it literally.
- **A command-line entry reads the file it names.** An entry is written in no document, so the clause states that the file it names supplies the presets: a whole-document entry matching against that document's own title heading, a section entry against the section it names.
- **What a match governs is stated once.** The governing preset decides the division the target joins, what a whole-file reference withholds of it, and the path a reference resolves to. The clause states that reach and states that it stops at the reference, so a document reached as a dependency applies its own `default` rather than inheriting the writing document's presets.
- **The word *profile* keeps its meaning.** `skill-markdown-v4` is the profile, reported by `-v`, and the glossary's one sense of the term is the grammar. The per-reference setting is a **preset**, and the clause states the distinction beside the key rule so a reader meets the two words where they first differ.
- **The codes are stated with their reach.** `W_PRESET_UNUSED` is named where the substitution clause states the warnings, and the clause states that a preset reached only through a deferral is not unused, and that a document's `default` being empty where no keyword matched leaves the reference as authored.
- **This row does not move the pin.** [I08:E00:W00](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/issues/434) moves it once for every clause in this initiative, so this amendment lands beside that one and the pin names the revision carrying all of them.
