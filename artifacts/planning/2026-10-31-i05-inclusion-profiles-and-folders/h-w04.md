## Overview

E09's option criterion is marked superseded and its documented row withdrawn, so that epic records a channel the tool no longer has as history rather than as a claim.

## Problem

A delivered epic asserts what this removal takes away.

- **AC15 is ticked.** It states that the option, its effect and its accepted form appear in `invocation.md` and agree with the help output, delivered in PR #428.
- **AC1, AC2, AC3 and AC18 describe fixtures that pass the option.** Their behaviour survives — the mapping still redirects — but their invocations do not.
- **The option row goes with the option.** `make invocation` holds the table and the help output together, so the row cannot stay.
- **E09 already carries superseded criteria.** AC6, AC11, AC13 and AC20 are marked for `root`'s retirement and the referrer's removal, and this is the same pattern.

## Proposal

**The scene.** E09's epic body holds a *Supersession* section stating which criteria a later epic voided, and each such criterion carries a note linking to it.

**The design.**

- **AC15 joins the superseded set.** It keeps its tick, because the row that delivered it landed, and gains a note stating why the row is gone.
- **The paragraph names this epic.** It states that `-m` was retired and that a preset is now the only channel.
- **The mapping criteria are not superseded.** AC1 onward state what a substitution does, and a preset does the same thing. Their fixtures are rewritten by this epic's corpus row, not withdrawn.
- **The row is not renumbered.** AC15 keeps its number, as the four superseded criteria before it did.
