## Overview

E07's `mock`-named criteria are marked superseded and its documented row withdrawn, so that epic records a channel and a spelling the tool no longer has as history rather than as a claim.

## Problem

A delivered epic asserts what this removal takes away.

- **AC15 is ticked.** It states that the option, its effect and its accepted form appear in `invocation.md` and agree with the help output, delivered in PR #428.
- **AC2, AC6, AC11, AC20–AC22 and AC25–AC31 rest on the option's reach, `root` or the folder form.** Each is superseded and names the epic that supersedes it.
- **AC1, AC3 and AC18 describe fixtures that pass the option.** Their behaviour survives — the mapping still redirects — but their invocations do not, and their names carry the word `mock`.
- **The option row goes with the option.** `make invocation` holds the table and the help output together, so the row cannot stay.
- **The entry's name goes with the option.** The preset entry is `path-sub`, so a criterion naming `mock` names a spelling the contract does not carry, and the two warning codes renamed in [I08:E06:W00](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/issues/461) name it too.
- **E07 already carries superseded criteria.** AC6, AC11, AC13 and AC20 are marked for `root`'s retirement and the referrer's removal, and this is the same pattern.

## Proposal

**The scene.** E07's epic body holds a *Supersession* section stating which criteria a later epic voided, and each such criterion carries a note linking to it.

**The design.**

- **AC15 joins the superseded set.** It keeps its tick, because the row that delivered it landed, and gains a note stating why the row is gone.
- **The criteria naming `mock` join it too.** The behaviour each states survives under `path-sub`, so the criteria are not withdrawn for being wrong; they are marked so a reader is not sent looking for an entry or a code that no longer exists.
- **The paragraph names this epic.** It states that `-m` was retired, that a preset is now the only channel, that the entry is `path-sub`, and that the two warnings are `W_PATH_SUB_USED` and `W_PATH_SUB_UNUSED`.
- **The row is not renumbered.** AC15 keeps its number, as the four superseded criteria before it did.
- **The `path-sub` entry is landed elsewhere.** [I08:E01:W03](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/issues/436) states the entry's shape and its refusals, so this record points at it rather than restating it.
