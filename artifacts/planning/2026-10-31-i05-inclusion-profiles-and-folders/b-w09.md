**Component:** invocation.md

## Overview

The invocation and the source page state `prosiform` as presets: what a preset holds, how one is selected, and what is refused.

## Problem

The documentation states a flat frontmatter block.

- **`invocation.md` shows root, group and depth.** Its *Conversion metadata* section presents the three fields and states that all three are the file's.
- **`docs/sources.md` gives the key table.** Its *Frontmatter* section lists `root`, `group` and `group-depth` with what each holds and states that a key outside them fails.
- **Nothing states what a selection does to a declaration.** A reader has no word for the compilation a preset governs, and no rule for the fallback.
- **The pair encodings are new.** Two list fields hold single-entry mappings and one holds `=` strings, and each needs its shape stated once.

## Proposal

**The scene.** `invocation.md` holds a *Conversion metadata* section and an option table; `docs/sources.md` holds a *Frontmatter* section with a key table.

**The design.**

- **The source page states presets.** Every `prosiform` key is a preset name holding a mapping, the entries are `group-depth`, `exclude` and `mock`, and every other key is refused. The two pair encodings and their refusals are stated beside them.
- **The invocation shows one.** `invocation.md` carries an example preset beside the existing example, showing a grouping, a mock and an exclusion, and states the selection rule and the fallback.
- **The term is defined where it is used.** *bundle compilation* joins the source page's account of what an entry reference reaches, so a reader meets the word before the rule that turns on it.
- **No option row is added.** A preset supplies values to options that already exist, so `make invocation`'s two-way comparison is unchanged: no option is added, and the metadata section changes rather than the table.
- **Every statement of a root is already gone.** [I05:E11](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/issues/434) rewrote those pages, so this task amends what that one left.
