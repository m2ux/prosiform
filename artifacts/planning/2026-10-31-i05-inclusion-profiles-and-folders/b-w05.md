**Component:** src/prosiform/cli.py

## Overview

Several presets may govern one reference, so their settings are combined, and a setting two of them state differently is refused rather than silently resolved.

## Problem

W04's lookup returns a set of names, and nothing states how their entries meet.

- **Two presets can state one thing.** `default` and a matched preset may each hold a `path-sub` entry, a `group-depth` entry or an `exclude`, and the reference is governed by both.
- **Union is the only reading that keeps both statements.** A `default` placing a document in one division and a matched preset placing it in another are two placements an author wrote, and the multi-division rule already admits both.
- **A disagreement is not a union.** Two presets mapping one `path-sub` key to two alternatives state one reference two ways, and any choice between them is silent about which the author meant.
- **The scope tests the superseded rule.** "A preset is the entire argument set for its compilation" is the rule this epic drops, and the pair of fixtures here is what makes its absence visible.

## Proposal

**The scene.** `traverse` resolves a reference and asks the presets governing it for their settings; `compiled` assembles a bundle and `group_depths` collects placements per division.

**The design.**

- **Settings union across the governing presets.** Each entry kind combines additively: two `group-depth` pairs are two placements, two `exclude` lists are both applied, and two `path-sub` maps are both consulted.
- **A `path-sub` key stated twice is refused.** Two governing presets mapping one normalised identity to two alternatives fail with `E_INVALID_USAGE`, exit status `2`, naming both presets and the key. One alternative for one key is the whole point of the entry, so a second is a contradiction rather than a preference.
- **One division at two depths is refused.** Two governing presets placing one document in one division at two depths fail with `E_INVALID_USAGE`, naming both presets and the division.
- **Refusals within one preset are unchanged.** Two entries normalising to one identity, and a key and its value normalising to one identity, are still refused where the preset is read, as [I08:E01:W03](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/issues/436) states them.
- **Nothing else narrows.** `exclude` lists from two presets are two withholdings of one target, which is a union by construction because both name sections to remove.
- **The choice is per reference.** The union is computed where the reference is resolved, so the same two presets may govern one reference strictly and another not at all.
