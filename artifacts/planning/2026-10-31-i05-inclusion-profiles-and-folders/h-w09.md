**Component:** checks/mock_scenarios.py

## Overview

The example mock scenarios, `checks/mock_scenarios.py` and the `make mock-scenarios` target are removed with `-m`.

## Problem

- **Each scenario passes `-m`.** The plan-mode artifact stub and the conventions stub are named on the command line, so the scenarios fail once the option is gone.
- **Each scenario bundles a copy of the example tree.** The tree is not maintained and [I08:E08](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/issues/465) removes it, so converting the scenarios to presets would maintain what is about to be deleted.

## Proposal

- **The scenarios go with the option.** `checks/mock_scenarios.py`, its tests, the `mock-scenarios` target and its entry in `make check` are removed in the unit that retires `-m`, so `make check` passes at every merge.
- **Substitution keeps its corpus.** The `fixtures/mock/` fixtures rewritten to presets by W03 carry `subst`'s behaviour; nothing the scenarios proved is left unproven.
- **Retired, not refused.** No refusal or replacement check is added.
