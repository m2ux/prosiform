**Component:** docs/sources.md

## Overview

Six pages state a root as a property of a source. Each is rewritten to state inclusion by declaration, and the derived boundary where a boundary is still what the page is about.

## Problem

The documentation teaches the declaration this epic retires.

- **`docs/sources.md` gives it a section.** *The root* states that every source has an effective root, that an explicit root resolves from the declaring file's directory, that an absent root uses the working directory, and it carries a diagram and a frontmatter table row for `root`. Its *Frontmatter* section states the admitted key set.
- **`docs/references.md` classifies by it.** Its *Classification* table and its Figure 1 both turn on "resolves under its author's root", and its operators section refuses a target "outside the root".
- **Three more pages name it.** `docs/architecture.md` describes a source's root as what decides its links, `docs/hierarchy.md` states that a root names no division, and `docs/glossary.md` defines *profile* and *root* among its terms. `invocation.md` states the metadata block and the bundling rule.
- **A picture carries it.** `docs/images/source.svg` and four other drawings show `root: ".."` in a frontmatter panel, and `make examples` re-renders them from `scripts/examples.json`.

## Proposal

**The scene.** `docs/sources.md` opens its account of a file with a frontmatter panel declaring `root: ".."`, then resolves a link against it.

**The design.**

- **The declaration is the subject of the page.** *The root* becomes *The declaration*: a document declares `prosiform` to be included, an empty block declares, a document with no block is carried as text, and a `root` fails the run. The frontmatter table loses its `root` row and the *Frontmatter* section states the refused key.
- **Classification states the declaration.** `docs/references.md`'s table says a dependency's target declares, and its Figure 1 asks whether the target declares instead of whether the path sits under a root. The operators section refuses a target which declares nothing or does not exist.
- **The boundary is stated once, where it is used.** The derived boundary belongs beside the entry reference and the diagnostic rather than in the source page's account of inclusion, so `docs/sources.md` states it in one paragraph and the pages that name it link there.
- **The glossary's entries change.** *root* is replaced by *declaration* and *derived boundary*; *profile* gains the two kinds of `prosiform` key its epic states. `make vocabulary` holds the glossary to the design's marked terms.
- **The drawings are re-rendered, not edited.** `scripts/examples.json` carries each case, and `make examples` redraws the panels from the files the case reads, so a panel showing `root` is fixed by fixing the case rather than the SVG.
