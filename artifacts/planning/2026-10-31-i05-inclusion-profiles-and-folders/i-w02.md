**Component:** evals/diagram-following/workflows

## Overview

Every document in the diagram-following eval's workflow tree declares `prosiform`, and every form still bundles.

## Problem

The eval's tree barely declares, and `make harness` requires it to bundle.

- **118 workflow documents, one declaring.** Only `bootstrap-audit/skill/SKILL.md` carries a `prosiform` block; the other seven forms reach their states through Mermaid `click` lines and ordinary links.
- **Under inclusion by declaration every one of those links becomes text.** The forms' bundles lose their states and the harness fails.
- **The tree is maintained with the eval.** A declaration added by hand to each document is one more thing to keep in step with the forms.
- **The forms differ only in their transitions.** So the rewrite is uniform: every document gains a block stating inclusion and no setting.

## Proposal

**The scene.** `make harness` bundles each form with the `pf` under test and compares by structure, so a form whose states stopped being carried fails there.

**The design.**

- **Every workflow document declares.** Each gains a `prosiform` block stating inclusion, and none carries a group, since a form's bundle is one document per entry and no form declares a merged group.
- **A document with no local link declares too,** because a form is bundled as a set rather than one file at a time.
- **The expected structure is what proves it.** The harness compares each form's bundle by the states it carries, so a form that lost its transitions fails.
- **The forms are otherwise untouched.** No heading, link or diagram line changes, so a form's behaviour is the one recorded in its baseline.
