# I05:E04:W05 Step References Linked to Their Sub-sections — October 2026

> Task delivery · Created 2026-10-28

## Executive Summary

This record holds the delivery of I05:E04 W05. Every prose reference the example corpus makes to a single step of a procedure becomes a link to that step's sub-section. This includes references by number, such as `step 4`, and by role, such as `the report step`. Each link names the step's heading and links its anchor. The usage-example bundles that carry the linked prose are regenerated.

The step sub-sections W02–W04 introduced give every step an anchor, but the corpus still names steps in prose that links nothing. A link to the sub-section is caught by `pf`'s unresolved-link check when a later insertion renumbers the step. A number written in prose would go stale silently.
