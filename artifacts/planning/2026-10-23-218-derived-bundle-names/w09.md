**Component:** src/prosiform/cli.py

## Overview

Single-entry compilation writes an entry-derived bundle filename automatically.

## Problem

Output filenames require manual reconstruction of entry identity.

## Proposal

Derive the output basename from the normalized path relative to the effective entry root, append the selected fragment when present and publish in the working directory. Retain exact -o file/stdout overrides and require an explicit destination for multiple distinct selections. Route derived destinations through existing publication safeguards and update stdout-dependent consumers in this unit.
