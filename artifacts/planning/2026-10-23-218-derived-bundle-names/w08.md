**Component:** invocation.md

## Overview

Authors can predict bundle names and avoid ambiguous flattened paths.

## Problem

Authoring guidance does not describe source-derived bundle names.

## Proposal

Document whole-file and fragment naming and combined-entry destinations. Recommend hyphens instead of underscores in file and directory names without adding encoding or filename rejection. Audit active examples and record the dependency for the planned authoring skill. Re-run representative corpus and installed-wheel checks against the final contract.
