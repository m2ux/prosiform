**Component:** main:src/prosiform/candidate.py

## Overview

Candidate validation refuses a candidate in which one emitted heading answers the targets of two members of one group, so the design's invariant that one destination answers one member holds by the build rather than by construction alone.

## Problem

- **Target integrity reads one target at a time.**
  It asks whether a destination's heading answers the target assigned to it, so a heading answering two members' targets passes for both.
- **A construction regression stays green.**
  A merged heading recorded as answering every contributor's fragment is what commit 02ec51b1 repaired, and no check would have caught it.

## Proposal

**The scene.** Each emitted heading records the targets it answers. A merged group's merged heading answers a fragment only where one document contributes it, a variant heading answers its own document, and a member subsection answers that member.

- **One member per heading.**
  Within a merged group, a heading whose answered targets name two documents fails validation under the design's "One destination answers one member" invariant, naming the heading, the group and both documents. The check sits beside target integrity and reads the provenance each heading records, not slugs.
- **The failure is the candidate's.**
  It raises `Invalid` as every candidate-validation failure other than a boundary does, naming the invariant, so no new error code is declared and nothing is published.
