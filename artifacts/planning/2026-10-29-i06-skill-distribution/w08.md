**Component:** fixtures (a new local fixture family)

## Overview

The one clause the usage-example fixtures proved that no other fixture proves, carried by a fixture whose own small source tree states it, so main keeps the coverage without a copy of the skill.

## Problem

`Input and output contract — Entry references` is declared by one fixture alone, and that fixture is a usage-example one. Removing the family would leave the clause uncovered and reading as covered on nothing.

## Proposal

- **One fixture, one small tree.**
  The fixture's source holds three short documents, one naming the other two as entries, so the clause is stated without any skill document.
- **The clause is declared as the others declare it.**
  The manifest names the clause and the case it takes, as every other manifest does.
- **The expected bundle is the build's.**
  The manifest records what a configured build produces for that tree, so the fixture holds the contract rather than a hand-written expectation.
