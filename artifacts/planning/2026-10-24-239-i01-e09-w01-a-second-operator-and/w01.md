**Component:** src/prosiform/closure.py

## Overview

A link whose material belongs to a sub-agent is stated as such in the source, and the bundle opens with the rule that says what the mark means.

## Problem

`closure.MARK` holds `¬` alone, so a link a sub-agent compiles is indistinguishable from one the reading agent compiles for itself. `entry_line` emits the entry sentence and nothing else, and the `prosiform` mapping admits no key that could carry the instruction.

## Proposal

Reserve `»` as a dispatch operator that withholds its target exactly as `¬` does and reaches the bundle carrying its mark at the target's URI-escaped working-directory-relative destination with its fragment retained. Admit `prosiform.dispatch` as a nonblank single-line string and emit it after the entry sentence, taking the value from the entry document and failing on two disagreeing declarations with `E_INVALID_CONVERSION_METADATA` naming both locations. Refuse a link whose text opens with both operators, naming the link. State the operator in `docs/glossary.md` beside the deferral, name the key and its emission in `invocation.md`, and state both in the deferral clauses of the design.
