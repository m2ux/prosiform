**Component:** main:src/prosiform/composition.py

## Overview

An immediate composition directive places the section it names beneath the section that writes it: one destination section per title within that scope, and beneath it one contribution per directive, headed by the name of the target's immediate parent and carrying the target's body and subsections with their blocks and relative depth kept. The directive's link points at its contribution, and candidate validation holds the composed output to the provenance that built it. The recognition predicate also stops reading a scalar that shows nothing as a directive.

## Problem

- **A recognised directive places nothing.**
  A directive inside a section bundles as the ordinary reference it is, so its target stands in its own file group and nothing sits beneath the section naming it.
- **An invisible label requests placement.**
  The predicate accepts any one scalar above U+007F, so a label of a no-break space or a zero-width space composes a section that no reader of the source can see was requested.

## Proposal

**The scene.** W01 recognises a directive from its plain label, and the bundle builds each group from its selected regions before the dispatch pass inserts the sub-sections each section collects.

- **A composition pass.**
  Between assembly and the dispatch pass, each group is walked in emission order; a heading part opens a section, and the immediate directives each selected region writes are collected for the section holding them. A directive composes where it names a section of a document the bundle compiles; a deferred directive, and one naming a whole document, are left to the rows that settle them.
- **Destination sections.**
  When a section closes, its directives are emitted after its own content: one destination section one level below the scope for each target title, in the order its first directive is written, carrying that title.
- **Contributions.**
  Beneath a destination section, each directive contributes a heading naming the target's immediate parent, one level below, then the target's body and subsections as the regions its source wrote, shifted so the target heading's depth maps to the contribution heading's. A target with no heading above it names no parent and is refused with `E_UNSUPPORTED_CONSTRUCT`; a composed heading past H6 is refused with `E_HEADING_DEPTH_OVERFLOW`.
- **The directive's link.**
  Each composing link is rewritten to its contribution heading's destination, located by the line and position the link is written at, so no later pass needs the predicate.
- **Provenance.**
  Destination and contribution headings are generated headings of their own kinds, and composed regions and their headings are marked composed: they answer no target, are held to their shift and to preservation, and stand outside the group's own coverage, source order and outline placement. The dispatch pass reads composed parts as content of the scope, so a scope's dispatch sub-sections follow its destination sections.
- **Visible labels only.**
  The design's Recognition clause and the predicate exclude whitespace, control and format scalars.
