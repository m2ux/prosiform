# Maintained Documentation Audit

## Method and Scope

Baseline: W09 merge `f04086096c666cc29241a7fda3dcbdfd9c354743`. The final task revision and observing results are recorded in [validation.md](validation.md). This audit reads the maintained primary pages in full, then traces their link-tag claims to implementation and observing tests. It separately reviews every feature obligation against its owning documentation. It is not an obsolete-word search presented as a completeness review.

`inventory.txt` is the exhaustive tracked Markdown/plain-text path inventory of the audited task tree. Paths below are relative to that tree unless explicitly marked engineering. Classification covers every path in that inventory: primary maintained guidance below; `evals/diagram-following/workflows/**` executable workflow material; `fixtures/**` source and expected-output specimens except its primary README; and the two historical eval reports. No task-tree planning directory is present. SVG examples are inventoried separately through `scripts/examples.json`.

## Exclusions and Unverified Claims

- Fixture source/expected Markdown is a specimen, not user instruction: deliberately broken examples must not be repaired into valid guidance. Every fixture is executed and compared by the complete corpus; source declarations and expected bundles remain unchanged.
- Every eval workflow source is executable scenario material, not general CLI guidance. Deterministic tests compile every form, follow selected deferrals and dispatched variants and compare scorer expectations. General prose in each state is scenario input, not a compiler capability claim.
- `evals/diagram-following/report.md`, `mvp-proof.md`, their JSON records and runbook shakedown rows are historical paid measurements. Their figures are checked by existing report tests, not regenerated or presented as evidence for the current link-tag feature. No paid evaluation is authorized or run.
- The output-budget historical measurement is not remeasured against today's compiler; its corrected reproduction commands are grammar checked, not executed against the historical skill. The explicit historical notice prevents those figures being asserted as current.
- Dated engineering plans, decisions, snapshots, generated diagrams/review bundles and old validation logs remain historical. Their current maintained navigation is reviewed; thousands of historical records are not rewritten or certified current.
- Mermaid browser rendering (`make render`) is outside `make check` and this feature's tooltip-selection boundary. No browser rendering result is claimed; the regenerated architecture examples are the repository's SVG renderer, not Mermaid-browser verification.
- Network links can be reported unreachable under the existing check's policy. Exact final counts/warnings belong in validation rather than an unconditional claim that every third-party page was fetched successfully.
- The mutable historical v1 design and the exact immutable design used by the build are different revisions. The maintained entry points now identify that boundary explicitly; this task does not silently replace or rewrite historical design evidence.
- Integration checks requiring absent `config/branches` remain unevaluated. Task documentation/feature observations do not establish readiness of default-branch or engineering integration reviews.

No material link-tag documentation obligation is intentionally omitted from the maintained guidance reviewed above. Claims beyond this feature (release execution, live agent behavior, historical size/performance, third-party uptime and external rendering) retain the explicit limitations above.
