# A Member Heading That Names One Document — October 2026

> Delivery · Created 2026-10-22

## Executive Summary

The subsection naming a member of a merged group is titled from the member's filename stem alone, so two documents of one stem in different directories take one heading text and are told apart only by the ordinal a renderer appends. The arrangement already answers the same question for a document contributing to a pooled section, where a variant heading carries the path segments that distinguish it; one fixture consequently states a member's name as `Alpha Notes` in its variant heading and as a bare `Notes` in the subsection naming it. This unit gives the member heading the rule the variant headings follow.

Two naming conventions in one bundle make a member's identity depend on where the reader meets it. Settling on the distinguishing path run gives the group one answer to what a member is called, and a stem that already distinguishes its member keeps the heading it has.
