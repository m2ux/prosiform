**Component:** [src/prosiform/grouping.py](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/main/src/prosiform/grouping.py)

## Overview

The heading naming a member of a merged group, carrying as much of the member's path as tells it apart from the others.

## Problem

The subsection naming a member is titled from the member's filename stem alone. Two documents of one stem in different directories therefore take one heading text: `notes/alpha/doc.md` and `notes/beta/doc.md` under `--group notes:1` emit `## Doc` twice, told apart only by the ordinal a renderer appends.

The group's variant headings already answer this for a document contributing to a shared merged section, carrying the path segments that distinguish it. A fixture distinguishing two documents of one name carries `Alpha Notes` and `Beta Notes` as its variant headings and a bare `Notes` as the subsection naming one of them, so one bundle states the name both ways.

## Proposal

The heading naming a member carries the shortest run of that member's root-relative path which distinguishes it from the group's other members, measured after the leading segments they all share, which is the rule the variant headings follow. Where a stem distinguishes a member on its own, the heading is that stem and nothing changes.
