**Component:** [src/prosiform/grouping.py](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/main/src/prosiform/grouping.py)

## Overview

The contributors to a pooled section named by headings of their own, whatever each of them writes beneath that section's heading.

## Problem

A pooled section splits into variant headings only where more than one contributing document writes body text above its first subheading. One document writing such body text beside another writing only subheadings leaves the section unsplit, and the group then names neither contributor.

- **No heading names either member.**
  `rules/agents.md` carrying `## Style` and a paragraph, beside `rules/common.md` carrying `## Style` and `### Draft`, under `--group rules:2` emits one `## Style` holding the paragraph with `### Draft` beneath it. Nothing in the bundle names either document, and a reader takes the subheading for the first document's content.

- **Both members reach one destination.**
  The entry line emits `[Agents](#style)` beside `[Common](#draft)`. The first names a heading both documents contribute; the second names a heading the second document authored rather than one naming that document.

## Proposal

A pooled section carries a variant heading for each document contributing to it, so the split condition is contribution rather than what the contribution is made of. Each document's content hangs beneath the heading naming it, and a whole-document reference to a member resolves there.

Where one document alone contributes a heading text, the section is that document's and no variant is emitted, which is the arrangement as it stands.
