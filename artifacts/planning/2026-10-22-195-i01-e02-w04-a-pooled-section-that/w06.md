**Component:** [src/prosiform/grouping.py](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/main/src/prosiform/grouping.py)

## Overview

One measurement of the run a member's name is shortened to, read by the subsection naming the member and by the variant heading naming it inside a pooled section.

## Problem

A merged group writes a member's name in two places, and each is measured against a different set of documents. The subsection naming a member is measured against every member the group merges; the variant heading naming it inside a pooled section is measured against the documents contributing that section alone.

A subset needs a shorter run than the whole group does, so the two names differ. Three members `g/a/notes.md`, `g/b/notes.md` and `g/c/other.md` merged under `--group g:2`, with `## Findings` contributed by `g/b/notes.md` and `g/c/other.md`, emit `### B Notes` in the overview and `### Notes` as that member's variant heading, and nothing in the bundle says the two carry one document.

The trailing word a group's own heading states is dropped from the subsection heading and kept by the variant heading, so `modes/plan-mode.md` is `Plan` in one place and `Plan Mode` in the other.

## Proposal

The run a member's name is shortened to is measured once, against the group's members, and both headings are read from it. A trailing word the group's own heading states is dropped wherever the name is written, because both headings sit beneath that heading.
