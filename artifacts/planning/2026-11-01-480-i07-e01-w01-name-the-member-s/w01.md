**Component:** src/prosiform/cli.py

## Overview

Reports a refusal raised while a merged group assembles with the chain that reached the member the fault was found in, so the route a diagnostic names is one that reached its source.

## Problem

`assembled` wraps a whole merged group in `assembling(paths[0], selected[paths[0]])`, so `E_UNSUPPORTED_CONSTRUCT` for a dispatch in an absorbed member title and `E_HEADING_DEPTH_OVERFLOW` both take their chain from the group's first member. The fixture `dispatch/a-dispatch-in-an-absorbed-member-title-is-refused` reports `chain = ["guide.md"]` for a fault in `checklist-⇉-publish.md`, a route that never reached that member, so the fixture declares no chain.

## Proposal

A reader follows a refusal's chain back to the document that holds the fault. Whichever member of a merged group raised it, the chain is the one that reached that member.

- **Assembly reads every document's chain.**
  `assembling` takes the chain map `reaching(selected)` returns and looks up the chain of the document the fault names (`Overflow.path`, `Unplaced.path`). One context wraps the whole assembly, file groups and merged groups alike.
- **The fixture declares the member's chain.**
  The absorbed-member fixture declares `chain = ["guide.md", "checklist-⇉-publish.md"]`.
- **A unit test covers a later member.**
  A heading overflowing in the second member of a merged group reports that member's chain.
