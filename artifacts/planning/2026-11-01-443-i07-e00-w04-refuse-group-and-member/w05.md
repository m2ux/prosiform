**Component:** src/prosiform/heading.py

## Overview

Holds every heading a bundle generates to one separator-keeping title case, resolves a titled parent's whole-document reference to its own title heading at every grouping depth, and emits the `Overview` section only where a member carries content beneath its subsection.

## Problem

`grouping.titled` split a name on its separators and joined the words with spaces, while `bundle.title_cased` kept every separator, so one bundle carried `My Rules` beside `Guides/Field-Notes`. A titled parent's whole-document reference resolved to its first H2, which at grouping depth 1 sits inside the parent's own title section and at depth 2 is a merged section other members share. The `Overview` section was emitted whenever the group had merged sections, even where every subsection it held was a heading alone.

The defects are findings 28, 38 and 40 of the [merged-epic review](../2026-10-31-merged-epic-review/findings.md); decision D5 of the [initiative record](../2026-10-31-i07-review-remediation/README.md) keeps separators.

## Proposal

**The scene.** A merged group generates its heading, a subsection per member and a variant heading per pooled contribution. The parent, the first entry, opens the group on its title where that title sits above the grouping depth.

**The design.**

- **One title case.** Group, member and variant names are written by `heading.title_cased`. A run of path segments keeps the slashes between them, so `guides/alpha/notes.md` is `Alpha/Notes`. The trailing word a group states is removed with the separator joining it, so `modes/plan-mode.md` stays `Plan`.
- **The parent reaches its title.** A whole-document reference to a titled parent resolves to whichever heading answers its title: the H1 opening the group, or at depth 1 the merged section or variant carrying it.
- **The overview wraps content only.** `Overview` is emitted where the group has merged sections and some named member carries content beneath its subsection. Heading-only subsections otherwise sit directly beneath the group heading at H2.
