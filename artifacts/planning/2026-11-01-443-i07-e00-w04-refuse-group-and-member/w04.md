**Component:** src/prosiform/heading.py

## Overview

Refuses a group name whose title case names nothing, and names a member whose name carries no word by its path text, so no group or member publishes an empty heading or an empty destination.

## Problem

Two distinct group names such as `-` and `_` title-cased to the same empty heading and published as indistinguishable H1s. A member at `rules/-.md` was named by an empty subsection and variant heading, and a whole-document reference to it was rewritten to the empty fragment `#`, with no diagnostic.

The defects are findings 37 and 39 of the [merged-epic review](../2026-10-31-merged-epic-review/findings.md).

## Proposal

**The scene.** A group heading is the declared name in title case, and a member's subsection and variant headings are the run of its path that tells it apart from the other members. Both are generated, so a name with nothing for title case to act on produces a heading with nothing in it.

**The design.**

- **One definition of a word.** `src/prosiform/heading.py` holds the title-case convention: a word is a run of anything other than a hyphen, an underscore, a slash or whitespace. Metadata validation and heading generation both read it.
- **A wordless group name is refused.** A `prosiform.group` value carrying no word fails with `E_INVALID_CONVERSION_METADATA`, naming the field and the remedy, before any bundle is written.
- **A wordless member name takes its path text.** Where the distinguishing run carries no word, the member is named by its path in title case, suffix kept. The Markdown suffix always carries a word, so no member is refused and no member heading is empty.
