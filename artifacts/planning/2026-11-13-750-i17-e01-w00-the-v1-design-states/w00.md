## Overview

The v1 design states how `pf` is invoked on a link: an entry is a published Markdown link, a Mermaid `click` line or class diagram `link` line, or a plain path, and `pf` decodes its destination, section, operator and presets. A `?` deferral takes its pick through `--pick`, which is invalid usage beside any other entry. The design is the contract the build, corpus and documentation rows of the epic answer to, so it lands first.

## Problem

- **The design has the agent convert a link into a command.**
  The CLI contract, the guide text and the Presets section have the agent split a link into `<document>#<section>` and a `--presets` value, and refuse a complete link as an entry.
- **A pick edits the link.**
  The agent's pick, the guide's `?` rule and the `E_ALTERNATIVE_UNRESOLVED` remedy have the agent rewrite the title with `?` replaced.

## Proposal

An agent reading a bundle meets a link, deferred or not, and runs `pf` on it. The design states:

- **The link is the entry.**
  The positional entry is a published Markdown link, a `click` line or a class diagram `link` line, decoded by the readers that classify links, or a plain path, which selects no named preset.
- **Operator marks are set aside.**
  A link entry's leading marks direct the agent, which has acted on them, and were checked when the source compiled, so `pf` strips and ignores them; the destination compiles as an entry with the link's presets.
- **A pick is its own option.**
  `--pick <alternative>` beside a link whose title carries `?` puts that alternative in force; the alternatives listing names `--pick` as its remedy. `--pick` beside an entry carrying no `?` exits `2`.
- **The design holds no `--presets` option.**
  Every statement, fixture family and conformance case naming it states the link entry.
