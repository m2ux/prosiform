**Component:** evals/diagram-following

## Overview

Holds the diagram-following corpus, and the render check over the diagram-link fixtures, to what each one claims to measure. The linked form measures whether a decision is resolved by following its link, so its entry gives the branch away nowhere; every form's entry is held to what that form may carry; and every fixture the render check lists is a bundle Mermaid parses.

## Problem

Two forms cannot measure what they name.

- **The linked entry gives the branch away.** Its flowchart draws the branch labels `high` and `low`, `revise` and `approve`, and each decision's link text names its branch (`the escalation rule`). A session reading `inputs/severity.txt` as `high` takes the edge labelled `high` without opening the rule the decision links.
- **No entry is held to its form.** Nothing reads a form's entry document, so a label or a diagram added to an entry that must not carry one passes every check.
- **A listed bundle can fail to draw.** The semicolon fixture's bundle carries `;docs/server.md` after a sequence link line. Mermaid's sequence lexer ends the statement at `;` and parses nothing from the rest, so mermaid-cli refuses the drawing. The render check never lists the fixture, and a refused drawing would end its run with a traceback rather than naming the fixture.

## Proposal

**The scene.** A session receives one form of one workflow. The linked form exists to show whether a session opens a decision's link before choosing its branch, and the render check exists to show that a bundle's diagrams draw for a reader.

**The design.**

- **The linked entry draws no branch label.** It draws the diagram form's edges unlabelled. Each decision names two rule documents, `rule A` and `rule B`, and each rule states the state its branch enters: ``The transition into `Escalate` applies.`` The branch is recoverable only by opening the rule, and the reader holds the rule's state to an edge the diagram draws out of the deciding state.
- **Each form's entry is held to what it may carry.** `workflow.py` declares the forms whose entry draws no diagram (`prose`) and those whose entry draws no branch label (`linked`). The corpus test reads every form's entry against those declarations, and a linked entry's edges against the diagram form's.
- **The semicolon fixture draws.** The text after the semicolon is a note statement, carried as written, so the fixture still shows that the destination ends at `;` and the bundle parses.
- **The render check lists every drawing fixture.** Both unlisted `rewriting/` fixtures whose bundles draw a Mermaid diagram are listed. A test holds the lists to exactly those fixtures. A drawing mermaid-cli refuses is reported as that fixture's defect.
