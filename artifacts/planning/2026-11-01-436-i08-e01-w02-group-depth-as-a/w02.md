**Component:** main:src/prosiform/metadata.py

## Overview

`group-depth` holds a list of single-entry mappings, each a group name mapped to its depth, so one document may join several divisions at different depths and the two halves of a pair are never written apart. `group` is refused.

## Problem

A preset declares one group, and the two halves of the pair sit in separate fields.

- **One division per file.** `Preset.group` is a single string, and `cli.group_depths` maps one name to the list of files declaring it, so a document cannot be carved into two divisions.
- **The pair is two fields.** `group` holds a string and `group-depth` an integer, paired only by both being present. They can be written apart, and `decode` refuses one without the other as a repair for a rule that a single field removes.
- **Coverage is per source.** Candidate validation holds each source region to being carried once in the whole bundle, so a document emitted under two divisions is refused as a construction fault.

## Proposal

**The scene.** `metadata._preset` reads `group` and `group-depth` as separate nodes, checks their pairing and returns one name and one depth on `Preset`. `cli.group_depths`, the member-heading warning in `cli.coincident` and the merged-group arrangement in `cli.assembled` each read that one name; `candidate.coverage` counts a region's occurrences across every group.

**The design.**

- **One field, a list of pairs.** `group-depth: [{protocols: 1}, {targets: 2}]`. Each entry is a single-entry mapping from a group name to its depth, held on `Preset.memberships` as a `Membership` carrying the group, the depth and where each half is written.
- **`group` is refused.** At the top level and within a preset, `group` fails with `E_INVALID_CONVERSION_METADATA` at its key, naming the field and the `group-depth` pair that replaces it.
- **The entry shape is strict.** An entry that is not a mapping, holds no key or two keys, or whose key is not a string fails with `E_INVALID_CONVERSION_METADATA` at the entry, named `prosiform.<preset>.group-depth[<n>]`. The group-name grammar and the one-through-six depth rule are held per entry.
- **A document may join several groups.** `group_depths` collects members per name across the run and `assembled` places a document in each merged group its memberships name, once however often its preset names one. Groups sharing a first document stand in the order its memberships name them.
- **Coverage is per group.** A region is carried once within each group, because each group is a separate top-level division of the bundle.
- **Depth still agrees per name.** Two members of one group at different depths raise `E_GROUP_DEPTH_CONFLICT`, naming that group.
- **The preset in force supplies the pairs.** Until keyword selection lands, `default` is the preset in force, so its memberships place the document. Where a linked document arrives in its parent is [I09:E02](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/issues/458)'s rule.
- **Migration.** Every fixture, example, test and drawing source that declared `group` and `group-depth` declares one `group-depth` pair, and the invocation, the source page, the hierarchy page, the glossary and the design's usage example state the list form.
