# I05:E09 W01 Mapping, Option and Identity — October 2026

> Task delivery · Created 2026-10-30

## Executive Summary

The substitution mapping, the `-m` option that carries it, and the identity it matches on. The mapping and the option are one change: a mapping nothing can be passed is unobservable, and an option with nowhere to put its pairs is unused. Resolution is reached through `inside`, `resolvable`, `bundled` and `withholding_target`, and `entry` normalizes positional references and `-f` through `resolve` directly, so both paths take the mapping and neither stage can bypass it.

The row lands the seam, the option, the identity matching, and the design's own `CLI contract` row for the option — which `make conformance` reads as a clause. Four corpus fixtures prove it: a redirected link compiled under its own heading, a substitute outside the writing source's root reached through a parent segment, a key naming an absent document, and a citation the author's root refuses.

An independent review found that the clause's promise and the seam's behaviour disagreed on citations. The seam now redirects one, and the clause, the guide and the glossary state what it does in the design's own vocabulary.
