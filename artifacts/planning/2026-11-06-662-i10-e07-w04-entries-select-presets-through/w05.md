## Delivered Contract

The command accepts one `--entry` and at most one `--presets`; its list reader is the same function the source reader uses. Matching reads occurrence tags, ignores unknown names and preserves declaration-order settings over default. Headings and visible labels supply no selection. Qualified output names use matched declared preset spellings. The guide specifies relative resolution, same-document fragments, shell quoting and leading-dash values.
