## Overview

The guide's `⇉` entry says to continue with the sub-agent's answer. A dispatching agent counted the dispatched bundle's input again after the answer came back, and once recorded its own count over it. This hot-fix tells the dispatcher not to check or redo the sub-agent's work and to take its answer as given.

## Problem

- **A dispatcher does the sub-agent's work again.**
  On a three-level counting specimen under 0.28.1, 4 of 5 Haiku 5.5 dispatchers counted the source after the answer, and one recorded its own count.
- **Saying whose work it is draws the work in.**
  Replacing F25's sentence with one dividing the work between dispatcher and sub-agent left 2 of 6 dispatchers doing the work themselves, and all 6 opened the child's bundle.

## Proposal

- **The `⇉` entry adds the dispatcher's part in the answer.**
  "Do not check or redo its work: take its answer as given." A workflow that needs an answer checked draws the check as a step of its own.
