# Guide Answer — November 2026

> Hot-fix · Created 2026-11-19

## Executive Summary

A dispatching agent checks or redoes the work of the sub-agent it handed a bundle on to, and can record its own result over the answer. The guide said to continue with the answer and never said the answer stands.

The fix adds "Do not check or redo its work: take its answer as given." to the guide's `⇉` entry, after "Do not open the bundle yourself: hand on its link.", and states the same where the guide is documented.
