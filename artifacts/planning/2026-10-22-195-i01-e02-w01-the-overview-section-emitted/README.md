# The Overview Section Emitted Only Where Needed — October 2026

> Delivery · Created 2026-10-22

## Executive Summary

A merged group arranges part of the source tree by heading rather than by file. That arrangement holds. Around it the group emits an overview section whether or not anything needs one, and leaves a member identified only by its own H1 named nowhere in the bundle. This unit makes the section conditional on a member's content having no heading of its own, and holds the guarantee that a group names every member it merges.

The arrangement is what a reader of a bundled skill meets first, so a wrapper that adds a level for nothing and a document the bundle never names both cost the reader directly. Settling them also frees the level the member subsections sit at, which is what a caller asking for a folder as a section with its documents beneath has been unable to reach at any grouping depth.
