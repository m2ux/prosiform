**Component:** [src/prosiform/grouping.py](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/main/src/prosiform/grouping.py)

## Overview

The overview section of a merged group, emitted only where a member's above-depth content would otherwise sit directly beneath the group's own heading, and the naming of every member the group carries.

## Problem

The section is emitted whenever a group is merged. Two members carrying no headings are each given a subsection named from their stem, and those subsections are then wrapped in a section naming nothing the arrangement had not named; no grouping depth reaches the shorter arrangement. Separately, a member whose only identity is its own H1 has that heading absorbed into a generated heading that is then discarded, so nothing in the bundle names that document.

## Proposal

The arrangement asks whether any above-depth contribution would sit beneath the group heading with no heading of its own. Where one would, the overview section carries the contributions as it does now. Where none would, the section is not emitted and the member subsections take its place beneath the group heading, one level higher than they sit today.

The guarantee that a group names every member it merges holds under both arrangements. A member the arrangement would otherwise emit nothing naming keeps the heading that names it, which moves up with the rest rather than leaving with the wrapper, and a member identified only by its own H1 is named rather than absorbed into a heading that is discarded.

The merged sections themselves, the grouping depth that selects them and the variant headings that distinguish their contributors are unchanged.
