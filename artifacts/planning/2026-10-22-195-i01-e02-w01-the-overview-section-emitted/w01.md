**Component:** [src/prosiform/grouping.py](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/main/src/prosiform/grouping.py)

## Overview

The overview section of a merged group, emitted only where a member's above-depth content would otherwise sit directly beneath the group's own heading, and the naming of every member the group carries.

## Problem

The section is emitted whenever a group is merged. Two members carrying no headings are each given a subsection named from their stem, and those subsections are then wrapped in a section naming nothing the arrangement had not named; no grouping depth reaches the shorter arrangement. Separately, a member whose only identity is its own H1 has that heading absorbed into a generated heading that is then discarded, so nothing in the bundle names that document.

## Proposal

A merged group arranges part of the source tree by heading rather than by file, and that arrangement holds: the grouping depth selects which source heading level pools, and variant headings distinguish the documents contributing one heading text. What changes is the structure emitted around those pooled sections.

- **The overview section gathers the member subsections where the group also emits merged sections.** A member's preamble carries no heading of its own, so it is read as that member's rather than as one more of the group's own sections. Where the group emits no merged section at all, the member subsections are its whole structure and sit directly beneath the group's heading, which is the arrangement a caller asking for a folder as a section with its documents beneath reaches.
- **A member takes a subsection wherever the heading names it.** It carries content above the grouping depth, or the heading absorbs the member's own H1, or the arrangement emits nothing else from it. The last two cases emit the heading alone, so a title is absorbed only into a heading the bundle emits and every member a group merges is named by a heading the bundle carries.
- **A member whose content all sits at the grouping depth takes no subsection.** The merged sections it contributes to name it, and a whole-document reference to it resolves to its first contribution.
- **The levels follow the subsection.** The subsection sits at the second level where the group emits no merged section and at the third beneath the overview section, and the member's above-depth content shifts to sit under it, one level less where the heading absorbs the member's own H1.
