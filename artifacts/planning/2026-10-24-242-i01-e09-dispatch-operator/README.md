# Dispatch Operator — October 2026

> Field refinement · Created 2026-10-24

## Executive Summary

A deferred link states when its material is compiled and never who compiles it. A skill that hands part of its work to a sub-agent therefore states that rule in prose, where the bundle cannot carry it beside the links it governs. This record plans a second operator, `»`, for a link whose material a sub-agent compiles, the metadata key that carries the rule into the bundle's opening line, and the removal of the deferral mark from what the bundle publishes.

The work matters because it is the first thing a bundle can say about who its reader is. Every existing mark describes the bundle's own composition; this one describes the division of labour among the agents reading it, and it does so in the one place a compiled document reaches a reader without being read in full.
