**Component:** main:invocation.md

## Overview

The invocation guide states that a file's `group` pair places its own sections wherever a parent bundles it, and what a nested group costs in heading depth.

## Problem

- **The guide states pairs without placement.**
  It states which group a document joins and at what depth, and not whose pair places a linked file's sections.
- **The nesting cost is unstated.**
  A named group nests inside the division that placed it, one heading level deeper per enclosing division, and a heading pushed past H6 is refused; an author reading the guide meets that only as a failed run.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The guide's Conversion metadata section states presets and `group` pairs, and links the pages that state them in full.

- **One clause on placement.**
  The sentence saying presets govern the document's own content alone gains a clause: a file's pair places its own sections wherever a parent bundles it, and the parent's pair places the parent's sections where a further link bundles the parent.
- **One paragraph on nesting.**
  A named group nests inside the division whose document placed its first member, one level deeper per enclosing division; an entry's group stands at the top level. A heading past H6 fails with `E_HEADING_DEPTH_OVERFLOW`, so a shared library linked from a deep division overflows and the author links it from a shallower division or keeps its headings shallow. The paragraph links the pinned design's Grouping section rather than restating its clauses.
