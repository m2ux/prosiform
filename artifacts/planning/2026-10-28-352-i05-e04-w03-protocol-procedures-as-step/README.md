## Executive Summary

This record holds the delivery of I05:E04 W03. Every procedure under `example/work-planner/protocols/` is converted to the step convention W01 states and W02 applied to the modes. Each step becomes a sub-section headed `N. Action`, one heading level below its procedure. The usage-example bundles that reach a protocol are regenerated to match.

A mode or another protocol often depends on one step of a protocol. Once each step roots its own anchor, that step can be linked, which W05 then does.
