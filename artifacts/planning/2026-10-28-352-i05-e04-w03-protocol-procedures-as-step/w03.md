**Component:** example/work-planner/protocols

## Overview

Every protocol procedure writes its steps as sub-sections, so the step a mode or another protocol depends on can be linked.

## Problem

- **A protocol step roots no anchor.**
  The twelve protocols hold 55 numbered steps, one `## Procedure` each, from 3 in [`issue-delivery.md`](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/fa6913e87ef4ece26e86900c386da9c6a4fd95c9/example/work-planner/protocols/issue-delivery.md#L13-L31) to 6 in [`epic-closure.md`](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/fa6913e87ef4ece26e86900c386da9c6a4fd95c9/example/work-planner/protocols/epic-closure.md#L13-L38). A link can name a protocol's procedure but not one of its steps.
- **The rule the corpus now states is unmet.**
  W01's Step sub-sections rule in [`guidelines.md`](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/fa6913e87ef4ece26e86900c386da9c6a4fd95c9/example/work-planner/guidelines.md) requires the sub-section form the protocols do not yet take.

## Proposal

`task-merge.md`'s `1. **Merge.**` becomes `### 1. Merge` under `## Procedure`, its instruction and bullets beneath.

- **Labels become headings.**
  Each bold step label becomes its sub-section's action, after the step's number, in title case, articles kept: `### 6. Return the Initiative's Findings`.
- **Text unchanged.**
  Each step's text stays as written. `epic-closure.md`'s prose references `step 4` and `step 6` stay for W05.
- **Fixtures.**
  Each usage-example fixture whose entry reaches a protocol takes the expected bundle the converted corpus compiles to.
