## Overview

The design declares the composition contract before any compiler implementation of it: the grammar, the destination scope, ordering, matching, recursion, budgets, deferral and the refusals.

## Problem

- **No clause states composition.**
  The design states what a link includes and how deferral withholds it, and no clause names a placement directive.
- **Every later row cites a clause.**
  Each composition fixture names the clause it exercises.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The design states references, deferral, presets, `subst` and the post-compilation invariants.

- **A *Section composition* clause.**
  Recognition, the destination scope, contribution headings, source-relative links, ordering and matching, recursion and budgets, deferral, the eligible target and the failures.
- **Composition under the link rules.**
  The containing heading's title selects the target's presets and the writer's `subst` redirects the reference.
- **The pin moves.**
  The README's design link moves to the engineering commit carrying the amendment.
