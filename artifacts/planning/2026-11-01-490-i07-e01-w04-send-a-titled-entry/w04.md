**Component:** src/prosiform/bundle.py

## Overview

Resolves a whole-document reference to a titled parent that heads its own file group to the parent's title heading, so one bundle names the parent document one way whether or not the parent declares a group.

## Problem

`group` in `src/prosiform/bundle.py` gives a parent heading its own file group no file heading: its title opens the bundle at the level its author wrote. A whole-document reference to that parent takes the destination of its first H2, so a second document's back-link to the entry lands inside the entry's first section. A titled parent inside a merged group resolves to its title heading at every grouping depth, so the two parent rules disagree, and the design's statement that the parent's title is its file heading where the bundle opens on it holds for the merged case alone.

## Proposal

A reader follows a link back to the guide from the notes it reads and arrives at the top of the guide, at the heading that names it.

- **One destination for the parent.** The parent's whole-document reference answers to the heading carrying its title. A parent carrying an H1 at the document level but no single title answers to its first such H1.
- **One rule in the module.** The module and `group` docstrings state the one rule for both the file-group and the merged-group parent.
- **A fixture shows it.** A `destinations` corpus fixture bundles a titled entry with a first section and a second document linking back to it; the back-link resolves to `#guide`, where the first-H2 rule gave `#steps`.
