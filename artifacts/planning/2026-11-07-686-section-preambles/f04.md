**Component:** main:src/prosiform/preamble.py

## Overview

Group the title and introduction of every fragment-only selected document beneath its parent folder, while preserving its selected sections' declared grouping.

## Problem

A fragment selection omits its document's context and can emit empty per-member overview subsections for structural ancestors.

## Proposal

Build a separate preamble contribution for grouped documents reached only through fragment selections. Accumulate each parent folder's contributions in a top-level group, preserve source bytes and links, and do not expand dependency discovery. A whole-document selection retains its own group without a duplicate preamble.
