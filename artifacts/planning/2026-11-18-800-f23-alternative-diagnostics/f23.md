## Overview

Three diagnostics around alternatives say the wrong thing. A `default` pair agreeing with a picked alternative's pair leaves the redirect silent, though `docs/selection.md` makes it a stand-in that warns `W_SUBST_USED`. A `?` dispatch whose brief an alternative routes, or whose target's alternative names a brief of its own, is refused under a remedy that would remove the pick. This hot-fix, with #801 and #807 folded in, makes the first warn and gives the others remedies the pick token admits.

## Problem

- **A stand-in redirect raises nothing.**
  `governing` and `copies` put a document's `default` pairs in force beneath the selected presets' pairs, and a selected preset's pair replaces `default`'s for its key. `layer` marks an alternative's pair routing unless an ordinary preset states it, so an agreeing `default` pair never reaches the standing pairs `routes` reads.
- **A picking dispatch's remedy defeats the pick token.**
  `briefing` drops `?` from the brief's tags, so `resolved` sees an ordinary copy and `unresolved` remedies it by naming the alternative in the dispatch's title, which turns the dispatch into a fixed pick.
- **An alternative naming a brief fails without the dispatch.**
  `briefed` raises `unresolved` with no placing, so the failure names no dispatch and its remedy asks for a pick in each reaching link.

## Proposal

- **`layer` reads the `default` pairs.**
  It takes the document's `default` pairs as `standing`; an alternative's pair agreeing with `default`'s for its key is no routing pair. `governing` and `copies` pass `defaults` to it, so the rule keeps one owner.
- **`resolved` knows a picking dispatch's brief.**
  It takes the bundle's dispatch hops, which `traverse` already returns, and maps each `?` dispatch's brief, as that dispatch places it. A copy none is in force for is placed by a reference whose own section is routed, a picking brief first, and `unresolved` remedies a carried brief by naming its section and stating that it must compile with no alternative in force.
- **`briefed` names the picking dispatch.**
  It passes the dispatch as `placing` with `varying`, whose remedy states that the brief must not vary by alternative.
