## Overview

The design states `prosiform` as presets and nothing else: what selects a preset, what `default` does, how selected presets combine, and what a selection governs. The pin names the revision carrying it, so every fixture this epic writes cites a clause the pinned design carries.

## Problem

The pinned design states a closed, flat key set, and a preset is not in it.

- **The key set admits two flat fields.**
  *Conversion metadata* admits `group` and `group-depth` at the top of `prosiform` and refuses any other key, so a preset has no clause to stand on and no fixture of this epic has one to cite.
- **A reference's governing settings have no name.**
  The design states that a target's declaration decides inclusion; it does not state that the section linking a document decides which of that document's settings are in force.
- **The keyword rule is a decision.**
  Matching a preset name against a heading's text as whole words is a choice about authored prose meeting declarative settings, and the design is where that choice is recorded.

## Proposal

**The scene.** *Conversion metadata* states the admitted keys and the field table; the grouping clause states membership.

**The design.**

- **`prosiform` holds presets alone.** Every key is a preset name holding a mapping of `group-depth`, `exclude` and `path-sub`; a key holding anything else, and a field outside the three, fails with `E_INVALID_CONVERSION_METADATA`. The cited bullet titles *The admitted keys*, *`root` is refused*, *An empty block declares* and *Unrelated fields have no conversion meaning* stand, so existing citations resolve.
- **Entry shapes beside the key rule.** `group` is refused in favour of `group-depth` pairs; `exclude` holds section names and name-to-level mappings; `path-sub` holds `ORIGINAL=ALTERNATIVE` strings resolved from the declaring document; a mapping entry holds one string key with an integer value; budgets and invocation options are refused in a preset.
- **A *Presets* clause.** A new subsection states selection by keyword (case-insensitive, whole-word, against the title of the section containing the reference), `default` always in force and reserved, the entry rule, union and its refusal as invalid usage with exit status `2`, the reach of a selection stopping at the declaring document, one document under several selections, a document in several groups, `profile` against `preset`, and the unused-preset warning with its deferral exception, stated by its effect: the catalogue test holds every `W_` code the design names to the schema's catalogue, so W06 names the code with the fixture that reaches it.
- **Ripple edits.** The grouping clause, the groups contract, *Exactly-once coverage*, *Metadata failures*, the path-resolution paragraph and *Metadata conformance cases* state the preset model.
- **The pin moves.** I08:E00 merged before this row, so `README.md`'s design link moves to the engineering commit carrying this amendment.
