**Component:** src/prosiform/bundle.py

## Overview

A bundle that dispatches opens with a `# Prosiform` section stating the protocols its dispatch sub-sections invoke, in wording the profile fixes, so an agent reading top-down meets each protocol before the step that uses it. Each sub-section's instruction links the protocol it follows. The changed output is the `skill-markdown-v4` profile.

## Problem

- **The protocols live outside the bundle.**
  How to dispatch, and how to dispatch together, is the example's own [bootstrap symbol table](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/f2d558a37f6999853b3e8335dd22bcd1a1e015c0/example/work-planner/bootstrap.md), in wording each corpus writes for itself.
- **The sub-sections name no protocol.**
  [`instruction()`](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/f2d558a37f6999853b3e8335dd22bcd1a1e015c0/src/prosiform/dispatch.py#L412) and [`TOGETHER`](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/f2d558a37f6999853b3e8335dd22bcd1a1e015c0/src/prosiform/dispatch.py#L108) say what to dispatch and not how.
- **The profile is unchanged.**
  `pf -v` reports [`skill-markdown-v3`](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/f2d558a37f6999853b3e8335dd22bcd1a1e015c0/src/prosiform/__init__.py#L8) though the same source bundles differently.

## Proposal

A guide dispatching its plan opens with `# Prosiform` and `## Dispatch`, and its `Dispatch Plan` sub-section reads "Dispatch `plan.bundle.md` to a sub-agent, as [Dispatch](#dispatch) states." A review procedure that also dispatches its reviews in parallel carries `## Dispatch` and `## Parallel dispatch`; a bundle using neither has no Prosiform section and is the bundle it was.

- **Presence.**
  `## Dispatch` is present whenever the bundle uses `⇉` or `⫽`, because a parallel dispatch is dispatch done together and its protocol defers to Dispatch. `## Parallel dispatch` is present only when the bundle uses `⫽`.
- **Placement.**
  The section is the bundle's first heading: ahead of the first group's file heading, or, where the parent opens on its own title, after the content above that title and ahead of any dispatch sub-section that content takes.
- **Fixed levels.**
  The section is `#` and its protocols `##` in every bundle, so each protocol sub-section is byte-identical wherever it appears. Its destinations are `#dispatch` and `#parallel-dispatch`, the bundle's first headings of those slugs.
- **Protocol links.**
  A dispatch sub-section's line ends "as [Dispatch](#dispatch) states"; a parallel sub-section's instruction ends "as [Parallel dispatch](#parallel-dispatch) states". Candidate validation holds each link an instruction carries to a Prosiform protocol heading.
- **pf's headings.**
  An authored heading equal to `Dispatch` or `Parallel dispatch` in a bundle pf emits it in is refused with `E_HEADING_COLLISION`.
- **Profile v4.**
  `PROFILE` becomes `skill-markdown-v4`, with every statement of the identifier, the design's profile clause and the pin.
