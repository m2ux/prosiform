## Overview

A document declaring alternatives needs one in force only where an alternative changes what its selected content compiles to. A section no alternative changes compiles under `default` and the ordinary presets its references select, so a reference to it names no alternative.

## Problem

- **A section no alternative changes still needs a pick.**
  Exactly one alternative is in force wherever a document declaring them is compiled, whatever the selected content writes. Composing such a section under no name fails with `E_ALTERNATIVE_UNRESOLVED`.
- **The caller names an alternative arbitrarily.**
  A route dispatching a stage under each alternative and composing the stage's one contract writes one alternative's name on the composition, and the published link carries that name to the reader.

## Proposal

- **The rule counts what the alternatives change.**
  An alternative is needed where one of them states a `group` pair, or a `subst` pair whose key a link in the selected content names. Content none of them changes compiles under `default` and the ordinary presets its references select.
- **A pick that is made is honoured.**
  A reference naming an alternative selects it, and several in force still fail.
- **The failure stands where a route is needed.**
  Selected content writing a routed link with no alternative in force fails with `E_ALTERNATIVE_UNRESOLVED`, listing the alternatives.
