**Component:** main:src/prosiform/closure.py

## Overview

A `subst` pair redirects only the links its declaring document writes. The document it links, the document linking it, a substitute and a dispatched target are each governed by their own presets alone, and the run's record of a pair is that pair's, keyed by its declaring document.

## Problem

The reach is delivered and unwitnessed, and the record is not the pair's.

- **No fixture holds the reach.** `Substitution.writing` puts a document's pairs above the run's keys for that document's links alone, and a dispatched target's bundle builds its own `governing` table, but nothing in the corpus fails when a pair reaches a further document, a substitute's fragment-only link or a dispatched bundle.
- **The record is keyed by identity alone.** `Redirects` marks a key served and records a substitution under the identity the key names. Two documents each declaring a pair for `rules.md` share one record: the first document's link serves the second document's pair, so a pair no reference used raises no `W_SUBST_UNUSED`, and two used pairs raise one `W_SUBST_USED`.

## Proposal

**The scene.** `traverse` and `compiled` pass `closure.inside` a mapping carrying each document's governing pairs, and `inside` reads `Substitution.writing(document)` for the link it resolves; every mapping of the run shares one `Redirects`.

**The design.**

- **The mapping is the writing document's.** Each link resolves through the pairs of the presets governing the document that writes it, and through no other document's.
- **A substitute is governed by its own presets.** A link the substitute writes, a fragment-only one included, resolves through the substitute's own pairs, so `[part](#part)` names the substitute's own section.
- **A dispatched target's bundle reads its own presets.** Each dispatched compilation builds its pairs from the documents its own bundle carries.
- **The record is the pair's.** A mapping `writing` returns names its writer, and a redirect served by that writer's pair is recorded and marked served under the writer and the key, so `W_SUBST_USED` and `W_SUBST_UNUSED` report once per declared pair.
