**Component:** main:docs/patterns/fork-join-set.md

## Overview

The Fork-Join Set pattern page: parallel composition and fork-join: processes started together and joined before the sequence continues, realised by the parallel dispatch mark and its parallel dispatch sub-section, with a specimen the build compiles and checks.

## Problem

- **The grammar affords it, and nothing names it.**
  The parallel dispatch mark and its parallel dispatch sub-section already behave as parallel composition and fork-join, but no page tells an author so.
- **An author re-derives it per skill.**
  Without a named pattern, each skill states its own version of the structure as private rules.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The catalogue's template and checks are in place, and the Shared Rule page shows a conforming page and specimen.

**The design.**

- **Paradigm.**
  Parallel composition and fork-join: processes started together and joined before the sequence continues, cited from Hoare, Communicating Sequential Processes.
- **Grammar.**
  The parallel dispatch mark and its parallel dispatch sub-section, each linked to its documentation section.
- **Specimen.**
  A tree at `docs/patterns/fork-join-set/`: one section dispatching three reviews in parallel, followed by a section joining their reports.
- **What the bundle shows.**
  One parallel dispatch sub-section listing the three bundles, which the set holds, committed as the specimen's expected bundle set.
- **Principles.**
  The page's `Related` names the SOLID principles it serves: single responsibility.
- **The limit to probe.**
  The page states whether the join can name what each parallel bundle returns; a limit found is stated under `Limits` and raised as its own issue.
