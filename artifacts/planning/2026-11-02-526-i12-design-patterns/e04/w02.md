## Overview

The Sequence Protocol pattern page: a protocol between communicating processes, as CSP states it and a sequence diagram draws it, realised by a sequence diagram's `link` and `links` participant menus, with a specimen the build compiles and checks.

## Problem

- **The grammar affords it, and nothing names it.**
  A sequence diagram's `link` and `links` participant menus already behave as a protocol between communicating processes, but no page tells an author so.
- **An author re-derives it per skill.**
  Without a named pattern, each skill states its own version of the structure as private rules.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The catalogue's template and checks are in place, and the Shared Rule page shows a conforming page and specimen.

**The design.**

- **Paradigm.**
  A protocol between communicating processes, as CSP states it and a sequence diagram draws it, cited from Hoare, Communicating Sequential Processes.
- **Grammar.**
  A sequence diagram's `link` and `links` participant menus, each linked to its documentation section.
- **Specimen.**
  A tree at `docs/patterns/sequence-protocol/`: a sequence diagram whose participants link the artifacts that take part.
- **What the bundle shows.**
  Each participant's artifact, every menu destination rewritten to it, committed as the specimen's expected bundle set.
- **Principles.**
  The page's `Related` names the SOLID principles it serves: interface segregation.
- **The limit to probe.**
  The page states whether a message arrow can link the operation it calls; a limit found is stated under `Limits` and raised as its own issue.
