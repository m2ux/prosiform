## Overview

The Linked Flowchart pattern page: flow-based programming, where the network is defined outside its components, and the statechart, realised by diagram `click` lines as references, bundled and rewritten, with a specimen the build compiles and checks.

## Problem

- **The grammar affords it, and nothing names it.**
  Diagram `click` lines as references, bundled and rewritten already behave as flow-based programming, but no page tells an author so.
- **An author re-derives it per skill.**
  Without a named pattern, each skill states its own version of the structure as private rules.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The catalogue's template and checks are in place, and the Shared Rule page shows a conforming page and specimen.

**The design.**

- **Paradigm.**
  Flow-based programming, where the network is defined outside its components, and the statechart, cited from Morrison, Flow-Based Programming; Harel, Statecharts.
- **Grammar.**
  Diagram `click` lines as references, bundled and rewritten, each linked to its documentation section.
- **Specimen.**
  A tree at `docs/patterns/linked-flowchart/`: a flowchart whose three nodes each click to a procedure section.
- **What the bundle shows.**
  Each node's section, every click rewritten to its section in the bundle, committed as the specimen's expected bundle set.
- **Principles.**
  The page's `Related` names the SOLID principles it serves: single responsibility, open-closed.
- **The limit to probe.**
  The page states whether a state diagram's states can link their procedures; a limit found is stated under `Limits` and raised as its own issue.
