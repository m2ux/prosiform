**Component:** main:docs/patterns/artifact.md

## Overview

The Artifact pattern page: DCI: data in an artifact's Fields, interaction in its Operations, and a mode as the context that binds them, realised by section selection, consolidation and placement order, with a specimen the build compiles and checks.

## Problem

- **The grammar affords it, and nothing names it.**
  Section selection, consolidation and placement order already behave as DCI, but no page tells an author so.
- **An author re-derives it per skill.**
  Without a named pattern, each skill states its own version of the structure as private rules.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The catalogue's template and checks are in place, and the Shared Rule page shows a conforming page and specimen.

**The design.**

- **Paradigm.**
  DCI: data in an artifact's Fields, interaction in its Operations, and a mode as the context that binds them, cited from Reenskaug and Coplien, The DCI Architecture.
- **Grammar.**
  Section selection, consolidation and placement order, each linked to its documentation section.
- **Specimen.**
  A tree at `docs/patterns/artifact/`: a mode calling two operations of one artifact by heading link; the artifact carries Fields and three Operations.
- **What the bundle shows.**
  The two operations called and the Fields section they link, and not the third operation, committed as the specimen's expected bundle set.
- **Principles.**
  The page's `Related` names the SOLID principles it serves: single responsibility, interface segregation.
- **The limit to probe.**
  The page states whether anything states which sections of an artifact are its public operations; a limit found is stated under `Limits` and raised as its own issue.
