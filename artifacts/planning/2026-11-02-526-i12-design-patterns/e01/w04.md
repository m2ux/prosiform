## Overview

The Facade Entry pattern page: a facade: one simple entry over a subsystem, and progressive disclosure of what it fronts, realised by the entry reference, deferral and the guide `pf` prints, with a specimen the build compiles and checks.

## Problem

- **The grammar affords it, and nothing names it.**
  The entry reference, deferral and the guide `pf` prints already behave as a facade, but no page tells an author so.
- **An author re-derives it per skill.**
  Without a named pattern, each skill states its own version of the structure as private rules.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The catalogue's template and checks are in place, and the Shared Rule page shows a conforming page and specimen.

**The design.**

- **Paradigm.**
  A facade: one simple entry over a subsystem, and progressive disclosure of what it fronts, cited from Gamma et al., Design Patterns (Facade); Anthropic, Equipping Agents for the Real World with Agent Skills.
- **Grammar.**
  The entry reference, deferral and the guide `pf` prints, each linked to its documentation section.
- **Specimen.**
  A tree at `docs/patterns/facade-entry/`: a skill entry naming two modes, each by a deferral.
- **What the bundle shows.**
  An entry bundle carrying neither mode, each named by the path its own run compiles, committed as the specimen's expected bundle set.
- **Principles.**
  The page's `Related` names the SOLID principles it serves: dependency inversion.
- **The limit to probe.**
  The page states whether an entry can list what it fronts without the list drifting from the tree; a limit found is stated under `Limits` and raised as its own issue.
