## Overview

The Contract and Implementation pattern page: an ML signature beside its structure, a header beside its implementation, and design by contract, realised by section composition, and the deferral that withholds an implementation, with a specimen the build compiles and checks.

## Problem

- **The grammar affords it, and nothing names it.**
  Section composition, and the deferral that withholds an implementation already behave as an ML signature beside its structure, but no page tells an author so.
- **An author re-derives it per skill.**
  Without a named pattern, each skill states its own version of the structure as private rules.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The catalogue's template and checks are in place, and the Shared Rule page shows a conforming page and specimen.

**The design.**

- **Paradigm.**
  An ML signature beside its structure, a header beside its implementation, and design by contract, cited from MacQueen, Modules for Standard ML; Meyer, Applying Design by Contract.
- **Grammar.**
  Section composition, and the deferral that withholds an implementation, each linked to its documentation section.
- **Specimen.**
  A tree at `docs/patterns/contract-and-implementation/`: a procedure step composing the contract section of two operations, each operation's body deferred.
- **What the bundle shows.**
  One composed contract section under the step, each operation's contract beneath its own parent's name, and neither body, committed as the specimen's expected bundle set.
- **Principles.**
  The page's `Related` names the SOLID principles it serves: interface segregation, dependency inversion.
- **The limit to probe.**
  The page states whether a contract section can be checked against the body it describes; a limit found is stated under `Limits` and raised as its own issue.
