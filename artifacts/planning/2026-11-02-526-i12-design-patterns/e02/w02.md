**Component:** main:docs/patterns/call-site-dispatch.md

## Overview

The Call-Site Dispatch pattern page: ad hoc polymorphism resolved at the call, as multiple dispatch selects a method from every argument and refuses an ambiguous call, realised by whole-word preset selection by section title, and the union of the presets one title selects, with a specimen the build compiles and checks.

## Problem

- **The grammar affords it, and nothing names it.**
  Whole-word preset selection by section title, and the union of the presets one title selects already behave as ad hoc polymorphism resolved at the call, but no page tells an author so.
- **An author re-derives it per skill.**
  Without a named pattern, each skill states its own version of the structure as private rules.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The catalogue's template and checks are in place, and the Shared Rule page shows a conforming page and specimen.

**The design.**

- **Paradigm.**
  Ad hoc polymorphism resolved at the call, as multiple dispatch selects a method from every argument and refuses an ambiguous call, cited from Bezanson et al., Julia: A Fresh Approach to Numerical Computing; Cardelli and Wegner.
- **Grammar.**
  Whole-word preset selection by section title, and the union of the presets one title selects, each linked to its documentation section.
- **Specimen.**
  A tree at `docs/patterns/call-site-dispatch/`: a caller section titled `Checks and Draft` selecting two presets of one callee, one stating a `group` pair and the other a `subst`.
- **What the bundle shows.**
  The callee placed by the one preset's pair and redirected by the other's `subst`, committed as the specimen's expected bundle set.
- **Principles.**
  The page's `Related` names the SOLID principles it serves: open-closed.
- **The limit to probe.**
  The page states whether a title selecting a preset by accident can be told from one selecting it by intent; a limit found is stated under `Limits` and raised as its own issue.
