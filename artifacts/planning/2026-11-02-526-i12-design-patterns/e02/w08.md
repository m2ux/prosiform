**Component:** main:docs/patterns/alternative-selection.md

## Overview

The Alternative Selection pattern page: a variant whose case its tag selects, realised by `choice` alternatives that the reference reaching a document picks by title, with a specimen the build compiles and checks.

## Problem

- **The grammar affords it, and nothing names it.**
  A preset holding `choice` is an alternative, and a link's title picks one, but no page tells an author this is selection by kind.
- **An author re-derives it per skill.**
  Without a named pattern, each skill states its own version of the structure as private rules.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The catalogue's template and checks are in place, and the Shared Rule page shows a conforming page and specimen.

**The design.**

- **Paradigm.**
  A variant type whose tag selects its case, cited from Cardelli and Wegner, On Understanding Types, Data Abstraction, and Polymorphism.
- **Grammar.**
  `choice` alternatives, the pick by link title and `subst` routing pairs, each linked to its documentation section.
- **Specimen.**
  A tree at `docs/patterns/alternative-selection/`: one document declaring two alternatives, each substituting a different section, and an entry picking one by title.
- **What the bundle shows.**
  The picked alternative's substitute and not the other's, committed as the specimen's expected bundle set.
- **Principles.**
  The page's `Related` names the SOLID principles it serves: open–closed, since a new kind is a new alternative.
- **The limit to probe.**
  The page states whether one reference can pick alternatives of two documents it reaches; a limit found is stated under `Limits` and raised as its own issue.
