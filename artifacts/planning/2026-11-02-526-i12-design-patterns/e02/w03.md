**Component:** main:docs/patterns/injected-strategy.md

## Overview

The Injected Strategy pattern page: a strategy chosen by dependency injection, with the entry as the composition root and the procedure's link as a port, realised by presets the linking section's title selects, and `subst` from a port to an adapter, with a specimen the build compiles and checks.

## Problem

- **The grammar affords it, and nothing names it.**
  Presets the linking section's title selects, and `subst` from a port to an adapter already behave as a strategy chosen by dependency injection, but no page tells an author so.
- **An author re-derives it per skill.**
  Without a named pattern, each skill states its own version of the structure as private rules.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The catalogue's template and checks are in place, and the Shared Rule page shows a conforming page and specimen.

**The design.**

- **Paradigm.**
  A strategy chosen by dependency injection, with the entry as the composition root and the procedure's link as a port, cited from Fowler, Inversion of Control Containers and the Dependency Injection pattern; Cockburn, Hexagonal Architecture.
- **Grammar.**
  Presets the linking section's title selects, and `subst` from a port to an adapter, each linked to its documentation section.
- **Specimen.**
  A tree at `docs/patterns/injected-strategy/`: a release procedure linking `strategy.md`, its `safe` and `fast` presets each substituting an adapter, and an entry section titled `Safe Release`.
- **What the bundle shows.**
  The safe adapter in place of the port, and not the fast one, committed as the specimen's expected bundle set.
- **Principles.**
  The page's `Related` names the SOLID principles it serves: dependency inversion, open-closed.
- **The limit to probe.**
  The page states whether a port can state that it must be substituted; a limit found is stated under `Limits` and raised as its own issue.
