**Component:** main:docs/patterns/generic-procedure.md

## Overview

The Generic Procedure pattern page: parametric polymorphism instantiated per type, as an ML functor applied or a generic monomorphised, realised by presets the linking section's title selects, `subst`, and one document bundled under two presets, with a specimen the build compiles and checks.

## Problem

- **The grammar affords it, and nothing names it.**
  Presets the linking section's title selects, `subst`, and one document bundled under two presets already behave as parametric polymorphism instantiated per type, but no page tells an author so.
- **An author re-derives it per skill.**
  Without a named pattern, each skill states its own version of the structure as private rules.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The catalogue's template and checks are in place, and the Shared Rule page shows a conforming page and specimen.

**The design.**

- **Paradigm.**
  Parametric polymorphism instantiated per type, as an ML functor applied or a generic monomorphised, cited from Cardelli and Wegner, On Understanding Types, Data Abstraction, and Polymorphism; The Rust Book, Generic Data Types.
- **Grammar.**
  Presets the linking section's title selects, `subst`, and one document bundled under two presets, each linked to its documentation section.
- **Specimen.**
  A tree at `docs/patterns/generic-procedure/`: two caller sections, `Epic Review` and `Task Review`, linking one review procedure whose `epic` and `task` presets each substitute its subject.
- **What the bundle shows.**
  An instance of the procedure per subject, each instance's subject link reaching its own subject, committed as the specimen's expected bundle set.
- **Principles.**
  The page's `Related` names the SOLID principles it serves: open-closed.
- **The limit to probe.**
  The page states whether an instance's heading can name the preset that made it; a limit found is stated under `Limits` and raised as its own issue.
