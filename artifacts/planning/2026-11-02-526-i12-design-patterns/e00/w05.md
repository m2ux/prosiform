**Component:** main:docs/patterns/shared-rule.md

## Overview

The Shared Rule pattern page: a mixin, and the traits rule that one method reached by two paths is no conflict; DITA's conref, realised by declaration, section selection and consolidation, with a specimen the build compiles and checks.

## Problem

- **The grammar affords it, and nothing names it.**
  Declaration, section selection and consolidation already behave as a mixin, but no page tells an author so.
- **An author re-derives it per skill.**
  Without a named pattern, each skill states its own version of the structure as private rules.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The page check and the bundle check are in place, and no page has passed them yet; this page is the first, and shows the template end to end.

**The design.**

- **Paradigm.**
  A mixin, and the traits rule that one method reached by two paths is no conflict; DITA's conref, cited from Schärli et al., Traits: Composable Units of Behaviour; DITA 1.3 conref.
- **Grammar.**
  Declaration, section selection and consolidation, each linked to its documentation section.
- **Specimen.**
  A tree at `docs/patterns/shared-rule/`: two procedures, each linking one rule section of `rules.md`.
- **What the bundle shows.**
  The rule section once, both procedures' links pointing at it, committed as the specimen's expected bundle set.
- **Principles.**
  The page's `Related` names the SOLID principles it serves: single responsibility.
- **The limit to probe.**
  The page states whether a rule linked from every site can be stated once without a link per site; a limit found is stated under `Limits` and raised as its own issue.
