**Component:** main:docs/patterns/trait-group.md

## Overview

The Trait Group pattern page: traits composed with their conflicts made visible, where a class would otherwise inherit one of two same-named methods, realised by `group` pairs, merged groups, member names and variants, with a specimen the build compiles and checks.

## Problem

- **The grammar affords it, and nothing names it.**
  `group` pairs, merged groups, member names and variants already behave as traits composed with their conflicts made visible, but no page tells an author so.
- **An author re-derives it per skill.**
  Without a named pattern, each skill states its own version of the structure as private rules.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The catalogue's template and checks are in place, and the Shared Rule page shows a conforming page and specimen.

**The design.**

- **Paradigm.**
  Traits composed with their conflicts made visible, where a class would otherwise inherit one of two same-named methods, cited from Schärli et al., Traits: Composable Units of Behaviour.
- **Grammar.**
  `group` pairs, merged groups, member names and variants, each linked to its documentation section.
- **Specimen.**
  A tree at `docs/patterns/trait-group/`: two documents declaring one group, each contributing a `Tests` section.
- **What the bundle shows.**
  One `Tests` heading with a variant for each member, committed as the specimen's expected bundle set.
- **Principles.**
  The page's `Related` names the SOLID principles it serves: single responsibility.
- **The limit to probe.**
  The page states whether a variant can be excluded or aliased, as a trait conflict is resolved; a limit found is stated under `Limits` and raised as its own issue.
