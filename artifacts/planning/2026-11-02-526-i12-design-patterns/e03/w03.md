**Component:** main:docs/patterns/decorator-section.md

## Overview

The Decorator Section pattern page: the decorator: a wrapper adding behaviour under the wrapped thing's interface, as around-advice does, realised by section composition beneath the decorator's own heading, with a specimen the build compiles and checks.

## Problem

- **The grammar affords it, and nothing names it.**
  Section composition beneath the decorator's own heading already behave as the decorator, but no page tells an author so.
- **An author re-derives it per skill.**
  Without a named pattern, each skill states its own version of the structure as private rules.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The catalogue's template and checks are in place, and the Shared Rule page shows a conforming page and specimen.

**The design.**

- **Paradigm.**
  The decorator: a wrapper adding behaviour under the wrapped thing's interface, as around-advice does, cited from Gamma et al., Design Patterns (Decorator); Kiczales et al., Aspect-Oriented Programming.
- **Grammar.**
  Section composition beneath the decorator's own heading, each linked to its documentation section.
- **Specimen.**
  A tree at `docs/patterns/decorator-section/`: a guarded publish section stating a precondition and composing the publish section beneath it.
- **What the bundle shows.**
  The decorated section beneath the decorator's heading, after the decorator's own content, committed as the specimen's expected bundle set.
- **Principles.**
  The page's `Related` names the SOLID principles it serves: open-closed.
- **The limit to probe.**
  The page states whether one decoration can reach every section matching a rule, as a pointcut does; a limit found is stated under `Limits` and raised as its own issue.
