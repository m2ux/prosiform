**Component:** main:src/prosiform/composition.py

## Overview

A composed reference obeys the rules every link obeys: the title of its scope selects the target's presets, whose `subst` governs the contribution, and the writing document's `subst` redirects the directive before its target is read. Fixtures pin both, so a later change to composition cannot route a directive around them.

## Problem

- **The link rules are unpinned for a directive.**
  W02 resolves a directive through the closure's reference resolution and composes the section it finds, and the closure records the directive as a reference reaching its target. No fixture shows a preset the scope's title selects governing a contribution, a `group` pair placing none, or a writer's `subst` redirecting a directive to a stub.
- **The composition module states no preset rule.**
  Its docstring says where a contribution resolves its links, not which presets and pairs govern the directive and the contribution.

## Proposal

**The scene.** Preset selection reads the title of the section containing a reference, and a document's `subst` pairs redirect the references it writes, from E06's merge into `main`. A directive is a reference: the closure selects its target's presets from the scope's title, and composition resolves the directive with the substitution in force for the writing document.

- **Presets.**
  The scope's title selects the target's presets, and their `subst` redirects the references the contribution writes, since a contribution is rewritten against its source under the pairs in force for that source. A `group` pair the selection states places the document where an ordinary reference bundles it and places no contribution.
- **Substitution.**
  The writer's `subst` redirects the directive before its target is read, so the destination section, the contribution heading and the content come from the substitute.
- **Statement.**
  The composition module's docstring states both, beside where a contribution resolves its links.
- **Main and the design pin.**
  The branch carries `main` with I08:E06 and I08:E08, and the README pins the latest design commit holding every clause in `main` and E04's own.
- **Debt.**
  W08's inline-only dispatch fixture names a section title the design states nowhere once its usage examples retire, which the independence check refuses; its description states the dispatch without it.
