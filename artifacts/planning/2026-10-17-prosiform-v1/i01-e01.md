## Overview

Specification revision is the order an accepted finding travels in and the revisions that travel it. The design changes first, the corpus follows, and the build follows the corpus. A finding accepted after this epic opens becomes a further row of it, so the refinement that remains is visible rather than implied.

## Problem

A correction applied in the wrong place leaves the contract wrong and the next implementation reading it.

- **A fix in the build leaves the design asserting the original behaviour.**
  The design is what a second implementation reads, so a bundling corrected only in code carries its correction nowhere.

- **A corpus changed first makes the design follow the fixtures.**
  A fixture edited to match an observed result turns an accident into the contract.

- **A revision with no trace cannot be reviewed.**
  A design change whose motivating finding is not recorded reads as an unexplained edit.

- **A clause added under refinement has no fixture.**
  It produces no failure, so the gap presents as a passing corpus.

- **An unbounded refinement has no visible end.**
  Without a measure of what remains open, there is no way to judge how close the specification is to settling.

- **An emission changed in the build alone leaves the corpus refusing it.**
  The bundle's opening sentence and the first entry document's placement changed in the build, so the design states neither and the fixtures still expect the emission that preceded it. `make check` fails on both counts.

## Proposal

- **Design, then corpus, then build.**
  An accepted finding changes the design clause it contradicts; the corpus gains or amends the fixture that would have caught it; the prototype changes until that fixture passes.

- **Each revision cites its finding.**
  The design and corpus revision history names the ledger entry that motivated the change.

- **Traceability is refreshed with each revision.**
  The clause-to-fixture mapping is regenerated whenever the design or the corpus changes, so a clause added without a fixture appears as uncovered on the revision that added it.

- **An accepted finding becomes a row.**
  Rows are added to this epic as findings are accepted, each covering the criterion stating what resolving it makes true.

- **A prerequisite section goes when bundling replaces it.**
  A skill's prerequisite section names whole documents, coarsely but completely, and the inline links inside a procedure name sections precisely but may not cover everything that list covered. Removal is therefore gated on a recorded bundling supplying what the list named, and a shortfall is a ledger entry rather than a silent loss.

- **The instruction replaces the list.**
  Where a section said which documents to read, the skill's own text says which invocation bundles them. The agent is told to bundle rather than left to infer it, and how clearly that reads is itself something use reports.

- **What remains open is reported from the ledger.**
  The summary is derived rather than maintained, so it cannot drift from the entries it describes.
- **The design states the emission before the corpus expects it.**
  The bundle's opening sentence and the first entry document's place are stated in the design, so a fixture expectation follows from a clause rather than from what a build happened to produce.

- [ ] **AC5.** A prerequisite section is removed only where a recorded bundling run supplies the sources it named, as the removal record shows.
- [ ] **AC6.** A source a prerequisite section named that the bundling did not supply is a ledger entry, as the ledger shows.
- [ ] **AC7.** A skill whose prerequisite section is removed carries in its own text the invocation that bundles what the section named, as the removal record shows.
- [ ] **AC8.** The design states the sentence a bundle opens with and the sentence each further entry reference adds, as the revision-order check reads it against the build's emitted opening line.
- [ ] **AC9.** The design states that the first entry document's title, preamble and section order lead the bundle, as the revision-order check reads it against a grouped bundle.
- [ ] **AC10.** The corpus expectations agree with the stated emission and `make check` passes, as the corpus run and the unit suite show on one revision.
