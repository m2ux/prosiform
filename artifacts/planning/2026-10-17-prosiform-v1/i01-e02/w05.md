**Component:** [invocation.md](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/main/invocation.md)

## Overview

The documented grouping rule and the examples and diagrams that show what it produces.

## Problem

The invocation guide states a grouping rule carrying a heading level, and its worked example passes two of them. The diagrams beside the documented commands show the heading-keyed arrangement and its mixed heading casing, so the documentation describes output no run now produces.

## Proposal

The documented rule is the folder slug, and the examples beside it carry the member arrangement a run of them emits. The rendered diagrams and the retained planning SVGs are regenerated against that output.
