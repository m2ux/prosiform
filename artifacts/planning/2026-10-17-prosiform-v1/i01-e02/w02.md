**Component:** [src/prosiform/grouping.py](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/main/src/prosiform/grouping.py)

## Overview

The destination a whole-document reference to a merged member reaches, held to a heading that names that member alone.

## Problem

A member whose content all sits at the grouping depth is resolved to its first contribution. Two members whose first contribution carries one heading text therefore resolve to one fragment, and a bundle emits `[Common](#style)` beside `[Agents](#style)`, sending two references to the same heading.

## Proposal

A whole-document reference to a merged member reaches a heading naming that member. Where the member's contributions are shared headings, the variant heading that names the document within the first of them is the destination, so two members contributing one heading text resolve to two headings.
