## Overview

The destination a whole-document reference to a merged member reaches, held to that member's own section.

## Problem

A member whose content all sits at the grouping depth resolves to its first contribution. Two members whose first contribution carries one heading text therefore resolve to one fragment, and a bundle emits `[Common](#style)` beside `[Agents](#style)`. A member named nowhere resolves to a heading that names a heading text rather than the document.

## Proposal

A whole-document reference to a merged member reaches the section that member carries. Each member has exactly one such section, so no two members resolve to one heading and no reference lands inside another member's content.
