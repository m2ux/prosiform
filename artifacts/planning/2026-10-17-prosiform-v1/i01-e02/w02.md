**Component:** [src/prosiform/grouping.py](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/main/src/prosiform/grouping.py)

## Overview

What each option gathers: sections selected by their heading name, and documents selected by the folder that holds them.

## Problem

Selection is made from a path and a depth, so a caller cannot ask for the sections a name identifies independently of where their documents sit, nor for a folder's documents independently of heading depth.

## Proposal

A section group gathers the sections carrying the named heading from every participating document beneath one generated section of that name, wherever in the tree those documents sit. A folder group gathers the contributing documents under a matching folder beneath one generated section of that name, each a subsection titled from its filename stem.
