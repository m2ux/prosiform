**Component:** [src/prosiform/grouping.py](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/main/src/prosiform/grouping.py)

## Overview

The overview section of a merged group, emitted only where a member's above-depth content would otherwise sit directly beneath the group's own heading.

## Problem

The section is emitted whenever a group is merged. Two heading-free members under `--group rules:1` are each given a subsection named from their stem, and those subsections are then wrapped in a section that names nothing the arrangement had not already named. Changing the grouping depth does not reach the shorter arrangement.

## Proposal

The arrangement asks whether any above-depth contribution would sit beneath the group heading with no heading of its own. Where one would, the overview section carries them as it does now. Where none would, the section is not emitted and the member subsections take its place beneath the group heading, one level higher than they sit today.

The member a group emits nothing else from keeps the heading that names it under either arrangement, so the guarantee that a group names every member it merges survives the wrapper's absence.
