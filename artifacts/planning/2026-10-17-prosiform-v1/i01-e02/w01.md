**Component:** [src/prosiform/grouping.py](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/main/src/prosiform/grouping.py)

## Overview

One section for each member a merged group carries, titled from that member and holding that member's content.

## Problem

The arrangement is keyed on heading text. Two documents each carrying `Style` and one heading of their own emit a shared `Style` section holding both, with the unshared headings as loose siblings, so neither document is readable as a document. Content above the grouping depth is wrapped in an `Overview` section whether or not its contributions already carry a heading, and a document whose only identity is its own H1 has that heading absorbed and discarded.

## Proposal

The group emits one section for each member, titled from the member's filename stem, holding that member's content with its own headings beneath it. Nothing is keyed on heading text, so no member is split, no variant heading is generated to tell contributors apart, and the overview wrapper has nothing left to carry.

Where a member's leading heading carries the member's own name, at whatever level it sits, that heading is the member's section heading rather than a second heading beneath a generated one. Two members whose stems agree take the shortest run of their root-relative paths that distinguishes them, after the leading segments every member shares are dropped, which is the rule variant headings followed.
