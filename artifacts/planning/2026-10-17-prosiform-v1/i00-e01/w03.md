## Overview

Continuous integration that runs every corpus fixture on each change.

## Problem

A corpus that runs only when someone remembers reports a regression after the change that caused it.

The checks run the runner against fixtures they write themselves, and no fixture of the corpus is executed. Every expectation the corpus declares therefore stands unverified, and the suite is green.

## Proposal

A continuous-integration job runs the whole corpus against the build under test on every change, and fails the change when any fixture fails. The job reports the failing fixtures by identifier.

The build under test comes from configuration, and the job treats its absence as a failure rather than as a reason to skip. A skipped corpus run sits in a passing suite saying nothing, which is the state this row exists to end. This row owns that choice because it is the one holding the job, and no check runs the corpus before it.

## Work Breakdown

| Part | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Analysis | The runner's invocation and exit behaviour as a job step, and what the job does when no build is configured |
| Plan | The job definition, its triggers, its reporting and its failure on absent configuration |
| Implementation | The job and its wiring to the runner |
| Review | The job confirmed to fail the change on a failing fixture, and on a build it cannot find |
| Test | A deliberately failing fixture fails the job, the passing corpus does not, and an unconfigured build fails the job rather than skipping it |
