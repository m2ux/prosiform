## Overview

The guarantee that an ancestor's destination never stands in for selecting its section, and the fixtures that would catch a change making it one.

## Problem

A structural ancestor is scaffolding: a heading emitted to keep a selected section in the outline that placed it, carrying no body. A reference naming that heading must therefore reach the section's content, not the scaffolding that happens to carry its name.

- **The two are indistinguishable by name.**
  An ancestor is emitted from the source heading it stands for, so it answers to that heading's fragment. Nothing in the destination tells a reader whether what it reaches is a section or a placeholder for one.

- **A guarantee nothing measures is an assumption.**
  [I00:E09:W02](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/pull/114) delivered ancestors and the behaviour holds today, but it holds as a consequence of where ancestry sits in the pipeline rather than as a rule anything states. A later change to that ordering would break it silently, because no fixture asserts it.

## Proposal

The behaviour is already correct and this row measures it rather than building it. Dependency closure expands a marked reference into its target's whole section before any group is assembled, so a heading a reference names is selected content by the time ancestry runs, and ancestry derives ancestors from the selection as closure finally left it. An ancestor is therefore only ever a heading **no reference named**.

That ordering is the guarantee. The row states it where a reader will find it and holds it with fixtures that fail if the order changes: a reference reaching a section that would otherwise have been an ancestor, beside an ancestor nothing references, in one bundle.

`candidate.answers()` is **not** what decides this. It refuses a `Generated` part that is not the filename wrapper, but an `Ancestor` is neither `Carried` nor `Generated`, so it reaches the clause that matches on origin fragment and would answer a target naming it. The guarantee rests on closure promotion alone, and a fixture is the only thing that will notice if that stops being true.

## Why This Row Is Small

AC9 arrived from the dependency closure epic, which could not deliver it because heading-only ancestors did not exist. W02 then delivered ancestors and the promotion fell out of the pipeline order rather than needing a mechanism, which the fixture `ancestor-promotion/a-link-to-a-heading-only-ancestor` already shows: `Alpha` carries its body because a marked link names it, and `Beta` stays heading-only because nothing does.

What is missing is the statement and the regression evidence. The row is kept rather than folded into W02 so that the criterion is measured by a fixture written to measure it, not by one that happens to exercise it.
