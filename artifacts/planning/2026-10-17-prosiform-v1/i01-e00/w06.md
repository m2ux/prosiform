## Overview

Gives each ledger entry a resolution state, so the freeze can read whether an entry is open, resolved or deferred.

## Problem

The entry schema declares seven fields and admits no others, and none of them states whether an entry is resolved or deferred. The freeze is refused while an entry is neither, so with no field to read, no entry can be judged against it.

## Proposal

A reader opens a ledger entry and finds one resolution state among open, resolved and deferred, and, for a deferral, what is deferred and why. The schema check refuses an entry with no state, with a state outside the three, or with a deferral carrying no reason.
