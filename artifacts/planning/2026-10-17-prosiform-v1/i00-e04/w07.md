## Overview

Promotion of a heading-only structural ancestor into the section a reference names.

## Problem

A link whose target is present only as a heading-only *structural ancestor* must expand closure to that section's selected body, replacing the ancestor rather than leaving a displayed title standing in for it. The state the rule turns on — a section present as a heading and nothing else — is produced by [E09:W02](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/issues/60), so no run before that row can reach it and no run can demonstrate the promotion.

## Proposal

Once a group emits structural ancestors, a marked reference naming one of them selects that section normally: the ancestor's heading is replaced by the transformed source heading and the section's selected body, emitted once, and an ancestor no reference names keeps its heading and none of its body. Closure expansion to a section the run has not selected already holds; what this row adds is the replacement and the evidence for it.

## The Fixture Waiting On This Row

[W04](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/pull/81) measured the criterion rather than planning around it: `ancestor` appears nowhere in `src/`, `checks/` or `fixtures/`, and selecting a subsection alone emits its own heading and body and no ancestor heading. The half of the criterion that does hold was measured there — a marked link to a section the run has not selected expands closure to that section's body, a fragment-only link from a descendant back to its own ancestor section included.

The fixture W04 authored declares the whole criterion and differs from the current build's output by exactly one heading: the heading-only ancestor of the subsection no reference names. It is the instrument AC10 names, and lifting its marker is this row's completion.
