## Overview

A merged group arranges several documents under one heading, and two of the arrangement's own structures stand between a reader and the content: a wrapper section that is emitted whether or not anything needs it, and a destination rule that sends two members to one heading. This epic settles both against what a run of the tool shows.

## Problem

The arrangement emits structure the content does not call for, and resolves two members to one place.

- **The wrapper is unconditional.**
  `--group rules:1` over two heading-free members emits the group heading, an `Overview` beneath it, and the members beneath that. The members already carry a heading apiece, so the wrapper names nothing the arrangement had not already named.

- **Depth is no lever on it.**
  The same sources under `--group rules:2` produce byte-identical output, so a caller cannot reach the shorter arrangement by choosing a different grouping depth.

- **Two members can share one destination.**
  Where every member's content sits at the grouping depth, a whole-document reference resolves to that member's first contribution. Two members whose first contribution carries one heading text therefore resolve to one fragment, and a bundle carrying `[Common](#style)` beside `[Agents](#style)` sends both references to the same heading.

## Proposal

- **The wrapper answers a need or is absent.**
  A merged group carries the overview section only where a member's content above the grouping depth would otherwise sit directly beneath the group's own heading. Where every such contribution already has a heading of its own, the section is not emitted and those headings take its place beneath the group heading.

- **Every member the group merges stays named.**
  A member the arrangement emits nothing else from keeps the heading that names it. That heading moves up with the rest rather than leaving with the wrapper, so the group continues to name every member it merges.

- **A member resolves to a heading it does not share.**
  A whole-document reference to a merged member reaches a heading that names that member, so two members contributing one heading text resolve to two destinations.
