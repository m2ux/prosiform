## Overview

A merged group arranges part of the source tree by heading rather than by file, so documents under one folder contribute their sections to a shared outline. That arrangement holds. What this epic settles is what the arrangement emits around it: a wrapper section carried whether or not anything needs it, a member the group names nowhere, two members reaching one destination, and a generated heading spelled one way inside a group and another outside it.

## Problem

The arrangement is sound; the structures it generates around the merged sections are not.

- **The wrapper is emitted unconditionally.**
  `--group rules:1` over two members carrying no headings emits the group heading, an `Overview` beneath it, and a subsection for each member beneath that. Each member already has a heading of its own, so the wrapper names nothing the arrangement had not named, and no grouping depth reaches the shorter arrangement: `rules:1` and `rules:2` emit identical output.

- **A member can be named nowhere.**
  A document whose only identity is its own H1 has that heading absorbed into a generated one that is then discarded, so the group carries its content with nothing naming the document it came from.

- **Two members can reach one destination.**
  A member whose content all sits at the grouping depth resolves to its first contribution. Two members whose first contribution carries one heading text therefore resolve to one fragment, and a bundle emits `[Common](#style)` beside `[Agents](#style)`.

- **Generated headings carry two spellings.**
  A group heading is title-cased and a document group heading is not, so one bundle emits `# Rules` beside `# procedure` and `# rules/codex`. The rendered diagrams reproduce the inconsistency.

## Proposal

- **The wrapper answers a need or is absent.**
  The overview section is emitted only where a member's content above the grouping depth would otherwise sit directly beneath the group's own heading. Where every such contribution already carries a heading, the section is not emitted and those headings take its place beneath the group heading.

- **Every member the group merges is named.**
  A member the arrangement would otherwise emit nothing naming keeps a heading that names it, whether or not the overview section is emitted, so the guarantee survives the wrapper's absence.

- **A member reaches a destination it does not share.**
  A whole-document reference to a merged member resolves to a heading naming that member, so two members contributing one heading text resolve to two destinations.

- **One convention covers every generated heading.**
  The title casing a group heading already takes reaches the document group headings and every other heading the compiler writes, while a heading an author wrote and a filesystem path a run names keep the spelling their source gave them.

- **A member heading names one document.**
  The heading naming a member carries the shortest run of that document's path which tells it from the other members, as a variant heading does, so two members of one stem in different directories are told apart by the bundle rather than by a renderer's ordinal.
