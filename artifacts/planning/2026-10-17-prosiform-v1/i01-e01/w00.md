**Component:** src/prosiform/bundle.py, src/tests, fixtures

## Overview

The bundle a run emits is what the design states, the corpus expects and the build produces. This row brings those three back into agreement after the emission changed without the design or the corpus following it.

## Problem

The opening line and the first entry document's placement were changed in the build alone. The design states neither, so the change carries no contract; the tests and the corpus state the emission that preceded it, so they refuse what the build now produces.

- `make check` fails at the head of `main`: the unit suite reports 41 failures and the corpus reports 186 fixtures failing, all on the text a bundle opens with.
- The design's clean-Markdown and entry-line-integrity clauses constrain the entry line's links and its commentary without stating the sentence around them, and no clause states that the first entry document's title, preamble and section order lead the bundle.
- The corpus expectations were left at the previous wording, so the fixtures state an emission the design never had and the build no longer produces.

## Proposal

The design states the emission, the corpus is re-pinned to it, and the build is left as it stands because it already produces it.

- **The design states what the entry line says.**
  The clause naming the entry line's purpose states the sentence it carries, for one entry reference and for several, so the wording is a contract rather than a build detail.

- **The design states the first entry document's place.**
  The clause governing whole-document inclusion states that the first entry document leads the bundle: its title and preamble at the levels the author wrote, its own sections in its own order, and every section the merge adds after them.

- **The corpus follows the design.**
  Each fixture's expected output is re-pinned to the bundle the stated emission produces. No fixture expectation is edited to match an observed result the design does not state.

- **The checks pass.**
  `make check` passes at the row's head, so every later row in this epic is measured against a green repository.
