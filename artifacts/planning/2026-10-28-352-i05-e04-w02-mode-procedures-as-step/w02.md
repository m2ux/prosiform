**Component:** example/work-planner/modes

## Overview

Every mode procedure writes its steps as sub-sections, so a dispatching session, a guideline or a reviewer can link the one step it means.

## Problem

- **A mode step roots no anchor.**
  The ten modes hold 86 numbered steps across 12 procedures; [`deliver-mode.md`](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/c388f7149e412e0a3d518ecae52bba306b5bcfbc/example/work-planner/modes/deliver-mode.md#L11-L131) alone holds 22, beside two `###` sub-procedures whose own steps are numbered too. A link can name a procedure but not one of its steps.
- **The rule the corpus now states is unmet.**
  W01's Step sub-sections rule in [`guidelines.md`](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/c388f7149e412e0a3d518ecae52bba306b5bcfbc/example/work-planner/guidelines.md#L33-L40) requires the sub-section form the modes do not yet take.

## Proposal

`plan-mode.md`'s `6. **Publish.**` becomes `### 6. Publish` under `## Procedure`, its instruction and bullets beneath.

- **Labels become headings.**
  Each bold step label becomes its sub-section's action, after the step's number, in title case.
- **Sub-procedures.**
  A `###` sub-procedure such as Run Delivery Session follows the steps, and its own steps become `####` sub-sections.
- **Action titles.**
  A step heading is not a procedure title, so it keeps the articles its label reads with: `#### 1. Read the Brief`. Action titles in `guidelines.md` states this.
- **The worked example.**
  `example/README.md`'s excerpts of plan mode's publish and record steps and revise mode's first step are the converted text.
- **Fixtures.**
  Each usage-example fixture whose entry reaches a mode takes the expected bundle the converted corpus compiles to.
