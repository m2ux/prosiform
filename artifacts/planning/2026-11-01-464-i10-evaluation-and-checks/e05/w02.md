## Overview

The eval page names the feature workflows and states what the proof run proves and what it cost.

## Problem

- **The proof file is unexplained without the page.**
  A reader finds a run record beside the baseline and no statement of what it shows.
- **Its cost is a fact a reader plans by.**
  A rerun spends the same budget.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The page states the forms, the scenarios and the scoring, with tables per workflow family.

**The design.**

- **A table of the feature workflows.**
  Each workflow and the permutations its scenarios cover.
- **One line on the proof.**
  What `mvp-proof.json` proves and its measured cost, read from the file.
