## Overview

The refusal of a dispatch in a heading the bundle emits as text alone, and of a dispatch with no text after its mark, states the design's reason: the bundle has no link to publish. No message, docstring or fixture manifest names a dispatch sub-section.

## Problem

- **The refusals name a section the bundle no longer generates.**
  The integration review of [#648](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/pull/648) found the CLI refusing a dispatch in an absorbed title because "no section a dispatch sub-section can stand beneath", the remedy naming "where a dispatch sub-section has a section to follow", and a dispatch with no text because "no sub-section heading can name it". The `Unplaced` and `placing_in` docstrings in `bundle.py`, the `assembling` and `dispatches` docstrings in `cli.py`, `composition.py`'s module docstring, `Link.served` in `dispatch.py` and the absorbed-member fixture's description said the same.

## Proposal

- **One reason, stated as the design states it.**
  A dispatch in a heading emitted as text alone has no link to publish; a dispatch with no text names nothing for its reader. The remedy is to write the dispatch in the body beneath the heading, as `docs/dispatch.md` says.
- **The fixtures observe the wording.**
  The two title fixtures expect `emitted as text alone` in the diagnostic.
