## Overview

The documentation states the guide as the home of the dispatch rules and every bundle's run-pf line, and no page describes the Prosiform section as current.

## Problem

- **The pages describe the retired section.**
  `docs/dispatch.md` has a *The Prosiform section* page section and a figure of it; `README.md`, `docs/glossary.md`, `docs/composition.md`, `docs/destinations.md`, `docs/architecture.md` and `fixtures/README.md` state it, and the README and glossary state the guide's old contents.

## Proposal

**The scene.** `docs/dispatch.md` owns the dispatch documentation; the other pages link it.

- **The dispatch page.**
  It states the run-pf line, the dispatch lists a bundle carries, and that the guide states the protocols and the returned-question marker; its figure follows.
- **One home, others link.**
  The README, glossary and the other pages lose every description of the Prosiform section and link the dispatch page or the guide; the README and glossary's guide entries state its new contents.
