## Overview

`¬⇉` and `¬⫽` retire: a link opening with either is refused, so dispatch has one form, compiled up front.

## Problem

- **Deferred dispatch is a second way to dispatch.**
  `closure.py` admits `¬⇉` and `¬⫽` as one compilation on two counts, published with their marks removed, so a reader cannot tell one from a citation and a deferred set member falls out of its set.

## Proposal

**The scene.** `closure.py` reads operator marks, including the combined `¬⇉` and `¬⫽`, and the refusals of other combinations.

- **The combined marks are refused.** (User, 2026-11-03.)
  A link opening with `¬⇉` or `¬⫽` fails with `E_UNSUPPORTED_CONSTRUCT`, naming the link, and the run publishes nothing. `¬→` and `[¬ x](x.md)` are unchanged.
- **Code that served them goes.**
  The deferred-dispatch branches in `closure.py`, `dispatch.py` and `candidate.py` are removed, and the deferred-dispatch fixtures become refusals.
