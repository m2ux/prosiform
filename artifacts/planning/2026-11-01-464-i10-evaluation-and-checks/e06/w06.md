## Overview

The documentation states the guide as the home of the dispatch rules, every bundle's run-pf line and the `Dispatch` and `In parallel` sections, and no page describes the Prosiform section or deferred dispatch as current.

## Problem

- **The pages describe the retired forms.**
  `docs/dispatch.md` has a *The Prosiform section* page section and a figure of it; `README.md`, `docs/glossary.md`, `docs/composition.md`, `docs/destinations.md`, `docs/architecture.md` and `fixtures/README.md` state it or `¬⇉`, and the README and glossary state the guide's old contents.

## Proposal

**The scene.** `docs/dispatch.md` owns the dispatch documentation; the other pages link it.

- **The dispatch page.**
  It states the run-pf line, the `Dispatch` and `In parallel` sections and the files they link, a set of one, the refusal of `¬⇉` and `¬⫽`, and that the guide holds the rules and the returned-question marker; its figures follow.
- **One home, others link.**
  The README, glossary and the other pages lose every description of the Prosiform section and deferred dispatch and link the dispatch page or the guide; the README and glossary's guide entries state its new contents.
