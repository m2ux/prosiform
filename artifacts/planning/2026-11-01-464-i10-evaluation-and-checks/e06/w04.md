## Overview

A deferred link is published in the form W00 settles, so an agent reading a bundle can tell a deferred link from a citation and apply the guide's instruction for its mark.

## Problem

- **A deferred link reads as a citation.**
  `[¬ text](path)` and `[¬⇉ text](path)` publish as `[text](path)`, the same shape as a link to a document that declares nothing, so the bundle gives its reader no sign that the target is compiled.

## Proposal

**The scene.** `rewrite.py` publishes a deferred link at its rebased path with its marks and the blanks after them removed.

- **The marks are published.**
  Under the recommended answer to the epic's open question, a deferred link keeps the marks its source wrote, `¬`, `¬⇉` or `¬⫽`, followed by one space. Should the user choose otherwise, this row takes the published form chosen.
- **Fixtures follow.**
  Every deferral and deferred-dispatch fixture's expected bundle carries the published form.
