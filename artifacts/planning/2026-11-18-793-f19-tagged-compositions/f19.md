## Overview

A section composed twice in one bundle, once with title tags selecting a preset that routes its links and once without, publishes both contributions under the tagged preset's routes. Each contribution, and each dispatch brief, should resolve under the presets its own directive or dispatch selects, layered over the document's `default`, while a document's own group keeps the union.

## Problem

- **One copy follows the other's routes.**
  The `subst` pairs in force for a document are the union of the presets every reference reaching it selects, and a composed copy's links resolve under that union, whatever the directive placing the copy selects.
- **A brief reaches it easily.**
  A brief is compiled as a composition under its dispatch's presets, so a target dispatched with and without a routing preset carries the tagged routes in every brief copy.

## Proposal

- **Each copy resolves under its own presets.**
  The mapping holds, beside each document's group pairs, the pairs for each composed copy, keyed by the document and the composing reference's tags: the document's `default` with the presets those tags select above it. A composed range records those tags, and every stage reading its links — traversal, placement, composition, dispatch placement, candidate validation and the run's own dispatch reading — resolves them under its copy's pairs.
- **The group keeps the union.**
  A document's own group resolves under the presets every reference reaching it selects, the composing ones included, and two references whose presets map one key to two substitutes are still refused.
- **Copies routed apart place their own dispatches.**
  A later copy of a composed region shares its first copy's or the group's dispatch places only where its dispatched links name the same targets; one its presets route elsewhere places its own.
