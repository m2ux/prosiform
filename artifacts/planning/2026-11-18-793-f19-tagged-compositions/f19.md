## Overview

A section composed twice in one bundle, once with title tags selecting a preset that routes its links and once without, publishes both contributions under the tagged preset's routes. Each contribution, and each dispatch brief, should resolve under the presets its own directive or dispatch selects, layered over the document's `default`, while a document's own group resolves under its including references' presets.

## Problem

- **One copy follows the other's routes.**
  The `subst` pairs in force for a document are the union of the presets every reference reaching it selects, and a composed copy's links resolve under that union, whatever the directive placing the copy selects.
- **A brief reaches it easily.**
  A brief is compiled as a composition under its dispatch's presets, so a target dispatched with and without a routing preset carries the tagged routes in every brief copy.

## Proposal

- **Each copy resolves under its own presets.**
  The mapping holds, beside each document's group pairs, the pairs for each composed copy, keyed by the document and the composing reference's tags: the document's `default` with the presets those tags select above it. A composed range records those tags, and every stage reading its links — traversal, placement, composition, dispatch placement and its loop walk, candidate validation and the run's own dispatch reading — resolves them under its copy's pairs.
- **The group takes its including references' presets.**
  A document's own group resolves under the presets its including references select. A composing directive's or dispatch's presets put nothing in force for it, so two copies whose presets map one key to two substitutes each publish; presets one tag list selects, and those of the including references reaching a group, are still refused when they clash.
- **Alternatives are counted per copy.**
  Exactly one alternative in force holds for the group across its including references and for each copy on its own, so two copies picking different alternatives publish and a copy needing one and picking none is refused. A `group` pair changes a group alone, so a copy needs no alternative that states only one.
- **Copies routed apart place their own dispatches.**
  A later copy of a composed region shares its first copy's or the group's dispatch places only where its dispatched links name the same targets; one its presets route elsewhere places its own. Two copies routing one briefed dispatch apart are refused, since the two sub-sections would share the heading the one link text gives.
- **Warnings follow the pair used.**
  A pair in force for a copy alone that no reference used warns as unused, and a reference taking an alternative's route stays quiet whatever stand-in another preset states for its key.
