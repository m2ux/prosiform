**Component:** fixtures

## Overview

The manifests the checks' own tests write are manifests the schema accepts, and the fixture families carry the names of the concepts their fixtures prove.

## Problem

- **Test manifests carry a retired field.**
  The conformance and independence tests write `stdout = "expected/stdout.md"`, which the manifest schema rejects since a bundle left standard output.
- **Two families are named for retired concepts.**
  `fixtures/run-set` holds parallel dispatch fixtures after the run-set shape was retired, and `fixtures/delivery` holds publication fixtures after the delivery wrapper was removed.

## Proposal

The scene: a reader listing `fixtures/` and a test writing a manifest.

- **Test manifests declare what the schema states.**
  The tests drop `stdout`, and a test validates each manifest they write against the schema.
- **Families renamed.**
  `fixtures/run-set` becomes `fixtures/parallel-dispatch`, and `fixtures/delivery` becomes `fixtures/publication`.
