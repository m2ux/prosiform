**Component:** main:src/prosiform/composition.py

## Overview

A directive names a section of a declaring document. A deferred directive keeps its place with one line naming the run that composes it. That run compiles the target as an entry. Operator symbols are written together.

## Problem

- **An ineligible target passed as an ordinary link.**
  A directive naming a whole document, a document that declares nothing, a remote address or a resource asked for a composition nothing could answer. The run carried it as text, or bundled the document.
- **A deferred directive kept no place.**
  `[¬ →]` was published as an ordinary deferral. No destination section or contribution heading recorded where a later run's content belongs.
- **The deferred form wrote a blank between two symbols.**
  The user requires operator symbols written together, with the one blank before the link's text. A deferred directive is `[¬→]` and a deferred dispatch `[¬⇉ text]`. `[¬ →]`, `[¬ ⇉ x]` and `[⇉ →]` are refused (user, 2026-11-02).

## Proposal

**The scene.** W01 to W05 recognise, compose, order, nest and bound immediate directives, and a deferral already confirms its target exists.

- **Symbols written together.**
  `opened()` in `closure.py` reads a link's operator marks. They are a run followed by a blank, or the deferral mark written against a qualifying label. `gapped()` finds a blank between two symbols, and `refusals_of_operators` refuses it. The design's *Operators that combine* states the rule; *Operator marks* and *The deferral mark* link it.
- **The eligible target.**
  `refusals_of_directives` refuses, under `E_UNSUPPORTED_CONSTRUCT`, a directive naming a remote address or a resource, a document that declares nothing, or a whole document. A missing document stays `E_TARGET_NOT_FOUND`. A deferred directive naming a silent or remote target meets the operator refusal first.
- **Deferred composition.**
  `Composing.directives` yields deferred directives beside immediate ones, and they share destination sections. `contribute` emits a deferred one's contribution heading and then one generated line, `[↳ <title>](<rebased path>)`. That line is recorded as an `Instruction` that `activates` the target. The directive's link points at the contribution heading with its mark dropped. A deferred directive closes no cycle.
- **Activation.**
  The line's destination is an entry reference, so the run it names composes the target's body there. Immediate directives compose; deferred ones keep lines of their own.
- **Validation.**
  Candidate validation holds the activation line's one link to its target.
