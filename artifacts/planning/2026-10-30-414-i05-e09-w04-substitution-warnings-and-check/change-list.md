## No change

- **The warning codes' names.** `W_MOCK_SUBSTITUTED` and `W_MOCK_UNUSED` are the user's decision recorded in the epic's record, and both the design and the schema carry them verbatim.
- **The emission point.** `publication` emits the run's notices once every budget, name and folder check has passed, beside each bundle's warnings, which is where `W_RESOURCE_PORTABILITY` already reaches the reporter. No second emission path was added for `-c`: publication is the stage `-c` skips, so both modes reach the same call.
- **The fixture names already landed.** `a-substitution-warns-with-its-target`, `an-unused-key-warns-and-publishes` and `a-mocked-check-publishes-nothing` are the names `fixtures.md` and the epic give.
- **The mapping's shape and the lookup.** The record is a field beside the keys, and neither the keys, the lookup nor its precedence changed. The one substitution per key follows from where the record is keyed rather than from a second lookup.
- **`-m`, `--mock` and its refusals.** W02 owns the three refusals and this row does not touch them; the rebase's conflict is resolved by keeping W02's shape check and passing this row's `spelled` map into it.
- **The independent row pairs.** `joins.md` states that W02 and W04 take separate pull requests, and this row is the second of that pair.
