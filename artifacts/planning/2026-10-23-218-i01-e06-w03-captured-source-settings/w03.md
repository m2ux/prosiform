**Component:** src/prosiform/source.py

## Overview

The source model carries validated conversion metadata from the same read that supplies the body.

## Problem

Source retains body text and its starting line but loses all frontmatter fields.

## Proposal

Extend Source and content to carry the decoder result and its provenance. Invoke decoding on the captured source, preserving the existing BOM and delimiter recognition and physical-line mapping. Keep hashes and raw-byte budgets over the full source. Translate decoder failures through the existing input-diagnostic boundary. Validate local metadata for normally opened deferred targets without adding them to the selected content. Update directly affected source fixtures in the same change.
