## Overview

Restatement writes a stale fixture's expected bundles from the build under test, so a change to bundle text or names is read as a diff. It is dry by default, and when written it changes the files under `expected/` and the manifest's `bundles` entry.

## Problem

A stale fixture is restated by hand, and a manifest re-rendered from parsed TOML changes more than its expectation.

- **Expected output is typed.**
  [The bundle comparison](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/blob/b0eb7a2335436f2ce9c35546d3bfd2e39f7ae3be/checks/runner.py#L284-L342) reports a difference and writes nothing.
- **A re-rendered manifest hides the line that changed.**
  Rendering a parsed manifest escapes its non-ASCII text, which is churn around the one entry a reviewer reads.
- **The earlier tools reach no documented place.**
  [Two scripts](https://github.com/m2ux/prosiform/tree/engineering/artifacts/planning/2026-10-24-corpus-drift-tools) sit in a planning record and are named nowhere the corpus maintains itself.

## Proposal

A change renames a bundle or moves a line of shared text, and the corpus run names every fixture that pinned it.

- **Restatement is dry by default.**
  Without `--write` each fixture it would rewrite is named and left alone.
- **When written, two things change.**
  The files under `expected/` and the manifest's `bundles` entry. Every other byte of the manifest is copied through.
- **The contract stays authored.**
  `exit_status`, `diagnostics`, `stdout_contains`, `clauses`, `description` and `awaiting` are never written.
- **The tool lives where the corpus maintains itself.**
  It joins `checks/`, imports the corpus's own runner and manifest reader, and is named in the checks guide and the fixture guide.
