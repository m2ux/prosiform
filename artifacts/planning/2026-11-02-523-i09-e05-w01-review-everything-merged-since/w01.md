## Overview

A whole-product review of everything merged into `main` since `contract/v0.15.0` against the pinned design, so every departure is found and fixed in this epic before the surface is documented and proven.

## Problem

- **Each epic reviewed its own slice.**
  No review has read the argument surface, placement and composition together against the design they implement.
- **A departure found later lands outside the MVP.**
  The proof run would measure a surface the design does not state.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The pinned design states the contract; the build on `main` implements it, epic by epic.

**The design.**

- **One record of every clause.**
  Each clause the design adds or changes since `contract/v0.15.0` is read against the merged code and its fixtures, and the record states whether it holds.
- **Every departure becomes a row here.**
  A finding is fixed in this epic, as a further row ahead of the documentation rows.
