## Overview

A root bounds what a source may include and what pf may open. Headings, order, member names and output names come from the document's title and its path relative to the working directory.

## Problem

- **The root named the file heading.**
  `# Modes/Understand-Mode` headed a file titled `# Understand Mode`; the title was absorbed only when its slug matched the path's last segment.

- **The root named the output and the order.**
  The derived output name, source and group order, and member names read the path from the effective root, so the same entry produced different names under different roots.

- **Coinciding root-relative headings were refused.**
  Two documents whose root-relative paths coincided (by suffix, case, or distinct roots) failed `E_AMBIGUOUS_FILE_GROUP`.

## Proposal

- **Head by title.** `document_title` (first heading, the only H1, at document level) heads the group and is absorbed where emitted; an untitled document takes `heading_text(path)` from its working-directory path.
- **Coinciding headings take ordinals.** The file-heading refusal goes; the member-name refusal stays.
- **Name and order by working-directory path.** `derived_output`, sorting, `member_headings` read the path; `Source.display` and `Source.identity` are removed.
- **Update the design** (`9d6d9731`) and repin `README.md`.
