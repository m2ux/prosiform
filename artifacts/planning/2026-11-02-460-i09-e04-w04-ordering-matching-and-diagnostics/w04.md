**Component:** main:src/prosiform/composition.py

## Overview

Composition orders its sections and matches its titles one way, so a bundle is reproducible from its sources, and each structural failure is a diagnostic naming the directive where it is written.

## Problem

- **Order and matching are unpinned.**
  W02 orders destination sections by first directive and keys them by title string, but no fixture shows a reordered reference, a title differing only by Unicode form or case, or two parents carrying one title.
- **Some structural failures are silent or misplaced.**
  A directive in a title its file heading absorbs, or in a title standing above the selection, is flattened to text and composes nothing. One naming a missing document is published as a link. An authored child heading equal to a destination section gives the scope two sections of one name. A composed heading past H6 names the target document rather than the directive.

## Proposal

**The scene.** W02 composes each immediate directive beneath its scope; W03 keeps each contribution its source's content. W01 refuses a directive in a preamble, and W02 one whose target has no parent heading.

- **Ordering and matching.**
  Keep W02's first-reference order and exact title keys; state them in the composition module and pin them with reordered-reference, Unicode-title and same-name-parent fixtures.
- **A heading emitted as text alone.**
  The check that refuses a dispatch in an unselected ancestor or a title above the selection also reads a directive there. The bundle's rewrite refuses a heading's immediate directive naming a bundled section that the composition placed no contribution for, which is a title its file heading or subsection absorbs. Both raise `E_UNSUPPORTED_CONSTRUCT` naming the link.
- **A missing source.**
  The directive refusal pass refuses a directive naming a Markdown path that holds no document with `E_TARGET_NOT_FOUND`; a missing section already fails there.
- **A conflicting child heading.**
  Each group's composition refuses an authored child heading of the scope equal to a destination section with `E_HEADING_COLLISION`, naming the directive and the authored heading's file line.
- **Depth.**
  A composed heading past H6 fails with `E_HEADING_DEPTH_OVERFLOW`, naming the directive, its location and its chain.
- **Recognition home.**
  The directive predicate moves to the closure module beside the operators, so the bundle can read a directive without importing composition.
- **Debt.**
  The dispatch heading-collision diagnostic located the authored heading at its content line; it now names the file line.
