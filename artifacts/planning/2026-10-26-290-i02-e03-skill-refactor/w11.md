## Overview

Recording an epic's delivery and routing its findings is one protocol. Closing an epic once its bases merge is one protocol. Issue Closure carries no kind branching of its own. Issue Delivery records through the operations that own each change. Task Merge starts at the merge and closes through Epic Closure.

## Problem

- Sync and Align record delivery inline (sync-mode.md:42-104; align-mode.md:216-244), and Sync applies operations Issue Delivery owns.
- Sync's close step (sync-mode.md:105-112) and Task Merge step 4 repeat the same sequence.
- Issue Closure branches by kind and states a fresh-file rule (steps 2-3).
- Issue Delivery uses Populate Epic Issue Body for an unplaced task, restates the Disagreement rule and pairs verify and tick by hand (steps 1-4).
- Deliver applies Merge Task Pull Requests before every Task Merge (deliver-mode.md:44-46, 118-121); Task Merge refreshes evidence with no operation and repeats Epic Closure's sequence (steps 1, 4).

## Proposal

- Delivery Recording reads delivery evidence, matches, follows Issue Delivery for each merged match, records the epic and routes findings, taking the caller's allowance.
- Epic Closure follows Review Pull Request and Epic Integration, applies Read Delivery Evidence and Record Epic Delivery, follows Issue Closure, then applies Record Initiative Delivery and Record Verified Criteria.
- Its steps apply Check Issue Completion and Close Tracker Issue.
- Its steps apply Place Unplaced Task Row and Record Verified Criteria and link Delivery Findings.
- Task Merge applies Merge Task Pull Requests and Update Task Branch, applies Read Delivery Evidence, and follows Epic Closure.
