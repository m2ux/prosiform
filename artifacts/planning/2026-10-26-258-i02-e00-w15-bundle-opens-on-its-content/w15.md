## Overview

A bundle opens on its first group's content. A source that wants the agent to follow a section says so in its own preamble, as the work-planner modes do.

## Problem

- **The opening line was generated, not authored.**
  Every bundle began with "Follow [X](#x). The other sections are material that procedure references.", a fixed wording a source could not change per bundle.

- **The dispatch key existed only to extend that line.**
  `prosiform.dispatch` was text appended after the entry sentence; with no sentence it has no place.

## Proposal

- **Remove the emission.**
  `assemble(groups)` joins the groups' parts; `entry_line`, `is_entry_line`, `entries_named`, `Group.entries`, `Group.entry_at`, `preamble_end`, `entry_slot`, `Selection.named`/`document` and `Member.targets` go.
- **Remove the dispatch key.**
  `metadata.py` admits `root`, `group`, `group-depth`, `group-heading`; `dispatch` fails as an unknown key.
- **Remove what read the line.**
  The generated-input policy (`E_GENERATED_INPUT`) detected the entry declaration and nothing else; candidate validation's entry line integrity held the line to the entries.
- **Update the design** (`f2ce75c7`) and repin `README.md`.
