## Overview

Bound materialization by active-path recursion and explicit resource accounting.

## Problem

Distinct-target deduplication cannot bound repeated materialization or distinguish an active inline cycle.

## Proposal

Expand through occurrence identities while retaining cached source reads. Reject active inline-path cycles with the complete source chain; permit independent repeats and ordinary dependency cycles. Charge the design-defined occurrence budget before allocation, depth on the active path and output bytes including generated headings before publication. Include nested and multiplicative expansion in those counters.
