**Component:** src/prosiform

## Overview

Reject inline targets and placements the declared composition model cannot represent.

## Problem

Ordinary link traversal permits external mentions and has no inline structural-context checks.

## Proposal

Validate reserved links before ordinary external/resource bypasses. Require an eligible in-root Markdown section and its immediate parent. Validate containing heading, exact authored-child collision policy and final heading depth on the composed section model. Preserve source locations and the occurrence chain in diagnostics, including failures inside nested contributions.
