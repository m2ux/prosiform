**Component:** src/prosiform

## Overview

Activate selected occurrences into their retained scopes on a later compilation.

## Problem

Compiling a deferred target alone produces an independent bundle and cannot restore its original placement.

## Proposal

Implement the design-defined activation selection over the original source compilation recipe. Validate selected occurrence identity and its source context; diagnose missing or changed identities. Compose selected bodies into the retained exact-title destination and run structural, dependency, cycle and budget checks there. Leave nested deferred occurrences unactivated unless explicitly selected. Generated bundles remain ineligible as source input.
