**Component:** src/prosiform

## Overview

Compose source fragments under their containing sections in deterministic order.

## Problem

The renderer arranges source targets into document groups without call-site placement.

## Proposal

Extend the bundle section model with destination scope and ordered contributions. Insert exact-title child sections after own content and before authored children. Preserve the selected fragment under its immediate source-parent title with relative heading depth and block structure. Keep source identity separate from displayed parent titles; never title-case or normalize authored titles. Use occurrence-qualified renderer identities for repeated same-name contributions.
