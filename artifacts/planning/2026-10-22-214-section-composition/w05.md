**Component:** src/prosiform

## Overview

Preserve every requested occurrence and each fragment link binding.

## Problem

Traversal deduplicates source targets and rendering assumes source-level inclusion.

## Proposal

Separate source caching and ordinary dependency closure from materialized occurrences. Rewrite links and reference definitions using each fragment source, disambiguate definition names and final anchors, and preserve source attribution. An inline-only reach creates no separate document group; an ordinary reach still creates its normal inclusion. Revalidate all final destinations after composition and rewriting.
