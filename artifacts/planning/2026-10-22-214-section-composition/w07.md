**Component:** src/prosiform

## Overview

Carry deferred occurrence identity without expanding its body.

## Problem

Deferred target records deduplicate destinations and retain no containing-section identity.

## Proposal

Use the declared source-occurrence and ancestry identity for every deferred inline directive. Preserve source context and original destination scope through composition and link rewriting. Apply current destination-existence checks without validating deferred body structure or traversing its dependencies. Ordinary deferred implementation links remain absent from materialized contracts.
