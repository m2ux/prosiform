**Component:** main:src/prosiform/composition.py

## Overview

Composed content keeps its own source. A contribution's links and reference definitions resolve against the document that wrote them, and every directive contributes, a repeated one and one beside an ordinary link to the same section included. The fixtures pin both, and the composition module states them.

## Problem

- **Source meaning is unpinned.**
  A contribution carries regions of another document into the writer's section, where a relative link, a fragment or a reference-style use would read as the writer's if it were resolved against the writer. Nothing in the corpus shows the contribution resolving against its source.
- **Repetition is unpinned.**
  A source read once could be thought composed once. Nothing shows a repeated directive, or a directive beside an ordinary link to the same section, each keeping its own meaning.

## Proposal

**The scene.** W02 composes each immediate directive's target beneath its scope and rewrites each composed region through the destination map, against the document the region comes from.

- **Source-relative contributions.**
  The composition module states that a contribution's links resolve from its source's directory, its fragments against its source's headings and its reference-style uses against its source's definitions, whatever the writer defines. A diagnostic raised inside a contribution names its source and line.
- **Every occurrence contributes.**
  The module states that each directive contributes in the scope that writes it, a repeated one and one naming a section an ordinary link also names included.
- **Fixtures.**
  Cross-directory, colliding-definition and diagnostic fixtures observe the first; repeated-reference and mixed ordinary and composing fixtures observe the second.
