**Component:** main:Makefile

## Overview

The example mock scenarios, `checks/mock_scenarios.py` and the `make mock-scenarios` target are removed with `-m`.

## Problem

- **Each scenario passes `-m`.** The scenarios fail once the option is gone.
- **Each scenario bundles a copy of the example tree.** The tree is unmaintained and I08:E08 removes it, so converting the scenarios would maintain what is about to be deleted.

## Proposal

**The scene.** `make check` names `mock-scenarios`, and `checks.md` and `checks/README.md` describe it.

**The design.**

- **The scenarios go with the option.** The script, its tests, the target, its entry in `make check` and every description are removed in the unit that retires `-m`, so `make check` passes at every merge.
- **Substitution keeps its corpus.** The `fixtures/mock/` fixtures carry `subst`'s behaviour.
- **Retired, not refused.** No replacement check is added.
