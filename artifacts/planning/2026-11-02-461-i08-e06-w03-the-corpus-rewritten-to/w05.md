**Component:** main:docs/sources.md

## Overview

The source page states a preset's `subst` as the only channel, so a link written under no `subst` is compiled as authored, and the corpus holds that as a fixture.

## Problem

- **The absence is stated nowhere.** With the option gone, a reference under a preset stating no `subst` substitutes nothing, which an author meets at the page rather than infers.
- **No fixture witnesses it.** A run compiling a link as authored beside a would-be stand-in has no corpus entry of its own.

## Proposal

**The scene.** `docs/sources.md` states the preset keys and their grammar; `invocation.md`'s bundling paragraph points to it.

**The design.**

- **The source page states the channel once.** Its `subst` paragraph states that a preset is the only channel, so a link under no `subst` is compiled as authored.
- **The bundling paragraph links rather than restates.** It states that a substitution is written in a document, that a pair reaches its declaring document's links and resolves from that file, and links Sources and the substitution mapping.
- **The fixture is `fixtures/inclusion/no-preset-substitutes-nothing`.** `guide.md` links `rules.md` under a `default` stating no `subst`, with `stand-in.md` beside it; the bundle carries `rules.md` and the run reports nothing.
