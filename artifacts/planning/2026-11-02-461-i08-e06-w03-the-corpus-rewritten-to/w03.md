**Component:** main:fixtures/mock/a-mocked-dependency-is-compiled/manifest.toml

## Overview

Every `fixtures/mock/` fixture whose behaviour survives `-m` states its pair in the entry document's `default` preset, with its expected result unchanged, so the corpus shows the mechanism moved rather than the behaviour.

## Problem

- **The corpus passes the option.** Seventeen fixtures under `fixtures/mock/` pass `-m ORIGINAL=ALTERNATIVE` in `arguments`, and they are the mapping's main evidence.
- **Some state a retired shape.** The entry redirect fixture states a capability W07 retires, and the duplicate-key, self-pair and fragment-only-key refusals are command-line shapes whose preset equivalents already sit under `fixtures/inclusion/`.
- **A changed expected result would mean the substitution moved.** Only where the pair is written should change.

## Proposal

**The scene.** A fixture declares a `source/` tree, an `invocation` and the bundle and diagnostics the run must produce.

**The design.**

- **Each pair moves into the entry document's `default`.** `default` is always in force, and every surviving fixture's link is written by its entry document, `guide.md`.
- **The declaration keeps every line where it was.** The pair is written as a flow mapping on the frontmatter line that held `prosiform: {}`, so each `W_SUBST_USED` location is unchanged.
- **The sides read the same.** Every fixture runs from `source/`, which is the entry document's directory, so each side's text is unchanged.
- **A retired shape is withdrawn, not refused.** The entry redirect and the three command-line pair-shape refusals are removed; the non-Markdown alternative refusal, which has no preset witness, moves to its preset form.
