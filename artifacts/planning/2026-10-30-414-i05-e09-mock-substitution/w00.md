**Component:** invocation.md

## Overview

The contract's statement of the mapping, and the pin that lets a fixture name it. Every later row lands its fixtures in the pull request that makes them pass, so the clause they cite and the pinned revision carrying it must exist before the first of them.

## Problem

`checks/conformance.py` reads the design at the revision `README.md`'s Design link pins, and a fixture declaring a clause that revision does not carry fails `make conformance` as a declaration naming no clause. A fixture-first plan therefore cannot land a single mock fixture until the contract states the behaviour, and `make check` runs conformance, so nothing in the epic can merge before this row.

## Proposal

**The scene.** The design lives on `engineering`; the build and the corpus live on `main`; `README.md` on `main` pins a commit on `engineering`. Conformance resolves that pin through `origin/engineering`, and `invocation.md`'s option table is held to the command's help output by `make invocation`.

**The design.**

- **The design gains one clause.** The link classification clause states the mapping as a thing that decides what a reference names, alongside an operator and the root. It states the redirect, the identity matching, the section key's precedence, the working-directory bounding that exempts a substitute from the writing source's root, and that a substitute is a source in every respect.
- **`invocation.md` gains a bundling rule.** The rule states the redirect, the identity matching, the section key's precedence, the working-directory bounding and the substitute's standing as a source. The option row lands with W01, because the help text it must reproduce cannot exist before the option does.
- **The pin moves to the amendment.** `README.md`'s Design link pins the `engineering` commit carrying the clause. The amendment merges to `engineering` first, so the pin cannot be written before it exists.
- **The vocabulary carries the new words.** The design marks each term it introduces, so the pinned design admits the words the epic's fixture declarations and clause state, and `docs/glossary.md` on the build branch defines them. Both halves are required: `make independence` reads the design at the pin and `make vocabulary` holds the glossary to it.
- **Two pull requests.** The design amendment merges to `engineering` and this row's body links it; the pin bump, the bundling rule and the glossary rows land on the epic base.
