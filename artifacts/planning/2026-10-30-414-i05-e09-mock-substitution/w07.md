**Component:** src/prosiform/metadata.py

## Overview

The substitute as a source in its own right: its conversion metadata decides which group it joins, and its declared root bounds the links it writes — with the two fixtures that show each.

## Problem

A redirect that carried the original's conversion settings would produce a document whose content came from one file and whose `group` came from another, and a group has one claimant per member. Equally, a substitute that inherited the *original's* root would have its own links bounded by a directory it does not live in, so a stand-in kept beside the tree under test could reach nothing.

## Proposal

**The scene.** `read_document` captures a source, then resolves its conversion metadata and its declared root from the bytes it read. The traversal holds each source's settings immutably against its own path, and every outgoing link resolves within that source's root.

**The design.**

- **Metadata follows the bytes.** A substituted reference normalizes to the substitute's path before the read, so `read_document` captures the substitute's bytes and derives its `group`, `group-depth` and `root` from them. No setting travels from the original.
- **A substitute declaring a group joins it,** under the same depth agreement every contributing member is held to. A conflict is reported against the substitute's own declaration.
- **A substitute's root bounds its outgoing links.** The root resolves from its declaring file's directory, as any source's does, and a link it writes outside that root stays a citation.
- **The key's side is exempt; the substitute's own root is not.** The exemption is for the mapping entry, which the invocation named. A substitute's own declarations bind its own content as any document's do.
- **The capture is shared.** A substitute reached by two routes is one source, captured once, rechecked once, and contributing one group membership.

## Fixtures

| Fixture | What its source tree and manifest declare |
| --- | --- |
| `fixtures/mock/a-substitute-declaring-a-group-joins-it` | `guide.md` and `notes.md` both declare group `rules` at depth `2`, and `guide.md` links `rules.md`, which declares no group. The run is given `-m rules.md=stand-in.md`, where `stand-in.md` declares group `rules` at depth `2`. The bundle merges three members beneath one `Rules` heading, and the substitute's section appears under a member heading naming its own path. |
| `fixtures/mock/a-substitute-root-bounds-its-own-links` | `guide.md` declares no root and links `rules.md`. `stand-in/entry.md` declares `prosiform.root: .` and links `detail.md` beside it and `../../outside.md` above its root. The run is given `-m rules.md=stand-in/entry.md`. The bundle carries the substitute and `detail.md`; `outside.md` is emitted as authored and never opened, so the bundle carries it as a citation and raises `W_RESOURCE_PORTABILITY` for nothing. |
