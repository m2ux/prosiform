# I05:E04:W04 Artifact Operations as Step Sub-sections — October 2026

> Task delivery · Created 2026-10-28

## Executive Summary

This record holds the delivery of I05:E04 W04. Every operation under `example/work-planner/artifacts/` is converted to the step convention. Each of its steps becomes a `####` sub-section under the `###` operation, headed `N. Action` with an action written from the step's leading instruction. The 31 artifacts hold about 423 steps across 118 operations, the bulk of the corpus. The usage-example bundles that reach an artifact are regenerated.

Artifact steps carry no label, so a mode or protocol could name an operation but not one of its steps. Each step now roots its own anchor, and W05's links rely on those anchors resolving in every bundle that carries them.
